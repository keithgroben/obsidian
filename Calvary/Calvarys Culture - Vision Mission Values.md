# Calvary's Culture

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uJIqoXriyXZ4TyAPY16bDK5oIBY4kPnN68sYPZNHCxo*

## Vision
Calvary is a community of growing sons and daughters of God who are empowered to carry out their life's ministry and are united in wholehearted, passionate worship of God.

**Growing, empowered, united worshipers.**

## Mission
To cultivate a community which is founded on love, acceptance and authenticity and to host gatherings which allow the Church to worship, encounter God and be equipped for their life's ministry.

## Values
Excellence in freedom, love and community.

## Core Values: Pillars we grow on
1. Worship
2. Honor
3. Family
4. Community
5. Hunger for God
6. Authenticity
7. Unoffendable
8. Healthy Boundaries

**1 Peter 1:3-9**
