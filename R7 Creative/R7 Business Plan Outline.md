## 2. Business Description

### Business Model

- Sell consultation

- Sell through cold calls

### Unique Value Proposition
We offer a systematized process and training that enables B2B salespeople and sales teams to systematically generate sales opportunities using low-cost, education-based, direct-response, prospect marketing.

### Target Market
- Small to medium businesses with $1,000,000+ in annual revenue
- 75 employees or under
- B2B Sales Teams reliant on outbound prospecting
- Sales Managers/Directors (decision-makers)
- Entrepreneurs/SMB Owners who handle sales themselves

### Where We Fit In
1. **Simplified Prospecting Solutions** — alternative to costly CRM platforms like Salesforce/HubSpot
2. **Social Selling Aides** — landing pages, website structures for lead capture, one-pagers, lead magnets, marketing automation, funnel strategies
3. **Baseline Online Essentials** — responsive website, social engagement (LinkedIn/Twitter/Facebook), analytics, regular content publishing

## 3. Market Analysis

### Target Audience
- Small Business Owners (door-to-door, cold calls, warm emails, social media)
- B2B Sales Teams
- Sales Managers/Directors
- Entrepreneurs/SMB Owners

### Market Size
- U.S. companies spend **$70B+ annually on sales training** (share of $101.8B workplace training market)
- ~$2,000 per salesperson on training
- **40% of salespeople** identify prospecting as the most challenging part of their role
- Companies using prospecting software generate **35% more leads**
- Top-performing sales reps dedicate ~5 hours weekly to prospecting
- Social selling boosts revenue for **63.4% of participating sales reps** and influences ~92% of B2B buying decisions

### Competitors

**1. Jeb Blount** — *Fanatical Prospecting*
- Multi-channel prospecting, time management, mindset training
- Strengths: Scalable, actionable, multi-channel focus
- Weaknesses: Volume emphasis, generic templates

**2. Mark Hunter** — *High-Profit Prospecting*
- Targeted prospecting, eliminating time-wasters, consultative selling
- Strengths: Efficiency, high-value focus, practical advice
- Weaknesses: Less volume focus, limited tech integration

**3. Stu Heinecke** — *Contact Marketing*
- Creative outreach, direct mail + digital hybrid, low-cost strategies
- Strengths: Personalization, memorable campaigns, flexibility
- Weaknesses: Resource-intensive, limited scalability, niche appeal

### Competitive Advantage
Systematized process tailored for small-to-medium businesses. Personalized coaching and training — scalable and cost-effective.

## 5. Marketing and Sales Strategy

### Marketing Channels

**1. Digital Marketing**
- **SEO** — Keywords: "sales prospecting training," "lead generation for small businesses," "B2B sales coaching." Blog content: "How to Generate Consistent Leads for Your Sales Team," "5 Cold Calling Techniques That Actually Work."
- **Social Media Ads** — LinkedIn/Facebook targeting small business owners, sales managers (won't do until we perfect organic)
- **Content Marketing** — Case studies, testimonials, downloadable lead magnets

**2. Email Marketing**
- **Lead Magnets** — "The Ultimate Guide to Prospecting for Small Businesses"
- **Nurture Campaigns** — 3-email sequence (common mistakes → client case study → CTA to talk)
- **Testimonials** — Client quotes to build trust

**3. Cold Calling**
- **Target Audience** — $1M+ revenue, <70 employees (LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo)
- **Process:** 300+ qualified list → call with scripts to set Zoom times → Dad sells on call → deliver products/services

### Sales Call — Key Elements (Four Things)
1. Prospect hasn't admitted they have a serious problem
2. Haven't shown they're truly committed to fixing it
3. Haven't admitted they need help
4. Haven't said they trust you to solve their problem

### Cold Call Script (Draft)
- "Hi [name], this is actually a cold call. Do you mind if I briefly explain why I was reaching out?"
- "I'm a [prospecting trainer]. Perhaps you tell me your sales people are great and I'll let you get on with your Monday."
- "Would either of these problems be relevant in your world?"
- "Is it more to get stuck on objections or the confidence to pick up the phone?"

### Cold Call Metrics
- Call-to-Appointment Ratio
- Appointment-to-Close Ratio
- Average Call Duration
- Objection Handling Success Rate
