# Not showing up high on Google Maps?

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/3406abe00ba480ed99bed2b059d556f3 (4 Ps Teaching database)*

## Problem
When is the last time you searched your business on Google Maps? Did you come up if you were right there? If you were 5 miles away? Have you ever heard a customer or a friend tell you they searched you online and didn't see you at all?

I remember onboarding a client, standing in their business, searching for "their business type near me" on my phone, and they were 11th down the list.

## Principle
Google has three underlying measurements: **distance, prominence, and relevance.**

- **Distance:** How far each potential business result is from the customer who is searching. If a customer doesn't share their location, Google calculates based on known location data.
- **Prominence:** How well-known or popular a business is. Based on info from across the web (links, articles, directories) as well as Google review count and positive ratings.
- **Relevance:** How well a Business Profile matches what someone is searching for. Provide complete and detailed business information to help Google understand your business.

## Process
If you haven't given your Google profile much attention, prominence is out, distance isn't in your control. But to avoid being buried, **improve relevance**.

### 1. Core Business Information
- **Business Name:** Exactly as it appears in the real world (no keyword stuffing — violates Google's guidelines).
- **Primary Category:** The single most specific category that represents your core business.
- **Secondary Categories:** Up to 9 additional categories.
- **Address:** Precise, accurate physical address (no P.O. boxes). Hide if you don't receive customers.
- **Service Area:** Specific cities, zip codes, local areas you serve.

### 2. Contact Information
- **Phone Number** (plus up to 2 additional — no fax numbers)
- **Website** (full URL, ensure Googlebot isn't blocked)
- **Chat / Messaging** (if eligible — Google Chat or WhatsApp)

### 3. Hours of Operation
- Main Business Hours (regular weekly)
- Special Hours (holidays, events, temporary)
- More Hours (delivery, takeout, drive-through, senior hours)

### 4. Details, Services, and Products
- **Business Description** — Helpful overview, no links, gibberish, or promotional jargon.
- **Attributes** — Wi-Fi, parking, wheelchair access, veteran-owned, woman-owned, etc.
- **Services** — Detailed list of specific services.
- **In-Store Products** — If retail, connect inventory.

### 5. Media & Visuals
- **Logo** — Clear, square image.
- **Cover Photo** — Preferred top-of-profile image.
- **Business Photos** — Recent, high-quality, exterior/interior/team/products.
- **Videos** — Up to 30 seconds, 720p+, up to 75 MB.

**Ongoing:** Regularly use **Posts** to share updates and consistently **respond to all reviews**.

## Practice: Google Business Profile Completion Checklist

### 1. Core Business Information
- [ ] Set your Business Name — exactly as signage
- [ ] Choose Primary Category — most specific
- [ ] Add Secondary Categories — up to 9
- [ ] Enter Street Address — exact, no P.O. boxes
- [ ] Define Service Area — every city, zip, area you serve

### 2. Contact Information
- [ ] Add Primary Phone Number — local when possible
- [ ] Add Additional Phone Numbers (up to 2)
- [ ] Enter Website URL — https://, Googlebot not blocked
- [ ] Enable Chat / Messaging

### 3. Hours
- [ ] Set Regular Business Hours (every day)
- [ ] Add Special Hours (holidays, events)
- [ ] Add More Hours (service-specific schedules)

### 4. Details
- [ ] Write Business Description (750 chars, no links, no promo language)
- [ ] Check every Attribute that applies
- [ ] List every Service you provide
- [ ] Connect In-Store Products (retail only)

### 5. Media
- [ ] Upload Logo (square)
- [ ] Upload Cover Photo
- [ ] Add Exterior Photos
- [ ] Add Interior Photos
- [ ] Add Team Photos
- [ ] Add Product / Portfolio Photos
- [ ] Upload at least one Video (30s max, 720p+, <75MB)

### 6. Keep It Active
- [ ] Start posting Google Posts regularly (weekly minimum)
- [ ] Respond to every review — positive and negative
