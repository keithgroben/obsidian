# Why Your Social Media Doesn't Need to Go Viral — It Just Needs to Not Look Abandoned

## 1. COLD OPEN / STATUS HOOK (0:00–0:20)
*"We worked with a local service business owner. When we started, he was getting 4 inquiries a month. We changed one thing about his social media — not how often he posted, but what he posted. He went to 15 inquiries a month. No ads. This video is about exactly what that change was."*

## 2. FRAME THE PROBLEM — The Three Types of Business Owners

### Type 1 — The Ghost
Hasn't posted in months. Page looks abandoned. Usually three reasons: don't see value, no time, or scared of getting it wrong.
**Cost:** When a referral looks them up on social and sees nothing, they click off and call someone else.

### Type 2 — The Receptionist/Relative
Someone IS posting — could be the receptionist, cousin, or sister. But every post is a graphic: "Book a call." "Schedule a visit." "Contact us today."
**Cost:** All ask, zero give. The page is active but not educating anyone to make the right decision.

### Type 3 — The Graphic Poster
Posting consistently. Logo posts, service posts, offer graphics. Looks professional. But their competitor is posting the exact same thing.
**Cost:** Zero reason to choose them over the next guy. Every caller is a price shopper who ghosts.

*"All three have the same problem. Their social media has no social element to it. Nothing that gives to the community. Nothing that builds trust. That's what we're going to fix today."*

## 3. PROMISE (2:30–3:30)

**FIRST:** Understand why social media is the most powerful free advertising tool a local service business has access to right now.

**SECOND:** The exact types of posts that convert someone already looking for services into calling your business rather than your competitor — positioning.

**THIRD — the big one:** Social media is a trust signal for local service business. NOT where leads come from. It's something people look at to give them another reason to call you instead of your competitor.

## 4. THE STORY / PROOF

*"These are the types of posts we've been doing for this client. None of this is viral. None of this is fancy. Ask yourself — is the goal to go viral? Or is it to show your potential customers how you can add value in a way your competitors can't?"*

**Post types shown:**
- Before/after pictures and videos of service being delivered
- Videos mid-job — showing the work actually being done
- Pictures of clients standing in front of finished service with big smile
- Educational content answering 2-3 questions from every sales conversation

*"The result: 4 inquiries a month became 15."*

## 5. WHY SOCIAL MEDIA IS THE BEST FREE ADVERTISING

*[Gary Vee's* Jab Jab Jab Right Hook *— Page 2 passage here when filming]*

*"When his competitors are doing simple graphics and asking for schedules, and we came at it from a completely different angle — helping the consumer make an informed buying decision through content — who are you going to trust more? That's what we did. 4 calls a month to 15."*

## 6. THE DIRECT APPLICATION — WHAT TO POST

Community service content that informs people on making a smart buying decision. After seeing your content they know:
- What your company is
- What their experience could be like working with you
- What to look for when purchasing services in your industry
- Common misconceptions cleared up

### The 4 Post Types

**Post Type 1 — Work in Progress / Job Site Content**
Before and after pictures and videos. Process mid-job. People want to see what you actually do.

**Post Type 2 — The People Behind the Company**
Who answers the phone. Who shows up. Who owns the place. People hire people, not logos. Show faces, even hobbies and interests.

**Post Type 3 — How You Work With Customers**
Walk through the experience. Consultation process, what to expect, quality of care throughout — and after the job.

**Post Type 4 — Your Take on Something in the Industry**
A quick opinion. A common mistake you see. One thing you'd tell every customer before they hire anybody. Positions you as the expert before they ever speak to you.

*"Imagine your social media had all 4 of these. Before and afters. Faces. Videos of how you work with customers. Posts educating people. Now imagine you did that consistently for 3 months. How valuable would that page be to someone who knows nothing about the service?"*

**And then — occasionally, like twice a month — you can ask.** Book a call. Schedule a visit. But only after 6 to 9 posts of pure value. Then you've earned the ask.

## 7. CLOSE / CTA

*"Here's the reality. Every month your page looks like everybody else's — or worse, looks completely dead — you are leaving trust on the table. Trust is the bridge between somebody buying from you and not."*

*"Pick one content type from what I just covered. Post it once this week. Just one. The goal moving forward is 2 to 3 times a week. If you need help coming up with ideas, ask ChatGPT or Claude."*

*"Imprint this philosophy from Gary Vaynerchuk: **Document, Don't Create**. You already have a day-to-day business life. Document what you're already doing and put it on the internet. You don't need to create. You don't need to be creative. Just document."*
