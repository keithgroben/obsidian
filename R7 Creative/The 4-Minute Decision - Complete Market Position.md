## Executive Summary

**Position Statement:** *"We Keep You in the 4-Minute Decision"*

We help service-based businesses survive the critical 4-minute window when urgent customers decide who to call. While competitors focus on generic social media management, we ensure you have recent, authentic proof of active work when it matters most — turning invisible losses into phone calls.

## I. The Market Reality: The John Story

### The Universal Customer Journey
- **30 seconds:** Boss drops urgent problem on desk
- **4 minutes until lunch:** Must find solution provider NOW
- **2 minutes:** Google search → Maps results → Opens 5 businesses
- **90 seconds:** Rapid elimination based on "digital pulse check"
- **30 seconds:** Down to 2 survivors who get callbacks

### The Invisible Crisis
**80% of businesses fail this test daily and never know it happened.**

### The New Competitive Battlefield
Businesses aren't competing on quality, experience, price, or website design. **They're competing on who looks more present than local competitors during 4-minute decisions.**
