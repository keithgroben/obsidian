## 1. PURPOSE
R7 Creative is a digital marketing agency that helps local businesses improve their online presence. This Agreement allows Affiliate to refer clients to R7 Creative and earn commissions for successful referrals.

## 2. AFFILIATE RELATIONSHIP
- Affiliate operates own independent business (not employee/partner/agent)
- No authority to bind R7 Creative to contracts
- Affiliate handles own taxes; W-9 required before payments

## 3. AFFILIATE'S ROLE

**What Affiliate Does:**
- Identify clients who may benefit from services
- Offer free digital marketing consultation
- Schedule consultations using R7 Creative's online calendar

**What Affiliate Does NOT Do:**
- Participate in sales/negotiations
- Make promises about services or pricing
- Resell services as their own
- Run paid advertising using R7 brand
- Incur expenses expecting reimbursement

## 4. R7 CREATIVE'S RESPONSIBILITIES
- Conduct all consultations and sales
- Deliver all services to clients
- Provide Affiliate with limited marketing materials and calendar access
- Assign a dedicated partner manager
- Monthly check-ins
- Handle all client support and service issues

## 5. COMPENSATION

### Commission Structure
- **$200 Finder's Fee** — Paid when referred client signs and makes first payment
- **Flat Monthly Residual** — Paid every month client stays active
  - 4-Minute Winner: $55/mo
  - 4-Minute Dominator: $95/mo

### CRITICAL CONDITION — Residuals Stop Upon Termination
**Monthly residual payments will ONLY be paid while this Agreement is active. If this Agreement terminates for any reason, all future residual payments stop immediately, even if the referred clients continue with R7 Creative. Affiliate has no ownership rights to future residuals after termination.**

### Payment Details
- Method: Direct deposit only
- Timing: Net 15, after R7 receives payment from client
- Requirements: Bank info and completed W-9

### Qualified Referral
(a) Client completes the consultation, (b) signs service agreement, (c) makes initial payment.

No guaranteed earnings. All compensation depends on successful referrals and client retention.

## 6. PERFORMANCE TARGETS
**Goals (Not Requirements):**
- 3-5 scheduled consultations per month
- 3 closed sales per quarter

## 7. MARKETING & BRAND USE
- Promote R7 Creative's services as R7 Creative's services (not as own)
- Use only approved materials
- Follow brand guidelines
- R7 Creative controls all client relationships

## 8. PROTECTION OF AFFILIATE FROM CLIENT ISSUES
**Affiliate Has No Liability for R7 Creative's Services.**
R7 Creative is solely responsible for all client relationships and service delivery. R7 Creative will defend and indemnify Affiliate from any claims made by referred clients about R7 Creative's services.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties keep confidential: client lists, pricing, commission rates, business strategies, proprietary methods. **3-year survival** after Agreement ends.

## 10. TERM & TERMINATION
- Quarterly Review: Every 3 months to assess success
- Either party may terminate at any time with written notice
- Upon termination: **All future residual payments stop immediately**
- Commissions earned before termination paid on normal schedule

## 11. MUTUAL PROTECTIONS

**Affiliate Protects R7 Creative From:** Affiliate's marketing practices, employee claims, tax obligations, business operations.

**R7 Creative Protects Affiliate From:** Service quality claims, professional liability, billing disputes, refund requests.
