## STEP 1: Optimize Your Google Business Profile
- NAP consistency across platforms
- Business hours — keep accurate and updated
- Products and services — fill out every service with description
- Categories — pick several, not just one
- Post new photos consistently

**What this means to you:** Your GBP is a free salesperson working 24/7. Right now most owners have that salesperson sitting in the corner with no information, wrong hours, and a phone number that doesn't match the website.

## STEP 2: Start Posting Content
- **What to post** — show the process, the people, the finished work, before and after
- **Where to post** — GBP, Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
- Facebook and LinkedIn likely top-performing — that's where your customers actually are
- **Why it works** — when someone gets referred and looks you up, they need somewhere that builds trust
- Consistency matters more than volume

**What this means to you:** You didn't lose them because your work was bad. You lost them because you were invisible online. You're not trying to go viral. You're just giving people a reason to trust you before they pick up the phone.

## STEP 3: The Review Sprint
- Ask IMMEDIATELY after delivering the service
- **Exact script:** *"Hey I'm with [company], we did [specific thing] for you — would you be opposed to leaving us a quick review?"*
- "Would you be opposed to" works better than "can you" — harder to say yes to direct ask, easier to say no to "would you be opposed"
- Stay on the phone until they do it. Don't hang up and hope.
- For old clients: pull spreadsheet, call 2 people a week, send direct link
- **2/week beats 10 in one day** — Google flags unnatural spikes as bots

**What this means to you:** Every job you complete without asking for a review is a missed trust signal.

## STEP 4: What To Do If They Leave 3 Stars Instead of 5
- Respond to every review — good and bad
- Your response is for the **next customer reading it**, not the person who left it
- What to never say when responding to a negative review

**What this means to you:** A bad review isn't a death sentence. A bad review with no response IS. Your competitor ignoring their bad reviews is handing you an opportunity every single week.

## THE RESULT
- Print shop: 4 inquiries/month → 15 — just from GBP optimization and content
- Review sprint hasn't even started yet
- You're not getting new customers — you're converting the ones who were already looking

**What this means to you:** You don't need more marketing. You need to stop losing the people who are already finding you. Fix your profile, post content, start getting consistent reviews — and the phone starts ringing without spending a dollar on ads.
