## Vendor Management

### Creating Purchase Orders
1. Book order in Antara
2. Navigate to **PO** section
3. Select vendor from dropdown
4. Add products being purchased
5. Include vendor-tagged art files
6. Review and send PO

### Receiving Vendor Invoices
1. Locate order in system
2. Click **Actions** > **Receive Invoice**
3. Match invoice to PO
4. Verify amounts
5. Mark as received
6. System syncs with QuickBooks

### Vouching Process
- Enter vendor invoice details
- Match to purchase order
- Verify line items and amounts
- Approve for payment
- Payment tracking handled in QuickBooks

## Financial Processing

### Order Payment Workflow
1. Order booking creates invoice in QuickBooks
2. Customer payment received (external process)
3. Payment recorded in QuickBooks
4. Antara syncs payment status from QuickBooks

### Credit Application
- Credits appear in customer account
- View credits: **Customer** > **Credits** tab
- Apply credit to new order or issue refund
- If refunding: record in Antara ledger to reconcile

### QuickBooks Integration
- **One-way sync:** Antara → QuickBooks
- Antara pushes: orders, invoices, credit memos
- QuickBooks does NOT push back: payments, refund confirmations
- Manual reconciliation required for actual money movement
