## System Overview

### Core Principle
Capture customers at their **peak happiness moment** and guide them through an immediate, authentic review process.

### State Flow
**Trigger Identified** → **Pulse Check** → **Happy Path/Unhappy Path** → **Review Request** → **QR Scan** → **Review Guided** → **Review Posted** → **Follow-up Complete**

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Identify the Happiness Trigger
Recognize when your customer is in their happiest emotional state (within 24hrs of service delivery).

### Step 2: The Pulse Check
**Script:** *"How happy are you with [specific service]?"*

**Decision Points:**
- **Score 8-10 + Enthusiastic** → Continue to Happy Path
- **Score 1-7 OR Negative** → Move to Unhappy Path

### Step 3A: Happy Path — The Review Request
**Script:** *"It would mean the world to me if you could leave us an honest review on Google. Would you be willing to do that?"*

### Step 3B: Unhappy Path — Issue Collection
- Document specific problems
- Record preferred follow-up method
- Commit to resolution via customer success
- Handoff within 24 hours

### Step 4: QR Code Scan
**Script:** *"Would you mind taking your phone out right now and scanning this QR code? It'll take you right to the place to review us."*

### Step 5: Guide the Review Process
**Key Message:** *"We're not asking for 5 stars unless we deserve it. Give us the stars you think we deserve, be descriptive, and if possible, add a photo or video."*

### Step 6: Follow-up
**Script:** *"Thank you so much! If anything changes in the future or you need anything, please reach out to us directly so we can handle it."*

## Training Modules

### Module 1: Happiness Trigger Identification
Recognize service delivery moments that create peak satisfaction. Map customer journey for optimal timing. Train emotional state recognition.

### Module 2: The Pulse Check Conversation
Master scripted but natural approach. Read verbal and non-verbal cues. Execute branching logic.

### Module 3: The Review Request
Use authentic phrasing that converts. Handle objections professionally.

**Common Objections & Responses:**
- "I don't have time" → "It only takes 30 seconds"
- "I don't do reviews" → "This one time would mean so much"
- "I'll do it later" → "Would you mind doing it now while I help?"

### Module 4: Live Review Guidance
QR code technology + backup methods. Help customers write authentic, detailed content. Encourage photos/videos.

### Module 5: Follow-up and Relationship Building
Express appreciation. Set expectations for future interactions. Build long-term review culture.

## Quality Standards & Rules

### One-Shot Rules
- **Single Opportunity:** Each customer gets ONE review request per service
- **Cooling Period:** If declined, wait 90 days before next opportunity
- **Resolution First:** If unhappy, resolve issue before any future review asks

### Authenticity Requirements
- Must reflect genuine customer experience
- No incentives, discounts, or quid pro quo
- Staff must complete training and certification
- Reviews must include specific service details

### Human Touch Points (NEVER Automate)
- Reading customer emotional state
- Timing the happiness trigger
- Authentic conversation flow
- Helping write genuine review content
- Handling objections and concerns
- Building relationships
