# R7 Marketing — Channel Partner Strategy

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/2f86abe00ba480c6aa6ad1ea2b14dbd9*

Shifting R7 Creative from a general agency to a specialized partner for physical marketing companies (print shops, wrap shops, and sign makers) is a sharp move.

## 1. Defining the "Channel Partner" Model

To scale this with minimal time, aim for a **White-Label Fulfillment** model:

- **The Hook:** They sell the "Digital Growth Pack" (GBP, Website, Social) as their own premium recurring service.
- **The Split:** They keep the high-margin markup for "managing" the relationship; you get a flat, predictable wholesale fee for fulfillment.
- **The Reality:** You provide them with a "Partner Sales Kit" (one-pagers and scripts) so they do the selling, while you handle the "You Capture, We Create" workflow in the background.

## 2. The "Seen, Considered, Hired" Framework

| Pillar | Service | Value Proposition for a Print Shop |
|---|---|---|
| Seen | Google Business Profile | "We make sure your shop is the first one that pops up when someone searches 'vehicle wraps near me'." |
| Considered | Optimized Website (SEO/AEO) | "We turn your gallery of work into a high-converting machine so you stop losing leads to 'contact us' forms that go nowhere." |
| Hired | "You Capture, We Create" Social | "You're already taking photos of every finished sign and wrap. Send them to us; we'll turn them into the content that proves you're the best." |

## 3. The Systematic "Limited Time" Outreach Plan

**Phase 1: The "Shock & Awe" Outreach (Low Volume, High Impact)**
Instead of 100 cold calls a day, try the **Double-Tap Method**:
- **Identify:** Pick 10 high-end wrap or sign shops per week.
- **The Digital Audit:** Send a 2-minute Loom video pointing out one thing on their GBP or Website that is costing them money.
- **The Phone Call:** Follow up 24 hours later. "Hey, I sent you a video about your Google profile — I'm the guy who helps print shops add a digital recurring revenue stream. Do you have 5 minutes to talk about a channel partnership?"

**Phase 2: The Physical Impression**
Since they live in a physical world, use it. After a good discovery call, send a handwritten note or a branded "Social Media Capture Guide" for their shop. It proves you understand their medium.

**Phase 3: The "You Capture, We Create" Internal Demo**
Since you can't use your own GBP, use LinkedIn as your portfolio. Document the process of you building the R7 Creative niche. Show the "before and after" of a print shop's social media that you've managed.

## 4. Addressing the Logistics

**White-Label Onboarding Portal:**
- Use the "One-Eyed Graphics" print shop site as your "Beta Case Study." Document every win there to show future partners.
- **Automation:** Use a simple intake form (Tally or Typeform) where the partner or client uploads their weekly photos/videos. This feeds directly into your Notion or project management tool.
