## Presentation Outline: The Great Commission, Your Results, and the Alliance

### PART 1 — What the Great Commission Is

The Great Commission isn't one verse. It's a mandate repeated across Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, and Acts.
Jesus is saying the same thing from multiple angles.

It has three inseparable parts, and all three rest on one foundation:

- **Evangelism:** preaching the Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ — sins forgiven, bodies healed, dead raised, demons cast out, hearts healed, hope restored, purpose given.
- **Discipleship:** equipping the saints for the work of the ministry; believers matured to the stature of the fullness of Christ.
- **Demonstration of God's power:** the Holy Spirit and fire. Manifestations of the gifts, baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire — not a watered-down or "quenched" version.
- **Foundation:** the community of the church, local and global. Fellowship and united in purpose.

These aren't options. These aren't 'things' the church does. They're the product Jesus created, and the product we're responsible to keep producing.

---

### PART 2 — Giving Jesus His Results (The Parable Framework)

Three parables.

1. **Minas (Luke 19:11-27)** — Invest it. Put it to work.
2. **Talents (Matthew 25:14-30)** — Double what He gave you.
3. **Ten Virgins (Matthew 25:1-13)** — Buy oil. Be ready when He returns.

One message.

> God gives every believer a spark of life when they're born again. That spark is your mina. Your talent. Your lamp.

The plan laid out for you in the Bible isn't survival, it isn't barely get by, it isn't 'hope some how, some way that you're making a difference'. It's **full maturity**. It's the fullness of the stature of Christ. From child to adult. From child to heir. You are the seed of Abraham (Galatians 3). The power to get wealth is yours so God can establish His covenant on the earth.

If you still aren't clear: God will make you successful and you will make his mission successful in return.

The oil is the Holy Spirit — not a tame, watered-down acknowledgment of Him, but the baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire that Jesus Himself promised. You can't light your lamp without it.

**The whole body works together** — like an assembly line, a factory, a math problem being solved — to produce the results Jesus harvested in His earthly ministry. He is still the head. His activity has not stopped. We are the body continuing that work.

Giving Jesus His results means:
- Financial success
- Operational success
- Sales — which for the Church is evangelism
- Marketing — which for the Church is preaching and media distribution
- The product of the Church is Jesus, through the preaching of the gospel and the demonstration of the Holy Spirit's power

---

### PART 3 — Why I'm Forming the Great Commission Alliance

Two reasons. They're both true at the same time.

**First: It's my mandate.** There's no other way to say it. The Lord led me — through ministry, through marketing, through operations, through business — into exactly the position I'm in. I didn't fully see it while it was happening. But I was following Him.

**Second: There's a gap.** The Church is building alone. Entrepreneurs are building alone. Neither side is speaking the other's language. The body is fragmented. The parts aren't supplying what the other parts need.

The Great Commission Alliance is for the people who already have that burning in them — who know their life is about giving Jesus His results — and who need to be connected to the rest of the body so the whole thing can work.

It's not primarily a membership structure. It's not a business. It's operating the Father's business. The family business. What Jesus was doing at 12 years old in the temple — *"Didn't you know I'd be about my Father's business?"* — that's the posture.
