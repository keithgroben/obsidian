# The Great Commission Manifesto

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/2cd6abe00ba480aea148c4501bc8232b*

---

## Acts Restoration Movement

We believe we are witnessing the restoration of the Book of Acts in function throughout the body of Christ. A move of God that some consider the last great move of God, and can be called The Acts Restoration Movement. While we cannot be certain if it is the final move, one thing is sure: there is power and purpose in it.

This time is marked by people who are moving toward God rather than waiting for God to move toward them. They draw on God's power in faith (Mark 5:30), live holy lives, look for and hasten the return of Christ (2 Peter 3:11-12). They understand the urgency to act because the time is very near (1 Peter 4:7). They are unafraid to rebel-against-the-rebellion against God, working together to build Great Commission results for our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.

Jesus said, "The works that I do you will do also, and greater works than these will you do" (John 14:12). He did not stop working, but continues His work through us.

The bride-groom is coming (Matthew 25:1-13). We live as sober-minded disciples of Jesus Christ (1 Peter 4:7), coordinating our efforts for Great Commission results.

*This manifesto serves as a banner for all those who have lost their lives for Jesus' sake and the Gospel's (Mark 8:35).*

---

## The Great Commission

**The church's three part mission is found across these key scriptures:** Matthew 28:19-20, Mark 16:15-18, Luke 24:46-49, John 20:21-23, Acts 1:5, 8.

- **Evangelism** — Winning the lost through preaching the Gospel
- **Discipleship** — Teaching them to observe everything Christ commanded
- **The Anointing** — Break through, miraculous power for success in Evangelism, Discipleship, and Church strengthening

*All three elements rest upon the community of the church (Acts 2:42-47).*

---

## Opposing Divisions

We reject the divisions Paul condemned when he said, "Some of you say 'I am of Apollos,' 'I am of Paul'" (1 Corinthians 1:12).

We are Christ's, committed to giving Him the results He Himself got (John 14:12).

We affirm sound biblical teaching and doctrine, and eliminate traditions of men that have made God's Word of no effect.

---

## Eliminating "Traditions of Men"

We commit to cease practicing Christian ways that have replaced God's power, as Scripture warns: "You have made the word of God of no effect because you have replaced it with the traditions of men" (Mark 7:13). In their place, our faith rests in the demonstration and the power of God (1 Corinthians 2:4-5).

---

## Holy Living and Eternal Urgency

Understanding that "the earth will be melted" (2 Peter 3:10-12), we live as people waiting for and hastening Christ's return.

We multiply our talents as faithful servants (Matthew 25:14-30) and ensure we have oil (the power of the Holy Ghost) when Jesus returns, unlike the five foolish virgins (Matthew 25:1-13).

We buy from Him gold refined in fire and white garments (Revelation 3:18), selling all we possess to obtain the treasure of the field (Matthew 13:44) — giving Jesus His results (Matthew 25:19-21): souls won, disciples made, His power demonstrated.

---

## One Mission, No Special Ministries

There are no special movements, no "isms," and no leading denominations (1 Corinthians 3:4-9) — we are Christ's.

> Verse 9: "For we are God's fellow workers. You are God's field, God's building."

All Christians have been given the ministry of reconciliation and must find their part God has assigned them (2 Corinthians 5:18-20). Everyone in the Body of Christ continues the ministry of Jesus Christ as He is the head and we are His body (Colossians 1:18, 1 Corinthians 12:27).

**Every mission and assignment from God must align with His Word and demonstrate one or many of the following:**

- Souls won (Luke 19:10)
- Souls discipled (Matthew 28:19-20)
- The power of God demonstrated (1 Corinthians 2:4-5, Mark 16:17-18)
- Church community built (Acts 2:42-47, Ephesians 4:11-16)

---

## Foundational Beliefs

**We believe these fundamental tenets of faith:**

- **Scripture is authoritative and inerrant** (2 Timothy 3:16-17)
- **God exists as Trinity: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit** (Matthew 28:19, 2 Corinthians 13:14)
- **Salvation comes by grace through faith alone** (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- **We are saved when we confess with our mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in our heart that God raised Him from the dead** (Romans 10:9-10)
- **Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man** (John 1:1, 14; Philippians 2:6-8)
- **Jesus was virgin born, lived sinlessly, died as our substitute, and rose bodily from the dead** (Isaiah 7:14, Hebrews 4:15, 1 Peter 2:24, 1 Corinthians 15:3-4)
- **Personal conversion and being born again are necessary** (John 3:3-7)
- **Jesus Christ will return** (Acts 1:11, Revelation 22:12)
- **We must live holy lives and put off sin** (1 Peter 1:15-16, Colossians 3:5-10)
- **Believers are baptized in the Holy Spirit and fire** (Acts 1:5, 2:1-4)
- **Believers are baptized in water** (Matthew 28:19, Acts 2:38)

---

## Conclusion

Our commitment is to give Jesus Christ the results He requires through coordinated effort, biblical direction, godly living, empowered by the Holy Spirit.
