# The Great Commission Alliance — Commitment and Membership

## The Great Commission Alliance

### Commitment Statement

By joining this alliance you agree to hold yourself accountable to giving Jesus Christ Great Commission Results as detailed in scripture (Matthew 28:19-20) and outlined in this manifesto. You agree to coordinate your efforts, resources, and time with other alliance members voluntarily to look forward to and hasten the return of Jesus Christ (2 Peter 3:11-12).

### Flexible Participation Model

The Great Commission Alliance operates on a voluntary participation model designed to meet you where you are while maximizing collective kingdom impact.

### Three Levels of Involvement

### 1. OBSERVERS (No commitment required)

**Access includes:**
- Attend monthly meetups
- Access past meeting archives & resources
- Utilize alliance community support
- Receive updates about alliance activities
- Learn about the mission with no obligations
- Discern if deeper involvement aligns with your calling

No time or resource commitments required.

### 2. PARTICIPANTS (Flexible time/resource commitment)

**All Observer benefits PLUS:**
- Contribute time, skills, resources, and advisement
- Help create alliance projects and systems for Great Commission advancement
- Vote on project proposals
- Access to collaborative projects
- Shared resources and tools
- Community support and networking opportunities
- Track personal contributions
- Post in community discussions
- View detailed project needs and timelines

**Commitment levels are flexible and time-specific based on your capacity:**
- 2-5 hours per month
- 6-10 hours per month
- 11-20 hours per month
- 20+ hours per month

Proportional access to alliance benefits based on your level of contribution.

### 3. FULL ALLIANCE MEMBERS (Equal investment required)

**All Participant benefits PLUS:**
- Introduce new projects and collaboration opportunities
- Must commit equal value toward existing alliance projects (ensuring mutual investment in our shared mission)

**Maximum access to all alliance benefits:**
- Mutually beneficial projects
- Pooled resources
- Mastermind groups
- Strategic partnerships
- Comprehensive community support
- Vote on funding allocation
- Access full financial transparency and allocation reports
- Serve on alliance leadership council
- Co-lead projects
- Access member-only strategic planning meetings

**Requirements:**
- Must have been a Participant for a minimum timeframe (suggested 3 months)
- Must have demonstrated consistent contributions
- Must commit equal value/stake toward alliance projects
- Projects introduced cannot be for individual benefit (must be systems/initiatives for collective good)
- Must be approved by existing Members

### The Alliance Advantage

Your commitment level is voluntary and adjustable. Life seasons change, and so can your level of engagement. What matters is that you participate authentically at whatever level you can sustain.

The more we coordinate together, the greater our collective impact. Members consistently report that alliance participation multiplies their individual kingdom effectiveness through shared resources, collaborative opportunities, strategic insights, and mutual accountability that would be impossible to achieve alone.

Start where you are, contribute what you can, grow as you go.
