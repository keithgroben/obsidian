## The Orange Flag Revolution

Some time while living in Phoenix, and not long after graduating from PFPC, but long enough after Anaheim that I turned my heart tender to the Lord again… I was laying on my couch in my bottom story apartment in Phoenix. It was cool enough to have the sliding glass door open. I was listening to Hillsong worship music. Funny enough it was probably on a laptop or discman.

While listening to worship and connecting with God, I was in a vision of heaven. The anointing was present in a powerful way.

I saw a large group, a large crowd that filled a city street for a very long way. They were all marching forward together in unison. They were carrying orange flags with black painted or dyed X's. They were also wearing patches of the symbol on their clothes and backpacks. Some even making the symbol from colored duct tape or electric tape. I then saw small flag statues and other small versions of products with the symbol. In those moments and more I felt the tangible presence of the anointing to set free and Breakthrough.

These people were rebelling against the rebellion against God.

The governments and the denominations had become so evil that they were in open rebellion against God. These people had been seemingly transformed (by anointing power) into indignant righteous rebellers who were rebelling against the rebellion. The orange flag revolution.
