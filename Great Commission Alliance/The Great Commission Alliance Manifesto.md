## The Great Commission Triangle

The foundation of our mission rests on three essential elements, all flowing from the community of the church:

- **Evangelism** - Making disciples of all nations (Matthew 28:19)
- **Discipleship** - Teaching them to observe all things Christ commanded (Matthew 28:20)
- **Manifestation of God's Power** - Demonstrating the Kingdom through signs, wonders, and transformed lives (Mark 16:17-18, Acts 1:8)

## Dismantling Denominationalism

We reject the divisions Paul condemned when he said, "Some of you say 'I am of Apollos,' 'I am of Paul'" (1 Corinthians 1:12). We are simply Christians committed to giving Jesus Christ what He wants in these three areas of execution. While we affirm sound biblical teaching and doctrine, we eliminate the traditions of men that have made God's Word of no effect (Mark 7:13), replacing them with the power of God.

## Holy Living with Eternal Urgency

Understanding that "the earth will be melted" (2 Peter 3:10-12), we live as those preparing for Christ's return. We multiply our talents faithfully (Matthew 25:14-30) and ensure we have oil when Jesus returns, unlike the five foolish virgins (Matthew 25:1-13). We buy from Him gold refined in fire and white garments (Revelation 3:18), selling all we possess to obtain the treasure of the field (Matthew 13:44) - giving Jesus His results (Matthew 25:19-21).

## One Mission, No Special Ministries

There are no "isms," no special prophetic or apostolic movements, no unique denominations. Everyone continues the ministry of Jesus Christ as He is the head and we are the body (Ephesians 4:15-16, Colossians 1:18). Every mission and assignment must align with Scripture and demonstrate one or many of the following:

- **Souls won**
- **Souls discipled**
- **The power of God demonstrated**
- **Church community built**

We also maintain these fundamental tenets of evangelical faith:

- The authority and inerrancy of Scripture
- The Trinity (Father, Son, Holy Spirit)
- Salvation by grace through faith alone (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- Salvation comes by confessing Jesus as Lord and believing God raised Him from the dead (Romans 10:9)
- The deity and humanity of Jesus Christ
- Jesus' virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary death, and bodily resurrection
- The necessity of personal conversion/being born again
- The second coming of Jesus Christ
- The living of a holy life and the putting off of sin
- Baptism in the Holy Spirit and fire
- Baptism in water

## Focused Engagement

We speak against the evils of this world and expose them (Ephesians 5:11), but our primary focus is creating strong disciples who embody Christ's mission. We build according to the results that God measures as successful, continuing the very mission that Jesus started while on earth. He is the head, we are the body (Ephesians 4:15-16), and we are committed to growing the community of the church in love (Ephesians 4:16).

**Our commitment: Give Jesus Christ the results He requires and do not love our lives to the death.**
