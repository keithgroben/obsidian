**The Great Commission Triangle** Three connected sides — evangelism, discipleship, and the power of God demonstrated — forming one closed shape. Remove any side and the triangle collapses.

---

**The Five Anchor Passages** (Jesus' post-resurrection commissioning, one from each — Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Acts)

- **Matthew 28:18–20** — "All authority… Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them… teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you."
- **Mark 16:15–18** — "Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel… And these signs will accompany those who believe…" _(longer ending; bracketed in most ESV editions)_
- **Luke 24:46–49** — "repentance and forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations… stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."
- **John 20:21–22** — "As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you… Receive the Holy Spirit."
- **Acts 1:8** — "you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses…"

**The Power Expansion**

_The prophecy: Spirit and fire_

- **Matthew 3:11** — John the Baptist: "He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and with fire."
- **Luke 3:16** — parallel, same phrase, same speaker.
- _(Mark 1:8 and John 1:33 carry the saying without "and fire" — Spirit only.)_

_Jesus is the one who baptizes in the Spirit_

- **John 1:33** — "he who baptizes with the Holy Spirit"
- **Acts 1:5** — "John baptized with water, but you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit not many days from now."
- **Acts 11:16** — Peter ties it back: "I remembered the word of the Lord… 'you will be baptized with the Holy Spirit.'"

_The prophecy lands — fire becomes visible at Pentecost_

- **Acts 2:1–4** — "divided tongues as of fire… and they were all filled with the Holy Spirit." (_pyr_ / πῦρ — same word as Matthew 3:11 / Luke 3:16; Luke echoes his own earlier language.)

_The purpose of the baptism is power for witness_

- **Acts 1:8** — "you will receive power [_dynamis_] when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses [_martyres_]."
    - _dynamis_ (δύναμις) — power; root of "dynamic," "dynamite"; the Gospels' standard word for miraculous works.
    - _martyres_ (μάρτυρες) — witnesses; root of "martyr"; one who testifies even at cost.
    - The grammar itself: Spirit comes → _dynamis_ received → they become _martyres_. Power is the stated equipping for witness, not an add-on.

_Spirit and power are one equipping (confirmation across the NT)_

- **Luke 24:49** — "clothed with power [_dynamis_] from on high"
- **Acts 10:38** — "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power" (the pattern from Jesus' own ministry, handed to the disciples)
- **Romans 15:13** — "abound in hope by the power of the Holy Spirit"
- **1 Thessalonians 1:5** — "our gospel came to you not only in word, but also in power and in the Holy Spirit"

_The demonstration confirmed in practice_

- **Mark 16:19–20** — "the Lord worked with them and confirmed the message by accompanying signs." _(longer ending — bracketed)_
