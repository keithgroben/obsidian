# The Global Great Commission Alliance: Vision and Purpose

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/2d56abe00ba4804dacedf0c0ae507aa8*

## Core Purpose

The Global Great Commission Alliance brings together leaders, resources, systems, and expertise to strategize and execute a unified plan for advancing the Great Commission. This alliance recognizes that people possess valuable assets beyond finances—they have systems that produce results, resources that can be shared or duplicated, and capabilities acquired through experience that others may need.

## The Four Pillars of Great Commission Results

The alliance is organized around four essential elements found throughout the Gospels and Acts:

### 1. Evangelism
- Sharing the actual gospel message as presented in Scripture (Acts 5:3 and throughout the Gospels)
- Leading people to salvation through the biblical gospel
- Reaching souls with the authentic message of Christ

### 2. Discipleship
- Teaching new disciples to obey all of Christ's commands (Matthew 28:19-20)
- Instructing believers to love God with all their heart, mind, soul, and strength
- Teaching them to love their neighbors as themselves
- Facilitating spiritual growth and obedience

### 3. Power
- Manifestation of signs and wonders
- Gospel power that transforms the human soul
- Miracles happening through the gospel (Romans 1:16 — "not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God to salvation")
- Healing and deliverance—both spiritual (forgiveness of sins) and physical
- Character development through the Holy Spirit's work

### 4. Community
- Building the infrastructure of the local church
- Providing administration and support systems
- Supporting multiple local churches and parachurch ministries
- Creating the organizational structure needed to accomplish the mission
- Following the biblical pattern from Acts: being together in the apostles' doctrine, having all things in common, and accomplishing the mission

## The Biblical Foundation

The early church model demonstrates these four elements working together:
- They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching
- They fellowshipped together and had all things in common
- They experienced signs and wonders
- They grew and fulfilled the mission

This is the standard and pattern we are to continue today.

## Kingdom Focus and the Church

**Key Understanding:** The Kingdom of God in this dispensation is expressed through the church.

- Christ is the head; we are the body
- The work Jesus did, we will continue—and even greater works (John 14:12)
- This is made possible by the Holy Spirit working through believers
- Any "kingdom business" or "kingdom ministry" is automatically qualified by being Great Commission results-oriented

### Current Dispensation
The present age is the dispensation of the church—from Pentecost until Christ's return. Any kingdom work must align with these four elements of the Great Commission.

## The Problem with Fragmentation

Many groups and ministries focus on only one element:
- Some focus solely on evangelism
- Others emphasize only power and miracles
- Some concentrate exclusively on discipleship
- Others focus just on community building
- Some separate business entirely from ministry

**The Reality:** All four elements must work together for complete Great Commission results.

## The Role of Business

Business is an integral part of the church community, not separate from it:

### Biblical Examples:
- The disciples had businesses they returned to
- Paul was a tentmaker
- Jesus was a carpenter before full-time ministry
- In Acts, believers pooled resources and managed property
- They didn't sustain themselves solely through converts' donations
- They perpetuated businesses to fund the work

### Old Testament Wisdom:
- Proverbs and Ecclesiastes speak about wisdom in business
- When possessing land, resources were established on that land
- Business is part of the community infrastructure

## Who Is the Alliance For?

The alliance brings together two primary groups:

### 1. Ministers
- Pastors, evangelists, and various ministry leaders
- Full-time and part-time ministers
- Those called to spiritual leadership

### 2. Entrepreneurs
- Solo entrepreneurs (80% of all businesses)
- Business owners of all types
- Executives and business leaders
- Kingdom-minded businesspeople

## How It Works

Imagine these leaders pooling together:
- **Knowledge and expertise** from different domains
- **Systems** that produce results (ministry and business)
- **Resources** that can be shared, duplicated, or acquired more efficiently
- **Capabilities** that others need but lack
- **Strategic planning** with clear project milestones
- **Committed action** with accountability
- **Financial resources** (in addition to all the non-monetary assets)

### The Synergy Effect:
- Business systems can work in the church
- Church systems can work in business
- Both are part of the organizational architecture of the Body of Christ

## The Vision

When ministers and entrepreneurs come together with Great Commission focus:

1. **Greater Scale and Impact**
   - Churches built more effectively
   - Ministries supported more substantially
   - Businesses grown with kingdom purpose

2. **Multiplied Results**
   - More effective evangelism
   - Deeper discipleship
   - Greater demonstrations of power
   - Sustainable business growth
   - Stronger community infrastructure

3. **Unified Structure**
   - Clear strategy and planning
   - Defined projects with milestones
   - Committed people working together
   - Coordinated action across the Body of Christ

## The Distinction

**Great Commission-Oriented vs. Ministry-Oriented**

Not all churches and ministries qualify for this alliance. The key distinction is:

- **Great Commission Results:** Focused on the four pillars—evangelism, discipleship, power, and community
- **Ministry Results:** Focused on their own organizational goals rather than advancing the biblical mandate

God is behind churches and ministries committed to Great Commission results. The alliance exists to unite those who share this commitment and to maximize their collective impact for the Kingdom.

---

## Summary

The Global Great Commission Alliance is about unifying leaders and resources around the biblical pattern of church growth and kingdom advancement. By bringing together the expertise, systems, and resources of ministers and entrepreneurs, and by maintaining focus on all four essential elements—evangelism, discipleship, power, and community—we can achieve far greater results than any individual or isolated ministry could accomplish alone.

This is not just about money. It's about pooling everything we have—knowledge, systems, experience, relationships, and yes, finances—to fulfill the mission Christ gave us before ascending to the Father.
