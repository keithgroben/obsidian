## OPENING

I spent 8 years in lay ministry, then 10 years as a senior pastor. Then 7 years in business mastering marketing and operational systems.
And what I keep seeing is the same gap everywhere: **ministers have vision but lack systems and funding. Entrepreneurs have systems and money but lack Kingdom projects worth pouring into.**
I'm bridging that gap with **the Great Commission Alliance**.

---

## WHAT THE ALLIANCE IS

**The Great Commission Alliance is a network of ministers and entrepreneurs who pool their knowledge, systems, resources, and capabilities** to generate Great Commission results.

Not ministry results: Great Commission results. There's a difference.

| MINISTRY RESULTS | GREAT COMMISSION RESULTS |
|---|---|
| Focused on their own organizational goals | **Evangelism — winning souls** |
| Building programs | **Discipleship — making disciples who make disciples** |
| Growing attendance | **Power — demonstrations of the Spirit** |
| Maintaining the institution | **Community — the Body functioning as one** |

Great Commission Results build a ministry.

Many churches and members are focused on ministry results. This alliance is for the ones focused on Great Commission results. That's the filter.

---

## HOW IT WORKS

Imagine you have ministers on one side and entrepreneurs on the other. Both have something the other needs:

| MINISTERS BRING... | ENTREPRENEURS BRING... |
|---|---|
| Vision and spiritual leadership | Business systems that produce results |
| Congregations to mobilize | Marketing and operational expertise |
| Kingdom projects that need resourced | Resourcing capacity |
| Discipleship frameworks | Operational discipline that executes |
| Relationships and community trust | Systems, technology and automation |

**Both are part of the organizational architecture of the Body of Christ.**
