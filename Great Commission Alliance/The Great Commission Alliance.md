## Introduction: The Acts Restoration Movement

We believe we are witnessing the restoration of the Book of Acts in function within the body of Christ - what could be called the Acts Restoration Movement, a great move of God that some consider the last great move of God. While we cannot be certain if it is the final move, one thing is sure: it is great.

This move is marked by people who are moving toward God rather than waiting for God to move toward them. They are the ones who will hasten the return of Christ (2 Peter 3:12). They understand the urgency to live holy because the time is very near (1 Peter 4:7). They are unafraid to rebel against the rebellion that stands against God, working together quickly and speedily on systems and projects in partnership to build effectively for our Lord and Master.

Jesus started something and declared, "The works that I do you will do also, and greater works than these will you do" (John 14:12) - not because He stopped working, but because He continues His work through us. This alliance and its accompanying manifesto will become a banner for all those who refuse to preserve their own life but choose to lose it for His sake and the Gospel's (Mark 8:35).

The sun is setting on our time before Christ's return. We must operate as sober-minded, proven disciples of Jesus Christ (1 Peter 4:7), coordinating our efforts for maximum kingdom impact.

**Commitment Statement:** By joining this alliance you agree to hold yourself accountable to giving Jesus Christ Great Commission Results as detailed in scripture (Matthew 28:19-20) and outlined in our manifesto. You agree to coordinate your efforts, resources, and time with other alliance members voluntarily to look forward to and hasten the return of Jesus Christ (2 Peter 3:12).

**Participation Model:** The Great Commission Alliance operates on a contribution-based model where deeper involvement requires greater commitment of time and resources.

**Three Levels of Involvement:**

**Collaborators** may attend monthly meetups and access meeting archives to learn from alliance strategies and projects. Primary benefit is gaining insights and encouragement from seeing coordinated Great Commission efforts in action.

**Participants** contribute time, skills, and resources to alliance projects and systems. Gain access to member directory for networking and collaboration opportunities. Time and resource commitments are specific and measurable based on project needs.

**Members** may introduce projects for alliance consideration and vote on project adoption, while committing equal value toward existing alliance initiatives. Full access to member directory and voting rights on alliance direction, ensuring mutual investment in our shared mission.

---

**Accepted Contributions:**

- Skilled time involvement in active projects
- Intellectual, virtual, or real property contributions
- Professional consultation toward active projects (Members only)
- Currency (fiat and digital)

**Important Note:** The Great Commission Alliance does not retain financial donations. All monetary contributions flow directly to the organizations executing specific projects, ensuring transparency and direct kingdom impact.
