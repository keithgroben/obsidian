# Definitions and Vision — Letter to Calvary Leadership

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ihDfx2mXdoz7ACzIM-VlLpTLOsdncs4L2Cj8xqtWGqM*
*Written during Keith's Calvary Assembly of God pastorate*

This letter is intended to give the leadership of Calvary Assembly of God definition of their roles individually and as a team, and some vision as to where God is leading us as a team, family and church. As with anything you read or hear, please do so prayerfully and with the mind of Christ.

The perfecting God does in us is His alone. It is not for us to stand in judgment on about us or anyone else.

---

## Justin & Elizabeth

You both have held with integrity the faith entrusted to you. You belong and are important in this house of faith. Make no mistake, you belong here. God determines where we should live at the times we should live there. He will continue to supply provision for you.

The refiner's fire is hot on your tails. An adjustment in mindset will make this all the more clear, important and easy to bear for both of you. There are things He is doing to you together and separate. However, you are being drawn closer to one another and God like never before.

### Justin
Place no confidence in the flesh, evaluate and do it again.

### Elizabeth
The help you want and need has finally arrived and the Lord has heard about it.

## Eric and Vallory

The Lord has laid out a decision for you all to make. The stakes are high and the reward is eternal. As it becomes clear what He is offering to you, will you take it or will you let it go?

## Kaylie

Do all that is in your heart to do for the Lord. Use your freedom for the sake of finding more freedom.

## Jordan

You are a valuable and treasured son, please act like it.
