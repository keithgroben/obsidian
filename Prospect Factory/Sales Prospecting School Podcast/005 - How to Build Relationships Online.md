# 005 - How to Build Relationships Online
## Summary
John Stephenson and Ted Stevenot reframe digital marketing as "digital prospecting": using online channels not to force a quick sale but to meet people, gather a return path, and build long-term relationships. Their core argument is that you should own a controlled hub (a self-hosted website) rather than depend on platforms whose algorithms and monetization can change overnight. They walk through choosing channels by where customers actually live, earning traction in one channel at a time, capturing email permission, telling your story over time, and differentiating through a unique "content tilt" that flows from genuine value.
## Key Takeaways
- Digital prospecting is about building relationships and a return path over time, not forcing a single-click sale; treat it as the online cousin of the phone work covered in [[Part 2: Phone Calls]].
- Don't try to occupy every channel; go where your customer base already lives and earn traction in one channel before spreading thin, the way you'd disciplined a phone effort through [[Time Blocking]].
- Platforms like YouTube and Facebook can change algorithms or demonetize you overnight, so the priority is control: own a self-hosted website hub that no third party can alter.
- Email is the most powerful return path because it reaches the inbox directly and lets you tell your story in pieces over time, much like building a permission-based [[Plus List]].
- Use traction channels (social, video, podcast, ads) to funnel people back to the website hub where you have control and can capture a return path.
- Differentiate with a 'content tilt' or unique selling position, which can come from experience, a fresh approach, or combining mediocre skills into something distinctive.
- A website is the place to build a patient library of well-thought-out answers to the objections and questions you hear repeatedly, turning recurring [[Part 3: Acceptance]] situations into reusable content.
- Lead with genuine value and 'atomize' your core content into many compatible formats so it travels well across channels and pulls long-term prospects into your funnel.
