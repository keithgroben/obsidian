# 042 - Listener Question - A Day in the Life of an Effective Prospector
## Summary
Responding to a Facebook listener question, John and Ted break down what a productive prospecting day actually looks like. Drawing on Jeb Blount's Fanatical Prospecting and Ted's 30-year career, they argue effectiveness rests on a "result triangle" of routine, objective, and metrics rather than tricks. Core points: prospect every morning at peak energy, stop procrastinating with prep, set the right objective (qualifying a mailing list, not closing on the call), track dials above all, and mix media (phone, email, mail). They frame skill growth as an apprentice-journeyman-master continuum and stress compounding daily discipline.
## Key Takeaways
- Build every prospecting day on a result triangle of routine, objective, and metrics; when the work feels painful, all three components work together to relieve it and keep you moving, which connects directly to [[4.1 Every Day of Phoning Is a Good Day]].
- Do your calling in the morning at peak energy and use [[Time Blocking]] so the routine itself spares you the willpower cost of deciding when to start.
- Set the right [[Call Objective]] before dialing; Ted's calls mostly qualify a mailing list rather than chase an immediate sale, which keeps expectations sane and an unsold call still counts as a win.
- Treat rejection as free intelligence that improves your [[Plus List]] and your [[Mailing Database]] every time someone disqualifies themselves, which is exactly why [[6.1 Dealing with Sales Rejection]] gets so much easier with reps.
- Stop letting research and list-perfecting masquerade as prospecting; preparing a list is not prospecting, so improve the list after hours, not during call time (see [[2.6 Keeping Your Lists Updated]]).
- Watch dials per hour as the one operative metric while calling; tracking [[Call Length]] and ratios over time is how yield (results over dials) climbs, the heart of [[6.3 Learning Your Ratios]].
- Mix media to lift yield: phone is the richest and hardest touch, but supplementing with email and direct mail builds know-like-trust and lets you reach prospects past tough gatekeepers, tying into [[4.2 Synergy Between the Plus List and Direct Mail]].
- Use your CRM and [[OK Code]] system so call outcomes auto-trigger templated follow-up email and mail, but don't gate your first 100 calls on building that automation.
