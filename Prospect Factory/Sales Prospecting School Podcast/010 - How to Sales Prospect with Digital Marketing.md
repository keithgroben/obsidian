# 010 - How to Sales Prospect with Digital Marketing
## Summary
John and Ted respond to a Digital Marketing Podcast episode on B2B social selling, pushing back on the framing that cold calling and direct mail are archaic. Their core argument: old-school and new-school methods are not at war; the strongest results come from mixing them. Digital content should reach a functionally targetable audience, deliver real value, and open a communication channel (usually email) that can escalate to a phone call. They stress aligning sales and marketing roles, measuring genuine value conversion over vanity likes, listening to prospects, and avoiding overzealous spam on either channel.
## Key Takeaways
- Old-school and new-school marketing should not be set against each other; combining phone, mail, and digital content beats relying on any one channel, which is why follow-up email and the [[Plus List]] complement live calls rather than replace them.
- Social media only works for B2B when it can reach a functionally targetable audience, which makes [[2.1 Defining Your Market]] the deciding factor for whether a given platform is worth using at all.
- Vanity metrics like likes and shares are not real value; measure an actual value conversion such as an email opt-in or a returned form, the same discipline behind [[6.3 Learning Your Ratios]].
- Digital content should funnel to a personal contact, so a call or email after engagement mirrors the synergy described in [[5.2 Automation and Follow Up]] and turns interest into a relationship.
- Most rejection on a cold call is aimed at product, price, timing, or methodology rather than the person, and internalizing that distinction is the heart of [[6.1 Dealing with Sales Rejection]].
- Sales and marketing must share roles and information instead of dumping blame on the salesperson, echoing the role-clarity and handoff discipline of [[Part 1: Groundwork]].
- Being overly aggressive on any channel kills the goose, whether it is the ignored Sirius XM call that forced a personal rejection or relentless retargeting ads with no [[OK Code]]-style listening to prospect interest.
- Effective social selling rests on consistency, give-and-take listening, and becoming a genuine thought leader in a narrow niche rather than a jack-of-all-trades, all built on a deliberate, easy-to-execute system.
