# 004 - How to Make Follow-Up with Sales Prospects Easy
## Summary
This episode argues that following up with sales prospects is most effective when it is simple, systematic, and easy to execute. The hosts open with a story about a pushy subscription-renewal call to illustrate the value of listening and respecting a "no." They then dig into the "one-pager": a single-sheet leverage piece built from five elements (flag line, supporting copy, general description with a unique value proposition, a proactive reply with call to action, and full contact info). They cover sending it by mail, email, and fax, and how a full pipeline produces inbound leads almost automatically.
## Key Takeaways
- A one-pager is a single-sheet workhorse used to follow up with prospects who showed marginal interest or who are hard to reach by phone, and [[3.2 The Five Basic Outcomes to Most Calls]] often ends in the offer to send one.
- Lead the follow-up with permission rather than assumption: instead of waiting for someone to ask, say 'would it be okay to send something,' which ties directly to the [[OK Code]] permission framing.
- The five elements of a one-pager are a flag line, supporting copy, a general description carrying your unique value proposition, a proactive reply with a clear call to action, and complete contact information, all covered in [[2.7 Mastering One-Pagers]].
- Use a provocative question as the flag line (a content-marketing-style headline) because good questions make no assumptions and invite the prospect to analyze their own situation.
- Asking for more detailed information in the reply form lowers volume but raises quality, producing fewer but better-qualified leads worth following up on.
- Easy execution is the secret weapon: cheap number-ten envelopes, copy paper, a first-class self-adhesive stamp, and a warm handwritten Post-it note make a [[6.4 Persistence]]-friendly system you can crank out daily.
- Storing a stable of 3-10 reusable one-pagers in your [[Customer Relationship Management Database (CRM)]] lets you fold the right piece into an envelope in seconds, building a trust bridge with almost no mental energy.
- Once the pipeline fills, follow-up materials generate inbound replies on their own, supporting [[5.2 Automation and Follow Up]] and turning prospecting into a self-running machine.
