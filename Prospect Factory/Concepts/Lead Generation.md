# Lead Generation

In Stevenot's system, lead generation is the engine room of the whole prospect factory. It is not a single tactic but a manufacturing process: you define a target market, build relationships and visibility (online and off), and run repeatable mechanisms that convert attention into named, reachable prospects who fill the pipeline. The "factory" framing is deliberate. Leads should come off a line, predictably and in volume, rather than from sporadic effort or luck.

The pieces fit together as inputs and machinery. Digital strategy, PPC, content, and marketing automation are the "four horsemen" that drive top-of-funnel flow, while the prospecting letter and referred-lead work convert that flow into real conversations. Automation and a defined system keep the line running without constant hand-feeding, so output scales beyond what one rep can do manually. The synergy with referred-lead prospecting matters here: generated leads and warm referrals reinforce each other rather than compete.

Why it matters: prospecting is the constraint on every sales result downstream, so the most important metric is how reliably the factory produces qualified leads. Maximizing the value of prospecting means annexing and compounding results over time, treating each campaign as tuning a system rather than a one-off push.
