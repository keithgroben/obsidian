# The Plus List

The Plus List is Stevenot's term for the prospect database that sits at the center of the Prospect Factory system: the running, organized list of people and businesses you've identified as worth pursuing. "Plus listing" is the act of adding a qualified name to it. The list isn't a one-time pull — it's an asset you build and maintain so that every prospect you ever touch stays in the funnel rather than leaking out after a single contact.

Its power comes from repetition over time. Because the Plus List is durable, it pairs naturally with direct mail: you send a prospecting letter, then another, then another to the same names, and the cumulative effect compounds. A single mailing converts almost no one; the same list mailed consistently keeps you in front of prospects until timing, trust, and need finally line up. That compounding is the whole point — the list turns scattered outreach into a system.

This is why the letter mechanics matter so much here. The five key pieces of a prospecting letter, the 12-step template, and the synergy between the Plus List and direct mail all describe the same loop: build the list, mail it well, mail it again, and let consistency do the heavy lifting that no single clever touch ever could.
