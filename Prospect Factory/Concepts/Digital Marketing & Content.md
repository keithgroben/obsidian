# Digital Marketing & Content

In Stevenot's system, Digital Marketing & Content is the inbound counterpart to direct prospecting: instead of only reaching out one-to-one, you build assets and channels that pull qualified prospects toward you and keep them moving down the pipeline. It treats lead generation as the engine of sales (episode 001) and frames digital work as a deliberate strategy rather than scattered tactics — a content strategy for finding prospects (015, 028), a paid-traffic layer through PPC (022), and the relationship-building that online presence makes possible (005).

The pieces fit together as a funnel. A documented digital strategy (021-023) sets the plan; content and online relationships fill the top; PPC accelerates volume; and marketing automation (023, 036) handles the unglamorous middle and bottom — follow-up made easier (009), data used to refine targeting (011), and prospecting value maximized over time (008). Stevenot bundles these into memorable frameworks like the "Four Horsemen" of lead generation (032-033) and "10 Rules for Marketing Your Business" (034).

The throughline is that digital marketing should feed a real pipeline (007), not just generate clicks — every asset, ad, and automated sequence exists to produce and nurture prospects you can actually close, including in niche markets (025) and against alternatives like cold email or cold calling (026).
