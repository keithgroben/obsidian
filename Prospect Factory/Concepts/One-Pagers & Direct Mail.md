# One-Pagers & Direct Mail

In Stevenot's system, the one-pager is the workhorse document of prospecting: a single, tightly written sheet that introduces you, frames the value you offer, and earns the next conversation rather than asking for the sale. It exists to be mailed, and direct mail is where it does its work. The core mechanic is the Plus List — your warmest, highest-potential prospects — paired with a disciplined Mail Cycle: you don't blast everyone once, you send to the Plus List on repeat through Incremental Mailing, layering touches over time so familiarity compounds and each piece builds on the last.

That repetition is the point. Stevenot treats the prospecting letter as a teachable structure — five key pieces, a 12-step template — not an inspired one-off, so it can be reproduced and improved instead of agonized over. Follow-up letters extend the same cycle, keeping you in front of prospects without leaning on cold calls. The Properties of Mail (it's tangible, it waits to be opened, it can't be deleted in a swipe) are what make this channel forgiving and durable where email and calls are not.

The synergy is the whole engine: a strong one-pager plus a defined Plus List plus a steady mail cadence turns prospecting from sporadic outreach into a system that runs on schedule and quietly debunks the myth that prospecting must mean constant cold-calling.
