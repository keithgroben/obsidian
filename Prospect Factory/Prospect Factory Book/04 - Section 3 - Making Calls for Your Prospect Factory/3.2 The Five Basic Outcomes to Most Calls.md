# 3.2 The Five Basic Outcomes to Most Calls

A call "outcome" is the result of a call. Below is a list of the five most basic types of call outcomes. In parenthesis are some estimated percentages for the various types of call outcomes based on my experience.

These percentages apply mainly to primary lists. Your results may vary depending on the type of product you sell, the quality of your list and the timing of your call. Also, note the possible "OK" Codes included with each call outcome.

The five basic call outcomes are as follows:

1. **The prospect is unavailable** - (50% of calls)
Possible "OK" Codes: "1" (Not Interested Currently), "2" (Send One-Pager by mail), "3" (Answering Machine, Busy Signal), or "5" (Send Immediate Email).

2. **The prospect is not interested** - (20% of calls)
Possible "OK" Code: "1" (Not Interested Currently).

3. **The prospect expresses future interest** - (8% of calls)
Possible "OK" Codes: "1" (Not Interested Currently), "2" (Send One-Pager by Mail) or "5" (Send Immediate Email).

4. **The prospect is interested** - (.07-2% of calls)
"OK" Code: "6" (Finished Lead).

5. **The prospect is disqualified** - (20% of calls)
(Less than 1% requesting "Do Not Call" or "Take me off your list," 19% are disconnected, out of business, no longer at this number or other insurmountable obstacle in which case the prospect should be coded as either a "4" and be double-checked later, or as a "7" or "8" and taken off your list at the appropriate time.)
Possible "OK" Codes: "4" (Disconnected Number), "7" (Disqualified Prospect) or "8" (Take Off List).

Based on these numbers, I know that when making telephone-prospecting calls I will experience a predictable variety of outcomes as a result of my calls.

And, I accept this.

I watch these outcomes materialize while I am calling like a state trooper watches cars go by on a highway. I "switch" on my mental radar so I can filter out and track the prospects I am interested in pursuing. As time goes by, interested and better-qualified prospects reveal themselves. By working in this manner, most calls are no more than routine or transactional.
