# 3.1 Call Objectives

## Understanding Call Objectives

The format used for making phone calls in this system is designed to achieve the objectives of:

- Comfortably putting you on the playing field in front of your prospects.
- Leaving the door open for greater interest to shine through when it is encountered.
- Giving you a clear expectation of claiming victory at the end of a day of calling even if you find no Finished Leads over the phone.

You will always be able to claim such a victory because at the end of every day of calling,

1. You will have undoubtedly found a list of prospects that gave you permission to send them One-Pagers.
2. You will have collected a significant amount of new, qualifying information about the prospects on your list, thereby improving it.

## Listening and Discerning

On the phone, it only takes a matter of seconds to establish who you are and what your call is about. After that, start listening carefully to discern which prospects are:

- Receptive prospects.
- Currently interested prospects.
- Better quality prospects.
- Prospects suited for other opportunities.

With practice, prospects' responses are easy to assess. By being mindful of what you are looking for, you will be able to categorize the type of response you are getting, and then quickly apply an appropriate "OK" Code to the call.
