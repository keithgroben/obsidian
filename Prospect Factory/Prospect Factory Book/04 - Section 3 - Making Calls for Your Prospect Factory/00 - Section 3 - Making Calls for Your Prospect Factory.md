# Section 3: Making Calls for Your Prospect Factory

At this point you have learned the basic framework elements required to build a Prospect Factory of your own. Now let's consider the essentials involved with making telephone-prospecting calls in conjunction with this system. As we progress through this section you will see how the calls bring together and make sense out of much of what you have already learned.

In this section, you will note that the most difficult challenge with making calls is not so much about figuring out how to handle Finished Leads once you find them, but rather: ==the most difficult challenge with making calls is figuring out what to do with all the not interested and not available prospects you come across while you are looking for Finished Leads.==

The first part of this section includes some discussion about setting objectives for making calls and how to categorize common outcomes to most calls. The second part addresses overviews about calling such as call length, pace, voice and temperament. The third part looks at essentials on "how to" make calls by breaking down and studying the elements of 25 sample calls.
