## Author's Note

In the early 1990s, starting out with $50 and a phonebook, my business partner and I built a company that would go on to make millions.

My principal duty for the business was sales "prospector."

This book shares lessons and systems learned from that 30 plus year career and after personally making over 500,000 sales calls.

In an age of hacks, shortcuts, and unicorn viral-success stories, it is my hope the world will benefit from a reminder that old-fashioned smart, hard-work still has a place when it comes to achieving success.

Best,

Ted Stevenot
