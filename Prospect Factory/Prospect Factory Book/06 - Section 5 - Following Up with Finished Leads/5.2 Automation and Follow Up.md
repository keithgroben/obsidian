# 5.2 Automation and Follow Up

Codifying and organizing follow-up opens the door to automated processes which can be a huge help. Most Customer Relationship Management (CRM) software systems include "workflow" utilities that can be triggered to set off a cascade of follow-up actions such as updating a lead's status and sending email messages at predetermined intervals.

The emails that are sent correspond with a template library that is populated with pre-written messages. Through email data-merges (i.e. Hi <First_Name> = "Hi Tom", etc.) messages can feel as if they written on a personal basis.

Thinking through the steps to automate recurring follow-up can be a game changer. Automating follow-up saves time, reduces errors, and improves your overall chance of success.
