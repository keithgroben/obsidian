# 5.4 Sample Follow-Up Letters

The letters introduced above will need to be written in specific terms to match up with your individual business. For a general start, however, I have included on the next few pages samples of five of the letters mentioned (i.e. letters "1", "5", "6", "7", and "8").

**Sample Follow-Up Letter 1: *Thank You for the Opportunity***

This message thanks the prospect for the opportunity and informs the prospect that you will be re-contacting him or her soon. I also work into the message a one-line statement referencing a unique value proposition. In this case, 25 years of experience with widgets.

> Hi <First Name>,
>
> It was great to speak with you today.
>
> Our company has been guiding businesses like yours with widgets for over 25 years.
>
> Thank you for the opportunity to be of help, and we will be back with you soon!
>
> Best,
>
> Your Name
> (Your complete contact information.)

**Sample Follow-Up Letter 5: *Your Quotes Are Ready***

Depending on your business, this message could take at least three different paths:

- Request that the prospect give you a call to set up a time to see proposals.
- Inform the prospect that you will be calling soon to set up a time to present proposals.
- Send the proposals with the message - with details on how the prospect can take action to buy – and promise to follow up.

> Hi <First Name>,
>
> A quick note to let you know, we received the widget quotes we recently requested on your behalf.
>
> See the attached quote sheet with a preliminary product comparison.
>
> Should you find this proposal favorable, the next step is to complete a purchase order. Attached is the order form you'll need including instructions.
>
> I will give you a call soon to see if you have any other questions.
>
> Regards,
>
> Your Name
> (Complete Contact Information)

**Sample Follow-Up Letter 6: *How do things look?***

The main reason I include a sample of this letter is to highlight its brevity. I am looking for input and feedback from the prospect as well as meeting my obligation to follow-up. Sometimes, a simple message like this is all it takes.

> Hi <First Name>,
>
> Just a quick follow-up regarding the widget quotes we recently sent you.
>
> How do things look?
>
> If you have questions or would like to see additional options, please let me know!
>
> Thanks,
>
> Your Name
> (Complete Contact Information)

**Sample Follow-Up Letter 7: *Any additional questions?***

Here again, it doesn't take a ponderous tome to do a good job of following up.

> Hi <First Name>,
>
> A quick note to make sure I am getting back to you with all the information you need regarding the proposals we recently sent.
>
> Are there any additional questions you have that need follow-up? Let me know!
>
> Best,
>
> Your Name
> (Complete Contact Information)

**Sample Follow-Up Letter 8: *Thanks again for the opportunity!***

This letter is appreciative and gracious, and it concludes the follow-up process. This is the time to say,

> *"I appreciate your interest, but now I think it is best to leave the future in your hands. I trust you will call me back if you have any additional questions. Thank you again for giving me this chance to be of service to you!"*

Once you have made a regular practice of sending a "release" message such as this, you will notice how a small, yet significant, percentage of prospects will hurriedly reply after receiving it and say, "Wait a minute! Don't release me yet! I'm still interested!"

> Hi <First Name>,
>
> This letter concludes my follow-up regarding the quotes and information I prepared for you on widgets.
>
> I appreciate the opportunity to be of service and am happy to help you in any way I can in the future!
>
> Please feel free to give me a call if you have further interest or questions.
>
> Regards,
>
> Your Name
> (Complete Contact Information)
