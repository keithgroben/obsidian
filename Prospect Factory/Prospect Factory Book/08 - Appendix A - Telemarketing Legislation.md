# Appendix A: Telemarketing Legislation

Federal telemarketing legislation was first enacted in the 1990s. Since then, federal and state legislatures have considered and, in many cases, enacted further legislation about telemarketing.

When I published the first edition of *Prospect Factory* in the early 2000s, such legislation was in a highly fluid and formative period. Then and now, a book remains an impracticable medium for providing up-to-date information regarding compliance with such laws.

*Therefore, it is critically important that you learn what you must do in order to comply with all pertinent industry, trade, communication, marketing, and telemarketing laws - and any other laws - in the jurisdictions in which you do business. If a law prohibits a technique outlined in this book, then do <u>not</u> utilize that technique!*

Here are some Internet sites that offer helpful information regarding telemarketing rules:

**The Direct Marketing Association**
www.the-dma.org

**The Federal Communications Commission**
www.fcc.gov

**The Federal Trade Commission**
www.ftc.gov
