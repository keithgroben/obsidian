# About the Author

Ted Stevenot graduated from St. Louis University in 1987 and started working in sales immediately after graduation. He started his own business at age 25 and has been successfully self-employed ever since.

*Prospect Factory* was first published in 2004. In recent years, two of Ted's children began working at startups – one in the Midwest and one in Los Angeles. When asked for advice about how to find and grow new business, he found himself handing out copies of the old book. Soon, the heat was on to write a revised edition.

Ted can be heard regularly on the *Sales Prospecting School* podcast (available on iTunes). Contact Ted online at: tedstevenot.com
