# Putting the Parts Together

Each part of the system is related to the next. At the start, market parameters are clearly defined, and an initial list is compiled and processed within those parameters using great care. Next, primary research is instigated to further qualify the prospects on the initial list. Primary research takes place in a variety of forms including, where permitted, telephone-prospecting calls.

Telephone-prospecting calls are made with the dual purpose of finding currently interested prospects and collecting qualifying information. By actively focusing attention on collecting qualifying information, a process of categorizing, upgrading, and downgrading prospects is set in motion.

As a byproduct of this process, subcategory prospect lists are revealed. One valuable subcategory list, for example, is a list of disqualified prospects. Identifying and keeping track of disqualified prospects is helpful because it facilitates the removal of such prospects from future lists and streamlines future prospecting efforts.

The most valuable subcategory list, however, is the [[Plus List]]. The Plus List has a high value because it contains a concentration of better-qualified prospects. This value is unlocked when the Plus List is used as the basis for a mailing list.

In my use of this system, I achieve a better results when I send mail to my Plus List than I do by sending mail to a typical mailing list of the same size, hands down. In fact, the initial list I start off with looks more like a typical mailing list than does my Plus List.

*Though I have seen my ratios move up and down over time, there have been times when well over 50% of my Finished Leads from a given prospecting cycle were derived from One-Pagers sent to Plus List prospects.*

One-Pagers used in mailings are also linked to the format of the overall system. One-Pagers are designed to be suitable for high-frequency, low-pressure contact. When the system is in motion, telephone-prospecting calls are made, and One-Pagers are sent to the same prospects again and again over time.

In the future, new names are added to the initial prospect list as they become available and disqualified names are set aside. The overall process is then repeated on an ongoing basis.

As time goes by, the initial prospect list and the Plus List become better and better qualified, efficiencies increase, and more and more ground is gained. All the while, a generous supply of Finished Leads is produced.
