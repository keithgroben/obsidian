# Looking Ahead

Decades ago, I gave this method of prospecting the name "Prospect Factory" because it is so systematic. Prospect names are put in one end and carefully taken through a series of steps that result in the production of Finished Leads at the end of the line.

The process is largely unemotional, and other than requiring consistent effort – work not much more mentally demanding than walking on a treadmill at the gym – it involves very few intangibles such as personality, charm, persuasiveness, good looks or a strong natural market of contacts. Further, this system does not require that you pursue or acquire referral leads, although it creates no conflict with referrals should you happen to obtain them.

One item not detailed in this book is addressing how to sell a Finished Lead once you have found one. Throughout this book, the assumption is made that once you have established a favorable connection with a receptive, currently interested prospect, you will be able to take it from there. While some ideas about organizing and managing follow-up are presented, the central focus remains on the dilemma of finding Finished Leads in the first place.

The next few sections begin the detailed description of how to build a Prospect Factory of your own.
