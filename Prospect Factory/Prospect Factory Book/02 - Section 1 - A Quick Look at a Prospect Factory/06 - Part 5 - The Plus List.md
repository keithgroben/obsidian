# Part 5: The Plus List

The fifth part of this system separates the smaller, more qualified list of prospects identified through calls and research from your initial prospect list. This smaller list of prospects is used to create a new list. This list is called the "[[Plus List]]," and Plus List prospects are treated differently than the prospects on your initial list. Because more information is known about these prospects and they are better qualified, extra time, attention and money is spent pursuing them.

The kind of information that can cause a prospect to be upgraded to the Plus List may vary from prospect to prospect. In the case of a telephone-prospecting call, the decision whether to upgrade a prospect from the initial list occurs immediately after a prospect is called.

As an example, if a call is placed to a prospective business customer requesting to speak with the person in charge of buying what is being sold and the receptionist responds,

"Oh, that's handled by Susan Jones."

Merely because the receptionist states that the company has a person who is designated to oversee the product being sold sometimes makes this prospect a little better qualified than a prospect about which nothing is known. Identifying this type of subtlety, however, slight, is the first element involved in the creation of a Plus List.
