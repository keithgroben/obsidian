# A Simplified View of a Prospect Factory

(An arrow loops from Finished Leads back up to Calls.)

1. **Groundwork.** Think about what it is you are selling. Formulate a general description of who buys your products and services and where they are located. Create or acquire an initial prospect list based on this information. Ideally the initial list contains thousands of names, but a smaller list could be used depending on the circumstances.

2. **Calls.** Where permissible, make telephone-prospecting calls to the list. Some of the people you call will become Finished Leads right on the phone.

3. **Acceptance.** Do not fight or argue with prospects over their responses. Accept prospects as they present themselves. If a prospect isn't currently interested, let go!

4. **Qualifying Information.** As calls are made, listen carefully to prospects' responses. As you do, valuable information will be revealed. Record useful information and code better-quality prospects future follow-up.

5. **[[Plus List]].** Separate the better-quality prospects into a separate list.

6. **One-Pagers.** Unlock the value of the Plus List by systematically sending One-Pagers its members by mail.

7. **Finished Leads.** The total number of leads from the phone and One-Pagers are combined. Over time, this total ends up being a substantial quantity of Finished Leads.

By working the system consistently and incrementally, leads emerge in a steady and manageable stream. In the future, disqualified prospects are set aside, and lists are refreshed by adding new prospect names as they become available.
