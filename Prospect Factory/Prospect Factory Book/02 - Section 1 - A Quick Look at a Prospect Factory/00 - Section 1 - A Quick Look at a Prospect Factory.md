# Section 1: A Quick Look at a Prospect Factory

The prospecting method presented in this book has an organized structure. It utilizes a collection of materials and activities which, when brought together, consistently produces large numbers of qualified sales leads over time.

A critical element is to establish a routine or "track" on which to run for prospecting. One benefit of doing so is that it requires less mental energy to execute a task when it is well thought out and blueprinted in advance.

You want to conserve and make the best use of your energy and stay focused on the things that matter most. Think of the eccentric genius who wears the same color outfit all the time. What's the reason? Not to waste precious mental fuel on inessentials that may throw him or her off course.

This system takes a similar approach. Once the machine is set up and running, it requires minimal mental expense and energy to keep momentum going.
