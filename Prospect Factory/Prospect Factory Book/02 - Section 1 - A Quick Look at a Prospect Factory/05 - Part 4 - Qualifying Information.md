# Part 4: Qualifying Information

The fourth part of this prospecting system relates to collecting and managing information. One unique element of telephone-prospecting calls is that a few of the prospects called will reveal themselves to be "Finished Leads" directly over the phone.

A *Finished Lead* is a prospect who establishes current interest in your products or services and who is willing to give you a favorable opportunity to present, meet with and/or follow up with him or her about what you sell.

Other prospects, however, will not be this interested. The focus with these other prospects will be to listen and collect what useful qualifying information you can about them.

As you make calls, the names on your prospect list are presented carefully and notes are taken about any useful or pertinent information you find along the way. The information you collect while calling is not "pushed" for. Instead, it is collected as it falls into your hands.

When you finish working you will have identified by keeping track of such information, a smaller list of prospect names. Depending on the circumstances, this smaller list may only be about 20-30% of the size of the original list. However, this new list will consist of a group of more highly qualified prospects.

The information you collect about prospects will only be valuable if you can make productive use of it. This system uses a set of numeric tags called "OK Codes" (more on the origin of this unusual name later) that enable you to classify and direct follow-up requests to the various qualified-through individuals identified through calling. The codes are easy to use and update once acquired through your Customer Relationship Management (CRM) database system.
