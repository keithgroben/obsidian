# Nuts and Bolts

The various elements of this prospecting system address:

- Establishing a concrete definition of the market for your goods and services.
- Developing a prospect list that encompasses your entire market or a targeted segment of it.
- Establishing a means to connect with and collect qualifying information about prospects in your market.
- "Unlocking" the value of qualifying information through systematic outreach.
- Developing proactive collateral (i.e. sales letters, email, direct response materials, etc.) to be used in your prospecting and follow-up activity.
- Maintaining and refreshing the system once in place.

While there is a considerable amount of detail regarding these and other issues presented here, let's begin with a brief overview of the six most significant component parts of the overall prospecting system.

All the following parts are related. In the end, they are joined to create a complete and integrated system.
