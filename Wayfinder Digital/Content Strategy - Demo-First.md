# Content Strategy: Demo-First

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/3106abe00ba48181b1bfd4c5a163aa6c*

## The Shift
Old model: Create content that attracts an audience and hope the right people find you.
New model: Create demonstrations that you show to specific people. The content is a byproduct.

The primary use is the one-to-one or one-to-few demo. The secondary use is public content. Most people do it backwards. They create for the masses and hope individuals notice. **You create for the individual and let the masses benefit from the overflow.**

## Content Is the Door, Not the Destination
Content doesn't replace connection. It amplifies it. Broadcasting into the void and hoping the right people show up is the loneliest possible growth strategy. It's the one that builders-who-don't-love-networking default to because it lets them keep building.

The people who found Disney, Franklin, Groeschel didn't find them through content alone. They found them through proximity. Physical rooms. Conversations. Shared projects.

## The Demo as Pillar Content
A presentation designed to be shown to one or two people, polished enough to work across every context: cast to a TV, shared on Zoom, pulled up on a phone, or dropped into a funnel.

**One asset, many surfaces.** The pillar is the demo. Everything else is fragments of it.

## Atomization Path
Once a demo is recorded, it becomes pillar content:
- **YouTube Shorts:** strongest 60-second moments
- **LinkedIn post:** core pain statement plus insight
- **Substack/Wayfinder piece:** deeper dive on the framework
- **Email:** build checklist answers rewritten as personal note
- **Graphic post:** one-liner version of pain plus invitation

## The "Shown To" Discipline
Track not just what you've published into the void, but who specifically you've put this work in front of. This makes isolation visible and nudges toward the behavior that breaks the wall down: showing your work to specific humans and seeing what happens.

**You don't need investors right now. You need three people who see what you see and want to be part of it.**
