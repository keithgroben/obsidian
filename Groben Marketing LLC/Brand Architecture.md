# Brand Architecture

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/3106abe00ba481079c8fd377dda6e38a (child of Keith Groben — Operating System)*

## The Hierarchy

### Keith Groben / Operational Architect (personal brand)
This is you. The person people connect with through demos and content. The cross-domain systems thinker who builds from lived experience. Everything else ladders up to this.

### R7 Creative / Catalyst Engine (product brand)
This is your RaaS (Results as a Service) offering. The branded product that delivers the content engine and intelligent systems to clients. R7 Creative is the company. Catalyst is the engine inside it.

### The Wayfinder (content brand)
Your blog and publication. A mix of what you're building, what you're learning, and what you believe. Systems, marketing, faith, nostalgia, and the patterns that connect them. Always short. Never fluff. Also published in shortened form on Substack.

### Great Commission Alliance (dormant dream)
A platform where Christians land to collaborate. Requires influence and funding you don't yet have. Shelved until the things you build create the authority to lead it. The demos come before the manifesto. The open letters and manifesto are preserved but not public-facing.

## How They Relate

Keith Groben is the face. People connect with you through demos and Wayfinder content. That connection leads to R7 Creative when they need their business fixed. The GCA activates when the first three have generated enough influence and resources to support it.

**The flow:** Content (Wayfinder) → Connection (demos) → Conversion (R7 Creative) → Calling (GCA)
