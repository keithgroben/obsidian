> Luke 6:19 
> And all the crowd sought to touch him, for power came out from him and healed them all.

- Touch: (MED) haptō 39x pr. to bring in contact, fit, fasten
- Power:  (Olive Tree Enhanced Strong's Dictionary) dynamis; from 1410; force (literally or figuratively); specially, miraculous power (usually by implication, a miracle itself)
- The touching comes after the seeking.
