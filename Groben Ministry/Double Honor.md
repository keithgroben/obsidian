Ephesians 4:7 — But grace was given to each one of us according to the measure of Christ's gift.
Continue through 14.
The point is here: he descended and ascended then gave gifts to men and women.

## Questions

**Does God love?**
Yes — John 3:16.

**Does God love?**
Yes, you see many acts of God's love through Jesus.

**What does the Bible say?**
God is love.
1 John — if you know God you love because God is love. We love because he first loved us.

**Is God power?**
Yes. You have power and can use it. Power in the tongue. Power in your thoughts. As a man thinks in his heart… power at work within you. In fact God does exceedingly abundantly above all that you can ask or think according to the power at work within you.

So you are responsible to love and responsible to exercise power since that is how God does things for you.
You are responsible to cleanse yourself and live holy.
You are responsible to hold oil, anointing. You are responsible to believe and have signs (power) follow you.

You love God, love people. You give what you were freely given. The message of reconciliation and the ministry of reconciliation. That's all of us.
You give the gospel and yourself as a living epistle.
This is the ministry.

**How do you get prepared for that?**
Ephesians 4.
