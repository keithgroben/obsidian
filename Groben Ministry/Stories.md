# Stories

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/3406abe00ba480c4b5d6c7b69defed57*

I remember taking several years as a pastor trying to figure out my unique ministry. I came up with… a b c.
And no matter how I floated that — website, slide, sermon, one on one — it never moved people, moved me, or created an impact.

And since impacts create lasting results and change with people…

So after that I sought the Lord from the pages of Scripture instead of websites and videos of other ministries.

I found out that the word of God tells me what my impact as a pastor is.

That transformation to the way I think went so powerfully deep into my soul, it changed the trajectory of my life.

It took me not only from running my church as a soul transformation factory by the power of the gospel preached, but sent me on assignment, by God, into business.

And in this assignment, I see what my mission is. To get the Body of Christ's businesses and churches marketed well, operating well, and growing — and to do that enough that I have enough money to send 72 evangelists every year with the money equating a full time salary. About $65–$75k per year.

I don't have a special assignment, and I often wonder why God picked me. I didn't ask for this, I was led into this. I was succeeding as a pastor, no problems. Ten years, then the switch.

And the issue I have now is finding these commissioned businesses and churches to spread my message, Jesus' message to. He's the best marketer and salesman. He's the best operator of an organization. And he is the one that will give your business and church an anointing whenever you align it with his mission. Co-missioned.

Because the great commission is the church's mission, there is no other mission. You are the church — it's not where you go or when you're there. You are the church. You, like me, don't have a special assignment. You have a very special assignment — Jesus' assignment.
