# All of my past is behind the closed door

The door is shut.

*Matthew 27:66*
So they went and made the tomb secure by sealing the stone and setting a guard.

- When Christ went to the cross He took all of my sin, died. My sin was still there.
- The people carried His body to the tomb. My sin was taken there.
- The people laid His body in there. My sin was laid there.
- The guards rolled a stone in front of the tomb. My sins were covered.
- They sealed the tomb shut. My sins were sealed in with the dead body of Christ.

The military guards stood in front of the tomb, ensuring no one could touch it, open it, or take the body of Christ out where my sins are placed! The devil could not open it to take my sins out behind the closed, sealed, guarded door! No person could look at them. No one could see them to talk about them. They couldn’t take a picture of it to spread around, they are shut up, sealed, guarded and laid in to the body of the Lord Jesus Christ!

My sins went down to hell with Jesus. They belong there, he didn’t. He went there willingly, taking the responsibility, punishment and conscience of my gross error of negligence and sin. He did not sin, he carried them to their rightful place. He then confronted the liar and accuser of the brethren. He disarmed them. There was no fight! He striped them of their weapons and armor so they cant even bring up my sins from hell! They can’t put them in my face!

They are dead and I am risen with the risen Christ.

When Christ came back up, risen from the dead by God His Father. That resurrection power transformed his body which was not even recognizable as a man... it transformed him into an alive, super-human man! The seal was blown away, the stone was rolled aside, and the guards fell out under the power of God!

Now it is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me!

The door is shut to all of my past. Everything from yesterday on, died with Christ and it is no longer I who live but Christ who lived in me. I rose with Christ. If God is my father and Christ is my elder brother, I am a god, I am a participant of the divine nature!
