**Keith,

[[List of Prophetic Words]]

During our last visit, we were sitting in the living room. You made comment about ezekiel being the book that you are connected to. You also made a brief statement afterwards saying, “but i dont understand it.” 

  

A few days after my return home, I kept hearing that in my Spirit.  Felt the Lord telling me to send this.  It is written in one of my early, original bibles.

  

Dated: August 23, 1982.  During a fast I asked the Lord these questions. 

1.   What do i pray for concerning my ministry?
    
2.  What is the ministry you desire of me?
    
3.  What is the glory of God?
    

  

Ezekiel became the man and book I most connected with.  Didn’t understand why either.  The results of the fast and those questions was the following:

1.  God said, “Ezekiel chapters 1-3 would be my ministry.  Pray to see and experience the glory of God.
    
2.  Ezekiel 3:14, Became outstanding to me.  For some reason, I did not understand until years later.
    
3.  All the things God told me to pray have come to pass since that time in 1982.
    

  

God said, “spend much time with me. Pray for these…”

  

1.  Ez 1:! That the heavens be opened and you see visions of God.
    
2.  1:3 that my word come expressly to you.
    
3.  1:3 that my hand rest strongly upon you.
    
4.  Key! 1:28, 3:12, 3:23 That you may experience and manifest the glory of God.
    
5.  1:28 That you fall on your face before me.
    
6.  2:1 That you may hear and receive my voice clearly.
    
7.  2:2 that my spirit may repeatedly enter into you.
    
8.  2:6 that you be given power over all resistance and works of the enemy.
    
9.  2:8 that you not withold, but openly proclaim the word i give to you.
    
10.  2:10that my word be spread before you, (not always a glorious word, easy, a message of woe)
    
11.  3:12 that my spirit may carry you to the appointed places.

His final exhortations to me:

Remember the glory of God

Preach, let the chips fall where they will 2:57, 3:11, 27

Yet shall know that there hath been a prophet among them. 2:5

Ez 3:14 so that spirit lifted me up and took me away, and i went in bitterness, in the heat of my spirit, but the hand of the lord was strong upon me. 

My final comments

1.  Studying the hebrew definitions of key words in these verses helped me to understand.
    
2.  3:14 is an expression of the degree of Gods burden he carried and strength of Gods hand to help him carry it in boldness and righteousness.**
