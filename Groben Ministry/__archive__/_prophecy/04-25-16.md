[[List of Prophetic Words]]

Dream of Stacie and I in a hotel. She was crabby and everything bothered her. She got drunk, became free and happy and wanted to do everything fun with me. God said: She needs freedom and happiness through the Holy Spirit just like she gets through being drunk

Open door in heaven. Revelation 4:1-2

Works of the flesh, fruit of the spirit. Galatians 5:19-23

-   Disdain: the feeling that someone or something is unworthy of one's consideration or respect; contempt. Works of the flesh close the door in heaven.
    
-   When intimacy is achieved by force it is extortion: the practice of obtaining something, especially money, through force or threats.
    
-   When intimacy is achieved through selfishness it loses its benefit.
    
-   You can lose the leg or you can lose the lock. Your leg is chains because of sin. The lock can come off by repentance and forgiveness, or you can cut off your leg. Vision of horse and carriage, prisoner. Bars fall off of Windows. Prisoner climbs out window, falls out, leg chained, fearful. Drug behind.
    
-   With no rug no one can wipe their feet before entering your house. With a rug the feet may still be dirty while they walk around. But washing their feet allows them to be themselves and wander around the house freely! Forbear one another's sins in love. Col 3:13: Make allowance for each other's faults, and forgive anyone who offends you. Remember, the Lord forgave you, so you must forgive others.
