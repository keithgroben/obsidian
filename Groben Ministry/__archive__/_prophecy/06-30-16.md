[[List of Prophetic Words]]

Ely is an inspirational place. At one time it prospered and it’s population was growing. Today, the community teeters between survival and settlement. I believe our cities destiny is that of prosperity and growth. We face a new dawn where we must realize our true potential. This potential must awaken in us then manifest into life! Fear will turn into bravery, dreams into plans, hopelessness into hope.
  

We will become a city where families come together, children play safely in the streets and neighbors are truly neighbors. Prosperity will no longer be an escaping hope. It will be realized by brave men and women who capture business & industry. It will be established by community values which build people up, Ely will come alive again!
