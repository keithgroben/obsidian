# Ministry Identity

Identity as it has evolved through transformation.

---

## Before Transformation

- **Duration:** 18 years
- **First 8 Years:** Combination of Bible school, internship, and lay ministry
- **Following 10 Years:** Senior pastor at Calvary Assembly in Ely, Nevada.

### Small Church, Big Heart

**Misconnection:** A big love for God, but led a small church. Small in every way: small in number, small in building size, and small in impact.

**Violation:** If you serve the Lord the way the Bible says to your church should not remain small. Even if you did have a small building and a small number of people, you should still have a big impact. Then, the numbers would grow by default.

**Reflection:** Growth was achievable in a small town with a dedicated group of people. The building should have been packed out and created a need to run more services and eventually buy a bigger place.

**Realization:** My vision was small, my perception was small, my knowledge was limited.

### Passionate and Misdirected

- Passionately wrong and misguided on many things
- Passionately right and well-guided on other things
- When everything doesn't line up, your church cannot grow in impact or numbers

---

## Examples

### Worship Services
- Long worship sets that focus on feeling
- "Hosting", soaking in the presence of God
- Effort and striving based worship instead of Biblical praise and worship
- Became weird at times
- We were wrapped up in "worship culture" rather than biblical worship
- We fixed this later by praising and worshiping God biblically

### Children's Ministry
- Purpose was primarily to keep kids engaged
- Goal was so parents could enjoy "big church" service
- The gospel ministers to people regardless of their age
- Jesus said: "Don't forbid the little children to come to me"
- My ignorance resulted in no gospel power for kids
- We got this right later and taught the kids as if they were "the big church", gave them the Holy Spirit and Fire.

## After Transformation

- Since 2024
- Leader of the Great Commission Alliance
- Skilled in business process, marketing, and leadership

### Mission
Bring together **ministers and entrepreneurs** for the purpose of getting souls won, disciples made, God's power demonstrated, church community built; aka Great Commission results.

### Strategy
- Help entrepreneurs and ministers build exponentially
- Create a community
- Complete systems and projects
- Measure and increase Great Commission results quarterly

**How:**
1. Share the Word of God and practical business applications every single week (podcast)
2. Reach the founding 100 alliance members
3. Virtual meetup once a month, for alliance members
4. Project tracking web app
5. Project management

### Current Identity
A leader in the church focused on:
- Results-oriented ministry
- Bible teaching
- Practical business results sharing
- Community building
- Alliance formation
- Great Commission execution

## Lessons Learned

1. Passion without proper biblical direction leads to misdirected effort
2. Trends like "worship culture" replace biblical purpose
3. Small vision produces small results, regardless of heart size
4. Every aspect of ministry must align biblically for growth (Great Commission results)
5. Good intentions don't compensate for misaligned vision and poor execution
6. Focus on results, not just activities
7. Biblical foundation over cultural trends
8. Intentional impact over passive ministry
9. Community building for greater Kingdom work
10. Practical application of God's Word consistently delivered

---

## Transitions

- Small vision → Great Commission vision
- Activity-based → Results based
- Isolated ministry → Alliance formation
- Passive impact → Intentional results
