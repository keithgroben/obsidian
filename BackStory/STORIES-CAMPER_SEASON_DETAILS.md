# CAMPER SEASON DETAILS - Family of 6 Living in Camper (July 2018 - April 2019)

## Purpose of This Document

This document provides the raw, unglamorous details of what it was like for Keith and Stacie Groben to live in a camper with 4 children for approximately 8 months. These details provide credibility and human connection for the "wilderness to breakthrough" narrative.

---

## TIMELINE

**Duration:** ~8 months, 10 days total
- **Start:** July 30, 2018 (arrived Town & Country, Florida)
- **Middle:** Brief house stay for a couple months (allowed school enrollment)
- **End:** April 9, 2019 (moved to Brandon apartment)

---

## THE CHILDREN DURING CAMPER SEASON

### Who Was There

**Four Children Total:**
1. **Kaylie** - Age 16 (born May 16, 2002)
2. **Jordan** - Age 13 (born March 17, 2005)
3. **Jackson** - Age 10 (born January 17, 2008)
4. **Khloe** - Age 6 (born January 19, 2012)

**Keith's Clarification:** "Well, really it was only 2 kids in the camper that did [school]. Both... no wait, there wouldn't have been. So back then it would be 4 kids."

---

## SCHOOL LOGISTICS

### The Homeschool Setup

**Two Different Homeschool Programs:**
- All done on laptops
- Very limited workspace

**Workspace Options:**
- Tiny camper table inside
- Picnic table outside the camper
- Keith's assessment: "It was almost impossible and very miserable"

### The House Miracle (Mid-Camper Season)

**Temporary Relief:**
- "Through a miracle we were able to be in a house for a couple of months in the middle of our camper life"
- Started at Town & Country RV site
- Got to stay in house for a few months
- **Critical benefit:** Allowed them to put down a residence
- **Result:** Allowed them to enroll Khloe in actual school

**Why This Mattered:**
- Gave kids normal school environment temporarily
- Provided more space for homework
- Legal residence for school enrollment
- Break from cramped camper conditions

### Homework Space Challenges

**The Reality:**
- "The homework space was an issue"
- 4 kids trying to do schoolwork in camper
- Two workspace options, both inadequate
- Outside picnic table meant weather-dependent
- Inside table meant zero privacy, constant noise

---

## DAILY ROUTINE

### The Synchronization Requirement

**Keith's Description:** "The daily routine was kind of rough"

**Morning:**
- "Everybody had to get up at the same time"
- No privacy for anyone
- 6 people in tiny camper space
- Getting ready for day required coordination

**Night:**
- "Everybody had to go to bed at the same time"
- No personal space
- No ability for parents to have adult time
- Kids couldn't have different sleep schedules

**Why It Was Rough:**
- No independence for anyone
- Teen daughter (16) had no privacy
- Parents had no privacy
- Every activity affected everyone

---

## FINANCIAL SITUATION

### The Desperation

**Keith's Assessment:** "We were very limited on finances for sure"

**Income Sources (Attempted):**
- Praying and believing for freelance jobs
- Web design work (when it came)
- Additional handyman site gigs
- **Attempted:** Cleaning houses - "it was very difficult"

**The Pattern:**
- "A lot of it is just more of the same stuff"
- "Just trying to figure it out while we're in Florida"
- Constant scrambling for any income
- Nothing stable or predictable

### The Grocery Miracle

**The Crisis:**
- "There was one time that we had no food and no money for food"
- Family of 6 with literally nothing to eat
- No money to buy anything

**The Family Prayer:**
- Gathered together as family
- Asked God to take care of all their needs
- Prayed the day/night before crisis point

**The Answer - Next Day:**
- Person at the RV park came to them
- Brought "a whole bunch of bags full of groceries"
- Said: "Hey, we're leaving our camper lot and we're not going to take these groceries. Could you use them?"
- Keith: "Yeah, yeah"

**What They Received:**
- "Just amazing amount of groceries by the hand of God"
- Enough to meet their immediate need
- Keith's reflection: "It was pretty awesome"

**The Timing:**
- Prayed as family asking God to provide
- Next day: provision arrived
- Through "random" encounter at RV park
- Person leaving didn't want to waste groceries

## THE LIVING CONDITIONS REALITY

### Space Constraints

**Physical Reality:**
- 6 people in camper meant constant close quarters
- No personal space for anyone
- Teenage daughter sharing space with parents and 3 siblings
- Zero privacy for marriage relationship
- Bathroom shared by all 6

### Emotional Toll

**On Parents:**
- No private time together
- Constant noise and activity
- Financial stress with no escape
- Trying to maintain hope while desperate

**On Children:**
- Teenage girl (Kaylie) in most difficult developmental stage for no privacy
- Younger kids adapting to instability
- Homeschool challenges adding to stress
- Seeing parents struggle financially

### The "Miracle House" Relief

**Why the house break was critical:**
- Gave everyone breathing room
- Allowed Khloe to attend real school
- Provided legal residence address
- Temporary return to "normal" life
- Let family decompress from camper stress

**But Then Back to Camper:**
- Had to return to cramped conditions after taste of normalcy
- Made camper feel even smaller
- But kept them going with hope

## WHAT MADE IT SUSTAINABLE

### Faith Foundation

**Daily Prayer and Belief:**
- Praying for freelance jobs
- Believing God for provision
- Family praying together (grocery miracle example)
- Trusting despite no visible path forward

### Work Ethic

**Keith and Stacie's Hustle:**
- Web design freelancing
- Handyman gigs
- Attempted cleaning houses
- Taking anything they could get
- "Just trying to figure it out"

### Family Unity

**The Sacrifice Together:**
- Everyone had to cooperate
- Same schedule for all
- Shared the hardship
- Prayed together
- Celebrated miracles together (groceries)

### Temporary Hope

**The House Break:**
- Proved conditions wouldn't be permanent
- Showed God was working
- Gave kids (especially Khloe) school normalcy
- Provided legal address for moving forward

## THE TRANSITION OUT

### How It Ended

**April 9, 2019:**
- Moved to Brandon apartment
- Keith had started at US Solar (December 2018 interview, started early 2019)
- Stacie joined US Solar as field marketer (April 2019)
- Finally had stable income to afford apartment

**What Made Escape Possible:**
- Keith's US Solar job (even though struggling in sales)
- Stacie joining US Solar for additional income
- Combined income enough for apartment
- Kids could attend real schools
- Family got privacy back
