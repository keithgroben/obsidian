## SPECIFIC CAMPAIGNS AND RESULTS

### Campaign #1: Sales Recruiting through Virtual Meetings

#### Campaign Structure

**Objective:** Recruit new door-to-door sales reps

**Strategy:**
- Broadcast Tuesday sales meetings to multiple offices across Florida
- Create massive content volume to attract recruits
- Let them experience the meeting vibe before applying
- Build awareness through sharable content

**The Sales Meeting Draw:**
- Meetings were "hype, encouraging, even spiritual"
- For a while, "really good until it wasn't"
- Draw was letting prospects sit in on meetings
- Created excitement and belonging before commitment

#### Production Workflow

**Production Day Details:**
- 4 people on production team
- Approximately 12-hour production day
- Captured with 4 cameras simultaneously
- Multi-camera setup for dynamic editing

**Content Creation:**
- Edited long-form content
- Prepared for multiple platforms:
  - YouTube (long-form video)
  - Rumble (long-form video)
  - X/Twitter (long-form video)
  - Spotify (audio only)
- Wrote descriptions for all platforms

**Short-Form Content:**
- Created 3+ pieces of content per day (minimum)
- Additional 6-10 highlight posts:
  - 1-2 highlight reels (video)
  - Photo collages
  - Swipe-through carousels

**Total Content Output from Single Production Day:**
- 1 long-form video (YouTube/Rumble/X)
- 1 audio version (Spotify)
- 3-10 daily short-form pieces
- 6-10 highlight posts/carousels
- **Weekly output:** Enough content for entire week scheduled in advance

**Approval Process:**
- Got approvals next day
- More sensitive Instagram-worthy pieces for speaker required approval
- Most content pre-approved and scheduled

#### Campaign Results - The Silent Funnel

**The Unique Approach:**
- NEVER mentioned "click link in bio" in content
- NEVER asked people to apply in captions
- NEVER text messaged people asking them to apply
- NEVER emailed recruitment appeals
- NEVER ran paid ads

**What Was Required:**
- Had to click link in bio (not prompted)
- Had to fill out application (not mentioned)
- Application was prerequisite for virtual meeting access
- This created a pool to draw recruits from

**Measurable Results:**
- 2-3 applicants per week minimum
- Sometimes more than 3 per week
- Consistent recruiting pipeline
- Self-motivated, self-selected candidates (higher quality)

**Technology Used:**
- Go High Level (funnels)
- Vista Social (content distribution)
- Adobe products (all content creation)
- DaVinci Resolve (editing)

**Why It Worked:**
- Content showcased culture and energy
- People WANTED to be part of it
- No hard sell required
- Self-selection filtered for motivated candidates

### Campaign #2: Marketing Internship Recruiting

#### Campaign Structure

**Objective:** Recruit interns for marketing department

**Philosophy:**
- Provide excellent place for people to intern
- Help interns become excellent at marketing
- Build pipeline for future full-time hires
- Give back through mentorship

**Brand Development:**
- Created own brand for internship program
- Own funnels separate from main company
- Own meeting automations
- Own application process
- Distinct visual identity

#### Content Strategy

**Content Volume:**
- 2 pieces of content per day initially
- Reduced to ~10 pieces of content per week
- More focused and targeted than sales recruiting
- Quality over quantity approach

**Key Difference from Sales Recruiting:**
- More direct in content about applying
- Clear call-to-action in posts
- "Of course you're going to get more people to take action if you're asking for the action"

**Additional Strategy:**
- Collaborations with other accounts
- Cross-promotion opportunities
- Leveraged existing audience

#### Results

**Timeline:** First 2 months

**Measurable Outcomes:**
- 12 applicants total
- 4 interns accepted offers
- 3 interns actually started (75% show rate)
- **Conversion Rate:** 25% from applicant to intern (3 of 12)

**Keith's Assessment:** "Very, very successful"

**Why It Worked:**
- Clear value proposition
- Direct call-to-action
- Consistent posting schedule
- Collaboration amplification
- Professional brand presentation

**Technology Used:**
- Go High Level (funnels, automations, applications)
- Vista Social (content scheduling)
- Adobe products (content creation)
- Link-in-bio funnels
