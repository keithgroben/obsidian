# BREAKTHROUGH STORIES - Keith Groben's "One Win After Multiple Losses" Moments

## Purpose

This document catalogs Keith's most shareable breakthrough stories—specific moments where persistence through multiple failures led to life-changing wins. These are the MOST SHAREABLE stories for social media, podcasts, and speaking. Each story follows the pattern: Setup → Crisis → Breakthrough → Lesson.

## STORY #1: The 35-36 Prospecting Calls (Fall 2018)

### Setup
- Church handyman was moving to a different state
- Gave Keith his complete client list as a gift
- List contained 35-36 names with phone numbers
- Keith was struggling in Tampa, living in camper, desperate for work

### Crisis
- Called every single person on the list
- Result: **NOBODY picked up the phone**
- Left 35-36 voicemails
- "Super discouraging"
- Looking at last 5 numbers on list - all out-of-state area codes
- Thought: "They probably don't even live here"
- Almost didn't call them
- Seemed pointless after 30+ failures

### Breakthrough
- Called out-of-state number anyway
- Left voicemail (like all the others)
- Person called back 2-3 days later
- Far out of town handyman job
- **Critical conversation:** Customer mentioned friend who did solar sales
- This connection led to US Solar opportunity
- **The call Keith almost didn't make changed everything**

### Lesson
The breakthrough is often in the last call, the last application, the last attempt. Most people quit at call 30 of 35. Success comes to those who make calls 31-35.

### Shareable Hook
"I made 35 phone calls. Zero pickups. I almost didn't make the 36th call—out-of-state area code, seemed pointless. That call led to the job that led to helping scale a company from $3M to $60M."

## STORY #3: The $1,300 Walk-In Sale Prayer (First Month of Sales, 2019)

### Setup: The Impossible Transition
- Keith joined direct sales team **after pastoring a church for 10 years**
- "Let that gravity just settle in"
- Prayed: "I so badly wanted the Holy Spirit to teach me to sell"
- Company was very young, very new, very disorganized
- **Provided absolutely NO training**
- **100% expectation you would perform well**
- If you weren't performing: "Something was wrong with you"
- Had been failing at getting 10 sales to become a closer
- Was working as field marketer - got maybe 4-5 sales total, couldn't complete it in reasonable time
- Company attitude: "We're going to hand you some paperwork and an iPad. You're going to figure it out."
- iPad = presentation tool, Paperwork = contracts
- Failing so much, not making any money
- Family of 6 depending on him after "recklessly abandoning" Nevada to move to Florida
- "Asked God to teach me to prosper and prosperity happens through time and process with skill. No other way."

### Crisis: The TrueGreen Escape Route
- **Thursday:** Applied to many jobs, interviewed with TrueGreen (lawn care sales)
- TrueGreen offered: Hourly base pay PLUS commission (massive upgrade from pure commission)
- Already had **second interview that Thursday**
- Was supposed to **start Monday** - orientation, provide driver's license, payroll setup
- They provided **4-week training** (which would be "amazing")
- Keith didn't have to go out Friday or Saturday at all
- Could have quit US Solar right then, started getting paid hourly rate immediately
- Relief: He already had the job secured

### The Porch Prayer: The Shift
- Thursday after interview, Keith talked with Stacie on their apartment porch
- Keith: "I'm just going to be faithful. I don't know to what, I'm gonna be honest. I think I'm just gonna be faithful to complete my job."
- "I'm going to go out and I'm going to not care about the outcome. I'm just gonna ask the Lord to help me."
- "I don't know what else to do because I've been praying and I've been trying and I've been failing. That was the pattern."
- **Two things changed:**
  1. Relaxed and didn't care (because backup job was secured)
  2. Peace after asking God for help that weekend
- Daily practice: Always thanking God for a sale - "Thank you God for my sale" - desperate, every day

### The Prayer That Changed Everything
- **Most important shift:** "God, if there's something I need to work out here, if there is something that you need me to work out here... if there is a lesson that I'm going to learn that is going to be helpful for me to accomplish the goal that I have asked you for, which is prosperity, then I will stay here."
- Changed from begging for success to asking: **"If there's something you need me to work out here"**
- Recognized: "I am working in faith with God. I am cooperating in faith working a job."
- "God always wants to show me something, teach me something through His word, reveal something to me"

### The Weekend
- **Friday:** Started working neighborhood - absolutely nothing happened
- But very relaxed, just had a good time walking around talking to people, not caring
- **Saturday morning (10:30 or 11 AM):** Knocked on door
- Young husband answered
- Keith gave pitch
- Husband: "Yeah, that sounds good. Do you want to come back in an hour?"
- Keith: "No problem." Asked: "Will any other homeowners be there?"
- Husband: "Oh yeah, my wife, she's the only other one."

### Breakthrough: The First Solo Close
- Came back one hour later
- Asked: "Do you mind if I come in?"
- They said yes
- Keith's thought: "Dang. This is crazy. I'm walking in."
- **The process:**
  - Took required pictures
  - Sat down, opened iPad
  - "Look, I'm just going to go through this screen by screen to make sure I don't miss anything. If that's all right with you. And then if you have any questions at the end, let's talk about those things."
  - They agreed: "Yeah, no problem."
  - Went through entire presentation screen by screen
  - Reviewed their electric bill
  - Showed cost and savings
  - **Asked for the sale (as trained):** "So if this all makes sense to you, the only thing I need is your driver's license in order to process the application."
- **They looked at each other:**
  - "Hey, you want to do it?"
  - "I don't care if you want to do it."
  - "Yeah, all right."
- Keith: "Do you need both of our driver's licenses?"
- "Yes, please."
- **Both gave licenses**
- Keith filled everything out, sent to financing
- **Approved**
- Filled out paperwork
- Called manager to complete final part
- **Why Keith didn't have all documents:** Manager told him they don't give new reps final loan documents "simply because they're not sure it's going to work out. They don't want to make the investment."

### The Result
- Sale paid **$1,300 commission total**
- Keith getting **$700 on next check (Friday)** - "outstanding"
- Didn't go to Monday TrueGreen orientation
- Stayed at US Solar
- **The confirmation:** Because his prayer was "if there's something I need to work out," this sale told him there WAS more to work out in this role
- "I worked out such a valuable education about how to not be withheld by barriers and to just keep moving forward and doing the work"
- "God was with me and helped me. I was battling panic and anxiety."
- **Perfect combination:** Peace when he prayed, really meaning what he prayed, already had backup job

### Biblical Foundation
- "If you believe that you will receive when you ask... then you will have the things that you're praying for"
- "Pretty simple for me to believe, receive and ask because I already had a job on the way"

### The Reality Check
- "I would love to say that everything worked out absolutely perfectly well afterwards"
- **Struggled for three weeks until next sale** (which became Story #11 - the 83-door sale)
- But manager Brandon was "really surprised that I did it"

### Lesson
Sometimes breakthrough comes when you stop desperately needing it and start asking, "What do you need me to work out here?" Peace in prayer + secured backup plan + shift from begging to learning = first solo close. The sale answered the prayer: there WAS something to work out.

### Shareable Hook
"Thursday: Got hired at TrueGreen. Starting Monday with training and hourly pay. Friday: Went door-to-door at US Solar anyway. Saturday: Closed my first solo sale—$1,300. Never showed up to Monday orientation. God answered: there was more to work out."

## STORY #4: The Rent Miracle Prayer - $850 Upfront (2006-2008 Freelance Era)

### Setup: Everything Was Going Great
- **Context:** Months of freelancing effort had been paying off
- Paid all bills
- Bought clothes
- Went to movies, went out to eat
- Paid tithes
- "We were just a happy growing family"
- "Everything was going well, we had plenty of money, which sometimes it's very rare for people"
- Keith had been posting 10+ Craigslist ads daily in different cities to stay visible
- Craigslist was time-based feed (not algorithm) - "You got to stay there, so you got to post all the time, you gotta hustle"

### Crisis: The $850 Shortfall
- **A few days before rent was due**
- Stacie: "Keith, rent's due. Do you have that $850?"
- Keith called bank for balance (no app back then)
- **Had maybe $180** - everything else was literally already spent/paid
- Stacie needed to know if they could pay rent

### The Impossible Prayer
- Keith went into bedroom to pray
- **The specific prayer:** "God, I need you to help me to find a job that's going to pay the rent. And I'm going to be able to get all of it upfront in my hand and not have to wait for 50% down and 50% later."
- **Why this was impossible:** "The whole culture of freelancing in the Phoenix area was half now, half on completion"
- Standard practice: Half upfront (skin in the game), half on completion (quality assurance)
- "It was a pretty good incentive system"
- But Keith needed the full amount immediately

### The Setup: Adding His Brother
- Keith had decided to help his older brother make money
- Also helped Keith as freelancer offer additional value
- Brother was "a very good artist" - custom illustrations
- This partnership would become the key

### Breakthrough: The Old Post
- After praying, someone contacted Keith
- **Critical detail:** Either very old post or "I'm pretty confident it was from an old post"
- "That would only have resurfaced if that person were searching me out"
- **They had to search specifically** - not just scrolling
- "Because Craigslist was a feed and everything would go down, that's why you had to post constantly"
- This inquiry came from someone actively searching for Keith's specific services

### The Client: Downtown Phoenix Real Estate
- **Who:** Woman from real estate firm in downtown Phoenix
- **What they did:** Buying up old rundown properties in particular district, revitalizing them
- **The need:** Custom illustration (one of last things needed to present to investors)
- "Super urgent and high priority for this person"
- She called Keith - "really nice and really slick"
- "Hey, is there any way that you can come tomorrow?"

### The Meeting
- Keith went to her office next day
- Met for a few minutes
- Talked about what he does
- She had already seen his proposal
- Keith told her about his brother, showed the artwork
- Her response: "Yeah, he's very capable."
- **Her question:** "How much do you think it would cost?"
- Keith's honesty: "I honestly had no idea. I'm gonna be honest. I didn't know how much it would cost."

### The Quote
- Keith: "Well, it's gonna probably be pretty expensive. It's gonna be **$1,200**."
- Her: "$1,200?"
- Keith: "Yeah. I mean you want it done fast too, right?"
- Her: "Okay."
- **Her question:** "What's it take for you to get started?"
- Keith: "**$850**" (THE EXACT RENT AMOUNT)
- Keith explained: "That's more than half, but you want us to rush it."
- Her: "Okay."
- **She wrote him a check on the spot**

### The Victory
- Keith came back, deposited the check
- **It cleared**
- Paid the rent
- "It was incredible, man. I don't know any other way to put it."

### The Feeling
- **Keith's reflection:** "I remember feeling so secure"
- Secure "just being a freelancer, just being an entrepreneur"
- Secure with his wife: "We would be able to accomplish anything"
- Secure with his relationship with God
- "That's a great story"

### The Difficult Client Aftermath
- Reality: "She turned out to be a pretty picky client"
- "Ended up wanting to nickel and dime me"
- At the end: "She sort of wanted to not pay me the rest of the money"
- **Keith's response:** "Well, if you don't want to pay us the rest of the money, then you're definitely not going to get what we created."
- She paid the rest
- Keith's assessment: "Very interesting lady. By far a Phoenix realtor... very interesting."
- "I ended up getting paid and moving on"

### Lesson
When you pray a specific, "impossible" prayer (all money upfront in a half-now culture), God can answer with mathematical precision ($850 needed, $850 quoted, $850 received). The breakthrough required: 1) Past relentless posting that created searchable history, 2) Partnership with brother for added value, 3) Specific prayer, 4) Willingness to stand firm on payment terms even with difficult client.

### Shareable Hook
"Rent: $850. Bank account: $180. I prayed: 'God, I need all the money upfront' (impossible in freelancing—everyone does 50/50). Client called from old Craigslist post. Quote: $1,200. 'What to get started?' I said: '$850.' She wrote the check. Exact rent amount."

- **Resolution:** "She finally decided to listen to me and they listened and they loved it"

Sometimes the breakthrough comes when you're completely out of your mind with desperation. The sale that ends your losing streak often requires you to walk around crying, speaking faith declarations you barely believe, and showing up at doors when every fiber tells you it's pointless. That fourth door after 83 rejections changes everything.

### Shareable Hook
"I knocked 83 doors. Nothing. I was crying, walking in circles saying 'God, thank you for my sale' over and over. Door 84 was a Spanish-speaking family. The presentation was chaos. The salesman almost walked out three times. They signed. That sale ended my 3-week losing streak."

## THE SPIRITUAL PATTERN: Asking God for Measurable Outcomes

### The Origin of the Pattern

This pattern in Keith's life—where he asks the Holy Spirit for specific outcomes in business that have measurable results—was **developed in crisis**. Not in comfort, but in panic. Not in peace, but in desperation.

### The Biblical Foundation

**3 John 1:2:** "Beloved, I pray above all else that you may prosper and be in health even as your soul prospers."

Keith recognized: **In order to have prosperity on the outside, he needed it on the inside first.**

### The Pressure Cooker

He learned this principle in the pressure cooker of absolute crazy change:
- **18 years in ministry total**
- **10 years as full-time pastor**
- Did freelance graphic design and had 3Fold Media with a business partner
- But **nothing prepared him** to have wealth move through his hands the way it did after he sought God for it

The transition from ministry to marketplace happened through:
- Extreme lack
- Panicking
- Crippling anxiety
- Zero perceived marketplace skills
- "Very difficult, very challenging"

### The Pattern Emerges

**The Process:**
1. Ask God for a nearly impossible outcome
2. Because Keith didn't know how to get the outcome, he had to do impossible things
3. "I wish that I could say I was always peaceful doing it"
4. Reality: "Most all of these stories and situations were sheer panic, desperation"
5. "Blowing up, turning my life completely upside down"
6. Doing things that were "stressful and out of order and out of character and out of place"

**Why it was necessary:**
- Had to improve his character
- Had to up his skill level
- Had to increase: diligence, keenness, aptitude, discernment, intelligence
- Operating through: word of wisdom, word of knowledge, spirit of prophecy

### The Impossibility of It All

## STORYTELLING APPLICATIONS

### For Social Media (LinkedIn, Instagram, Twitter/X)

**Format:** Setup (1 sentence) → Crisis (2-3 sentences) → Breakthrough (2 sentences) → Lesson (1 sentence)

**Example Post:**
> Saturday morning I had 8 appointments booked.
>
> By afternoon, 7 had fallen through. I said to my coworker, "I don't even know what faith is anymore. I don't know how to stay positive when my kids are depending on me."
>
> But I showed up for #8 anyway. Good attitude despite everything.
>
> That's the one I closed. $500.
>
> **The lesson:** Most people quit after failure #7. Show up for #8.

### For Podcasts

**Opening hook:** Start with the specific numbers and the crisis
**Middle:** Build the tension - what was at stake, the emotional state
**Resolution:** The breakthrough and what happened next
**Connection:** How this small win led to the larger outcome

**Example:** "I made 35 phone calls that day. Zero pickups. I left 35 voicemails. Looking at the last 5 numbers, I almost didn't call them—they were all out-of-state area codes. Seemed pointless. But I called anyway..."

### For Speaking/Presentations

**Visual element:** Show the progression
- Slide 1: 35 calls, 0 answers
- Slide 2: Call #36 - out-of-state, almost didn't make it
- Slide 3: The connection → US Solar → $60M

**Audience participation:** "Raise your hand if you've ever quit at step 30 of 35..."
