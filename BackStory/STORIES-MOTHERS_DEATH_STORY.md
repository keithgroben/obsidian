# MOTHER'S DEATH STORY - Barbara J. Groben (July 2017)

## Purpose of This Document

This document contains the full story of Keith's mother's death in July 2017, including the circumstances, Keith's role in handling everything, and the prolonged mourning process. This tragic event did NOT influence Keith's decision to leave the pastorate (those were separate issues), but it profoundly impacted him personally and added significant weight to an already difficult season.

---

## TIMELINE

**Death:** Less than a week before July 18, 2017
**Funeral:** July 18, 2017
**Suppressed Mourning:** 6 months initially
**Full Mourning Process:** Extended to 2022 (5 years)
**Final Resolution:** By 2022, Keith no longer thought of the situation and cried

---

## THE CIRCUMSTANCES

### The Location: Nevada's Great Basin Desert

**Context Keith Provided:**
- "In Nevada, whenever you're driving anywhere far away, you're on these 2-lane highways"
- "Every single commercial that you see of a car commercial and they're in the desert and it looks like weird is most likely the place where I lived: the Great Basin Desert"
- Car commercials are often shot there
- Open road races still happen there
- Movie "Rat Race" was filmed there (Silver Springs scene was actually Ely, Nevada)

**The Remoteness:**
- "That place was so remote"
- "You would have to drive hours to get anywhere, like 3 or 4 hours to get to a nearest big city"
- This remoteness is critical to understanding the accident circumstances

### The Highway Construction Pattern

**Common Occurrence in Remote Nevada:**
- 2-lane highways only
- When construction happens, one lane shuts down
- Traffic stopped 15-20 minutes on one side
- Then other side stops 15-20 minutes for their turn
- "It was not uncommon"
- Completely normal situation

### The Day of the Accident

**The Trip:**
- Keith's parents were heading to Vegas
- Going to visit Keith's younger brother
- Hit construction stop on 2-lane highway
- Stopped along with everyone else

**The Position:**
- Mother (Barbara) standing behind the Jeep Grand Cherokee
- "Just stretching her legs or whatever"
- Father slightly off to the side of the Jeep
- Car parked directly behind their Jeep

### The Impact

**The Negligent Driver:**
- Some guy "was not paying attention"
- Keith: "Which was very hard to believe"
- "It's straight, there's no turns"
- "You would have to be like looking at your phone to not pay attention"
- Did not see that everybody was stopped

**The Collision:**
- Driver traveling at highway speeds: "70 miles an hour plus"
- Hit the car parked directly behind Keith's parents' Jeep
- Impact launched that car into the air
- That airborne car crushed Keith's mother
- Same impact hit Keith's dad, causing him to fly 20 feet from where he was

**The Injuries:**
- Mother: "Literally was crushed by the pelvis down"
- Father: Thrown 20 feet, physically devastated but survived
- Keith's assessment: "That was absolutely insane and heartbreak"

## KEITH'S MULTIPLE ROLES

### The Impossible Position

**Keith Was:**
1. The son who just lost his mother
2. The pastor of the church his mother attended
3. The funeral coordinator for the family
4. The estate manager (father couldn't function)
5. The emotional support for his congregation
6. The emotional support for his family
7. The strong one everyone leaned on

### What Keith Had to Handle

**Funeral Arrangements:**
- Coordinating all details
- Making decisions under grief
- Supporting father who was destroyed

**Cremation:**
- Handling all cremation arrangements
- Decisions his father couldn't make

**The Estate:**
- Managing mother's estate
- Father "was so destroyed he couldn't do anything"
- "I had to shoulder the entire load"

**The Congregation:**
- His mother attended his church
- Had to deal with congregation's grief
- Had to continue pastoring through own loss
- "I had to deal with my congregation"

**The Family:**
- Comfort his family
- Be strong for everyone
- Hold everyone together while falling apart inside

### The Impossible Demand

**Keith's Position:**
- "I had to shoulder the entire load"
- "Comfort my family and be strong"
- Multiple roles, zero space to grieve
- Everyone depending on him
- No one he could lean on

---

## THE SUPPRESSED MOURNING

### Initial Suppression: 6 Months

**What Keith Did:**
- "I suppressed mourning for 6 months"
- Functioned through roles and responsibilities
- Kept moving, kept serving, kept being strong
- Didn't allow himself to fully feel the loss

**Why This Happened:**
- Too many people depending on him
- Too many roles to fulfill
- Pastor can't fall apart when congregation needs him
- Son can't fall apart when father is destroyed
- Brother can't fall apart when family needs strength

### Extended Mourning: Until 2022

**The Reality:**
- Initial 6 months suppression
- "That mourning stretched all the way out till like 2022"
- **5 years of processing this loss**
- "It finally became that I no longer thought of the situation and would just cry"
- Tears would come unbidden for 5 years

**The Weight:**
- Random moments would trigger tears
- Couldn't fully resolve grief while shouldering everything
- Processing happened in slow motion
- 2022 before he could think of it without crying

---

## THE RELATIONSHIP IMPACT

### What Keith Lost

**His Mother's Role:**
- "My mom really was like the champion of my life"
- "Really was like the cheerleader"
- "She had such a great personality"
- "She brought bubbly life to everything"
- "Just was so full of love"
- "Wow" - Keith's word for her impact

### The Family Disconnection

**Father's State:**
- "My dad was naturally very distraught"
- Completely destroyed by loss
- Couldn't handle any responsibilities
- Keith had to do everything

**Geographic Separation:**
- "Being disconnected from my family entirely for that period of time"
- Keith eventually moved to different state (Tampa, then Ohio)
- "Now I live in a different state"

**The New Reality:**
- "Connection with my dad is what it's going to be when we live in different states"
- "Those family ties sort of ended"
- Physical distance + emotional devastation = severed connection
- "But I have a lot of great memories of my mother. She was wonderful."

## DID NOT INFLUENCE PASTORAL DECISION

### Keith's Clear Statement

**The Separation of Issues:**
- "It did not actually influence my decision to move or leave the pastorate"
- "Those were two separate issues"
- Decision to resign was about God's call to prosperity (documented in BREAKTHROUGH_STORIES.md Story #10)
- Decision was already in process before mother's death

**The Perception vs Reality:**
- "It may look like from the outside that it did"
- Timeline proximity makes people assume connection
- July 2017: Mother died
- 2018: Resigned and left for Tampa
- But decisions were unrelated

**What It DID Do:**
- "It certainly complicated and made way more difficult everything else"
- Added weight to already difficult transition
- Made leaving even harder (losing mother AND leaving pastoral role)
- Intensified isolation and grief

## THE LONG-TERM IMPACT

### Unresolved Until 2022

**The 5-Year Process:**
- Couldn't fully grieve while pastoring
- Couldn't fully grieve while managing estate
- Couldn't fully grieve while father was destroyed
- Couldn't fully grieve while moving to Tampa
- Couldn't fully grieve while in camper
- Couldn't fully grieve while battling panic disorder

**The Layers:**
- 2017: Death + immediate aftermath
- 2018: Resignation + move to Tampa + wilderness begins
- 2018-2021: Panic disorder + camper + sales job + breakthrough
- 2020-2022: Marketing Director success while still processing
- 2022: Finally able to think of her without crying

### What 2022 Brought

**The Resolution:**
- "It finally became that I no longer thought of the situation and would just cry"
- Could remember her without tears
- Could think of the accident without breakdown
- Mourning process complete
- Good memories without devastation
