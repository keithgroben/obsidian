# STACIE'S PERSPECTIVE - The Partnership Story

## Purpose

This document is a framework to capture Stacie's voice and experience throughout the journey. Every interview will ask about her. Women in the audience need her perspective. The partnership story is incomplete without it. This document outlines what needs to be captured and why it matters.

**Critical Note:** Most of this document contains questions and frameworks for gathering Stacie's perspective. The actual content needs to come from Stacie herself through interviews and conversations.

---

## WHY STACIE'S STORY MATTERS

### For the Complete Narrative

Keith's success isn't solo—it's a partnership story. Every major decision, every sacrifice, every breakthrough involved both of them. Without Stacie's voice:
- The story sounds like "I did this" instead of "WE did this"
- The sacrifices aren't fully understood
- The partnership that made it possible is invisible
- The audience only gets half the picture

### For Specific Audiences

**Women in Audience:**
- Will relate to Stacie's experience
- Want to hear from the woman behind the story
- Need to see her as a full person, not just "supportive wife"
- Will ask: "What did SHE sacrifice? What was HER experience?"

**Married Couples:**
- Want to understand how the partnership works
- Need to see both perspectives on major decisions
- Will ask: "How do you decide together? How do you handle disagreement?"

**Families:**
- Want to know how children experienced the journey
- Need to understand the family dynamic
- Will ask: "Was it worth it for the kids?"

### For Interview Preparation

Every podcast, media interview, and speaking engagement will include these questions:
- "How did your wife feel about resigning with no backup plan?"
- "What was it like for her in the camper with 4 kids?"
- "How did your marriage survive Zone 5?"
- "What does she say now about the journey?"

Without prepared answers from Stacie's actual perspective, Keith will speak FOR her instead of letting her voice be heard.

## THE UNSUNG HERO NARRATIVE

### Why "Unsung Hero" Works

Every great story has a supporting hero whose contribution makes the protagonist's journey possible:
- Sam to Frodo
- Alfred to Batman
- Pepper Potts to Tony Stark

Stacie is that character in Keith's story—but unlike fictional supporting heroes, she needs to be heard in her own voice.

### The Reframe

**Old narrative:** "Keith did these things. Stacie supported him."

**New narrative:** "Keith and Stacie did these things together. Here's Keith's perspective. Here's Stacie's perspective."

### What This Achieves

1. **Full truth:** The story is incomplete without her voice
2. **Respect:** She's a full participant, not just background
3. **Relatability:** Women in audience connect with her
4. **Credibility:** Partnership shown, not just claimed
5. **Differentiation:** Most speakers only tell their own story
