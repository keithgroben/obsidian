# THEOLOGICAL DISTINCTIVES - Faith-Based Entrepreneur Positioning

## Purpose of This Document

This document articulates Keith Groben's theological and spiritual distinctives that set him apart in the faith-based entrepreneur space. These positions are not just theoretical—they're forged from 10 years of pastoral ministry, two cult-like environments, wilderness testing, and marketplace breakthrough. This positioning appeals specifically to faith-based entrepreneurs who are tired of hype, prosperity abuse, and compartmentalized Christianity.

---

## THE 7 CORE DISTINCTIVES

### 1. Results-Based Faith vs. Performance-Based Acceptance

#### The Distinction

**Performance-Based Acceptance (What Keith Rejects):**
- Your spiritual standing depends on how well you perform
- God's favor is earned through religious activity
- Acceptance is conditional on behavior
- Creates fear, shame, and religious striving

**Results-Based Faith (What Keith Embraces):**
- You're fully accepted in Christ (Ephesians 1:6)
- Faith produces tangible results in real world
- Belief without corresponding action is dead (James 2:22)
- Not earning acceptance—demonstrating faith through outcomes

#### Biblical Foundation

**James 2:22 (Keith's Core Verse):**
- "You see that faith was working with his works, and as a result of the works, faith was perfected"
- Faith and works cooperate together
- Works don't earn salvation—they prove faith is alive
- Results validate genuine belief

**The Integration:**
- Fully accepted by grace through faith (Ephesians 2:8-9)
- That faith naturally produces measurable outcomes (James 2:17)
- Ask God for specific, tangible results
- Expect breakthrough because God is real and active

#### How This Plays Out

**In Ministry:**
- Prayed for specific sales numbers and saw God provide
- Asked for rent miracle, received exact amount needed
- Family prayed for groceries, received bags of food next day
- Faith produces evidence

**In Business:**
- Not "hoping" for success—believing for specific outcomes
- Praying with precision, expecting measurable answers
- $20 library sacrifice → first client within week
- 83 doors + desperate prayer → immediate sale

**The Appeal to Faith-Based Entrepreneurs:**
- You're not trying to earn God's love through business success
- You ARE expecting your faith to produce real-world results
- Removes religious guilt while maintaining belief in supernatural provision
- Biblical, practical, and proven through Keith's story

### 2. Prosperity Theology Done Correctly

#### The Problem with Prosperity Gospel

**What Keith Rejects:**
- God is a cosmic vending machine (give money, get blessing)
- Wealth = automatic sign of God's favor
- Poverty = sign of lack of faith
- Manipulative seed-faith giving schemes
- Health and wealth guaranteed if you have enough faith

**The Abuse Keith Witnessed:**
- Toxic environments using prosperity teaching for control
- Leaders living lavishly while team couldn't pay rent
- Spiritual manipulation tied to financial giving
- False promises and hype without substance

#### What Keith Embraces Instead

**Biblical Prosperity Correctly Understood:**

**Ezekiel 37 Pattern (Dry Bones):**
- Wilderness season is real and God-ordained
- "Can these bones live?" - situation looks impossible
- God speaks life into dead situations
- Restoration comes after testing
- From death to army—complete transformation

**3 John 1:2:**
- "Beloved, I pray that in all respects you may prosper and be in good health, just as your soul prospers"
- Prosperity is holistic: spiritual, physical, financial
- Soul prosperity comes first (character, integrity, faith)
- External prosperity follows internal transformation
- Not get-rich-quick—get-whole-correctly

**Wilderness → Breakthrough Pattern:**
- Testing is part of the process (Keith's Tampa season)
- Character developed in pressure (camper, panic disorder, financial desperation)
- Promotion after proving (wilderness → Marketing Director → Fractional CMO)
- Breakthrough is real but comes after refinement

#### The Leadership Gap Keith Fills

**Keith's Positioning:**
- "There is a toxic prosperity gospel and teaching. But there actually is a real, biblical prosperity for everyone."
- Not denying prosperity—correcting HOW it's achieved
- What people hate about toxic prosperity gospel is the METHOD, not the concept
- Prosperity IS in the gospel—it can't be denied

**What Biblical Prosperity Actually Includes:**
- Your sins being forgiven (spiritual prosperity)
- Your body being healed (physical prosperity)
- Demons being cast out (spiritual authority)
- Relationships being restored (relational prosperity)
- Protection from enemies (divine covering)
- Safety from harm (supernatural security)
- Your provision—God never wants you to lack (financial prosperity)

**Rejecting the Devil's Doctrine of Lack:**
- "There's a sort of devil's doctrine in the church that says it is God's will for you to lack"
- Keith operated under this lie for many years
- "You're not going to remain poor to stay spiritual"
- Being poor means you don't understand what was spiritually given to you
- Poverty is not piety

**The Inside-Out Principle:**
- "You have to grow that wealth on the inside before you see it on the outside"
- Internal transformation precedes external manifestation
- Soul prosperity (character, integrity, faith) comes first
- Then God can trust you with external prosperity
- Not denying prosperity—preparing for it correctly

**Keith's Offer to Faith-Based Entrepreneurs:**
- "I'm offering faith that produces results that are measurable"
- Teaching of faith that produces measurable results
- Testing precedes promotion (proving your faith)
- Doing things excellent as worship to God
- Business that funds the mission
- Creating community for faith entrepreneurs navigating calling
- Providing tools to achieve beyond your own abilities
- Transparency and authenticity, not hype

**The Community Vision:**
- Message creates community that faith entrepreneurs can rally behind
- Navigating calling and working out the details together
- Genuine transparency and authenticity
- Not building empire—building family of marketplace ministers

#### How This Plays Out

**Keith's Story Proves It:**
- Camper season: 8 months of testing (Ezekiel 37 dry bones)
- God provided: groceries, $20 miracle, rent miracle
- Not instant wealth—slow refinement
- Breakthrough came: $3M → $60M company growth
- Now teaching others the pattern

**The Appeal to Faith-Based Entrepreneurs:**
- You can believe in God's provision without manipulation
- Wilderness is normal, not failure
- Prosperity is biblical when done with integrity
- Character must match calling
- Real breakthrough comes after real testing

### 7. Stewardship Over Empire Building

#### The Distinction

**Empire Building (What Keith Rejects):**
- Wealth and success for personal validation
- Needing bigger numbers to feel significant
- Using Kingdom language for selfish ambition
- Measuring worth by revenue and influence
- Building platform for ego

**Stewardship (What Keith Embraces):**
- Wealth and success fund mission and family
- Prosperity enables greater Kingdom impact
- Success is tool, not identity
- Measuring faithfulness, not just metrics
- Building influence to serve others

#### Biblical Foundation

**Matthew 25:14-30 (Parable of Talents):**
- Faithful stewards multiply what they're given
- Responsibility increases with faithfulness
- God expects ROI on what He entrusts
- Stewardship = managing God's resources for His purposes
- Burying talent is condemned, not celebrated

**Luke 16:10-11:**
- "He who is faithful in a very little thing is faithful also in much"
- Prove faithful with little before receiving much
- Stewardship in small tested before large entrusted
- Keith's pattern: faithful in camper → entrusted with Marketing Director → now Fractional CMO

**1 Timothy 6:17-19:**
- "Instruct those who are rich in this present world not to be conceited or to fix their hope on the uncertainty of riches, but on God"
- "To do good, to be rich in good works, to be generous and ready to share"
- Wealth is for generosity and Kingdom impact
- Hope in God, not in portfolio

#### How This Plays Out

**Keith's Story Demonstrates It:**

**Camper Season Testing:**
- Learned to trust God when having nothing
- Character refined before prosperity
- Dependence on God established
- Proved faithful when desperate

**Marketing Director Stewardship:**
- $1,500/month budget → $60M results
- Faithful with small resources
- Maximized what was entrusted
- Built team, developed interns, served with excellence
- Did not build empire—served company mission

**Leaving $60M Success:**
- Money changed people at Zone 5
- Keith left despite success because values mattered more
- Didn't stay for money and platform
- Walked away to maintain integrity
- Stewardship means knowing when to leave, not just when to stay

**Current Fractional CMO Model:**
- Serves multiple clients (not building personal empire)
- Helps others succeed (not just self-promotion)
- Teaches from experience (giving back)
- Using marketing skills to fund family and serve Kingdom businesses
- Steward of expertise, not hoarder of opportunity

#### The Philosophy

**Wealth Funds Mission:**
- Business success should enable:
  - Family provision and stability
  - Generosity and giving
  - Kingdom business funding
  - Helping others succeed
  - Creating jobs and opportunities
- Not: bigger house, fancier car, ego boost

**Influence Serves Others:**
- Platform is for:
  - Teaching what God taught you
  - Helping others avoid your mistakes
  - Sharing proven strategies
  - Pointing people to Jesus
  - Stewarding testimony for Kingdom impact
- Not: personal validation, fame, empire

**The Appeal to Faith-Based Entrepreneurs:**
- You want success for purpose, not ego
- You're building business to fund mission and family
- You're tired of Christian celebrity culture
- You want to steward influence with integrity
- You believe wealth should multiply Kingdom impact
- You're faithful with little, ready for more responsibility
