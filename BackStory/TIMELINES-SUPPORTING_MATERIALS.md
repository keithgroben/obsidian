### **2a. The "Last Five Calls" Pattern - Never Quit Before the Breakthrough**

**The Setup (Fall 2018):**
- Church handyman gave Keith complete client list (35-36 names)
- Keith called every single person
- Result: NOBODY picked up the phone
- Action: Left 35-36 voicemails
- Emotion: "Super discouraging"

**The Temptation to Quit:**
- Looking at last 5 numbers on list
- Out-of-state area codes
- Thought: "They probably don't even live here"
- Almost didn't call them
- Seemed pointless after 30+ failures

**The Breakthrough Call:**
- Called out-of-state number anyway
- Left voicemail
- Person called back 2-3 days later
- Far out of town handyman job
- **Critical conversation**: Customer mentioned friend in solar sales
- This connection led to US Solar opportunity
- **The call Keith almost didn't make changed everything**

**Pattern**: The breakthrough is often in the last call, the last application, the last attempt. Most people quit at call 30 of 35. Success comes to those who make calls 31-35.

**Significance**: Universal principle - perseverance through discouragement. Concrete numbers (35-36 calls, zero pickups, almost quit at 30) make it tangible and repeatable.

### **2b. The "$43 Landing Page" Pattern - Undervalue to Prove Value**

**The Crisis (March 2020):**
- March 16, 2020: "15 Days to Slow the Spread" - COVID lockdown begins
- Door knockers can't work
- Keith fired ~2 months earlier (January 2020), doing drywall at Zion Construction
- Stacie has stable job as Administrative Assistant to VP of Sales at US Solar
- Keith's fear: "How long until my wife doesn't have her job?"

**The Opportunity:**
- Company desperate: needs landing page to generate leads online
- Looking for somebody to create it
- Stacie: "My husband knows how to do that"
- Keith's introduction: "That I know how to do this stuff with media and marketing"

**The Underpricing:**
- Keith charged: $43
- Keith's assessment: "Which was dumb"
- Why so low? No confidence? Prove value first? Get foot in door?

**The Result:**
- "They ended up using it"
- "It works so well that company was able to start getting sales again"
- **Critical**: "That actually caused company to be in business during lockdown restrictions"
- **Keith's summary**: "My landing pages I created for them"
- Direct result: Hired as Marketing Director for US Solar + US Roofing

**The Journey:**
- January 2020: Fired from sales (middle/end of month)
- January 2020-April 2020: Hanging drywall (3 months)
- ~March 20-23, 2020: $43 landing page (less than week after March 16 lockdown)
- April 2020: Marketing Director
- **3 months from fired/drywall to Director**

**Pattern**: Sometimes you drastically undervalue yourself to get opportunity to prove yourself. Then the opportunity becomes the career. Price isn't the point when you're establishing credibility.

**Significance**: Career pivots don't always come from negotiating up - sometimes they come from proving value at any price, then letting performance speak.

**Key Financial Lessons from Keith's Journey:**

1. **Underpricing Can Be Strategic**: $43 landing page proved value → Marketing Director role
2. **Savings Buffers Disappear Fast**: $2,400 → $800 after truck repairs and unexpected costs
3. **Commission-Only Is High Risk**: 100% commission with no training = 13 months of struggle
4. **Bonuses Aren't Guaranteed**: Both Keith and Stacie had bonuses eliminated
5. **Impact Doesn't Equal Immediate Pay**: $60M growth at $850-1,300/week

**Pattern**: Keep producing results regardless of compensation → build track record → leverage for next opportunity. Keith's response to undercompensation was always to prove more value, not to demand more pay.

**Significance for Storytelling**:
