## KEY FINANCIAL LESSONS

### 1. Underpricing Can Be Strategic
- $43 landing page kept US Solar in business during COVID
- Led directly to Marketing Director position
- Sometimes proving value at any price opens doors

### 2. Savings Buffers Disappear Faster Than Expected
- $2,400 became $800 after truck repairs and unexpected costs
- Always plan for 2-3x expected transition expenses

### 3. Commission-Only Is High Risk/High Reward
- 100% commission with no training = 13 months of struggle
- But also potential for $500/lead and $1,200-$3,500/close
- Skills developed transfer to next opportunity

### 4. Bonuses Aren't Guaranteed Income
- Both Keith and Stacie had quarterly bonuses eliminated
- Base salary dropped when bonuses ended
- Don't build lifestyle around bonus income

### 5. Impact Doesn't Equal Immediate Compensation
- $3M → $60M growth at $850-1,300/week
- #2 most reviewed company in US at below-market salary
- Long-term value: skills, portfolio, track record
