# KEITH GROBEN - "ALMOST QUIT" MOMENTS

## THE PARADOX: STRENGTH BECOMES WEAKNESS

**Critical Context:**
Keith's ability to persist through difficulty and NOT quit is a tremendous strength that built his career. However, this same strength became a **blindness and weakness** at Zone 5—he stayed approximately 2 years longer than he should have because he doesn't quit easily.

**The Pattern:**
- Strength: Perseverance through hardship, loyalty, working through problems
- Weakness: Staying in toxic situations longer than wise, not recognizing when quitting IS the right move
- Learning: "Sometimes the strongest move is knowing when to walk away"

---

## BURNS CONSTRUCTION - THE FALL THROUGH THE FLOOR (2018)

### The Incident

**The Job:**
- Removing tile flooring from house with water damage
- Insulation had been put in wrong way
- Underneath floor was compromised

**The Accident:**
- Keith was being careful and following supervisor's instructions
- Fell straight through the floor
- Dropped about 4 feet
- Landed on hard sand and dirt mixture
- **Injuries:**
  - Gashed open hand from tile
  - Scraped up calf
  - Could have been much worse (face on jagged tile)

**Why He Should Have Quit:**
- Clearly didn't have the skill level for this work
- Didn't have adequate precautions/training
- Dangerous situation
- Small company couldn't have paid medical bills
- No workers' compensation or insurance protection
- Physical danger to his body (sole provider for family of 6)

**Why He Didn't Quit:**
- "My family needed me to work it out and to have a job"
- Financial desperation overrode physical safety
- Felt he had no other options
- Kept ongoing despite the danger

**The Lesson:**
This shows the desperation of the Tampa wilderness season—willing to risk physical injury because family needed income. But also shows the persistence pattern that would later keep him at Zone 5 too long.

## BURNS CONSTRUCTION - TRYING TO LEAVE BUT COULDN'T (Fall 2018)

### The Failed Exit Attempt

**The Attempt:**
- Keith tried to leave Burns Construction
- Wanted to do full freelancing and handyman work
- **Could not make it happen**
- "I just didn't have the sales skills to do it"

**The Humbling Return:**
- Had to ask if he could stay on longer
- Burns agreed to let him stay
- **Context:** Burns was "losing tons of money as a construction company"
- "Not really making any money" themselves
- They were nice enough to keep Keith on anyway

**Why This Matters:**
- Shows Keith recognizing his skill gaps ("I just didn't have the sales skills")
- Humble enough to admit failure and ask to return
- Burns' generosity during their own financial struggle
- Eventually did leave when ready—but had to build skills first
- This failure pushed him to the 35-36 prospecting calls that led to US Solar

**The Timing:**
- Tried to leave: ~November 2018
- Asked to stay on: After failed freelance attempt
- Finally left: When US Solar opportunity came (December 2018)
- Connection: The "last five calls" story happened during this period of trying to make freelance work

## UNION DIGITAL MEDIA - ALMOST DIDN'T APPLY (2007)

### The Self-Doubt

**The Hesitation:**
- Didn't think he had right training
- Didn't think he had right skills they needed
- Self-taught without formal education
- "More or less figuring it out"
- Believed he COULD do something
- But didn't feel like he "had arrived"
- Didn't think portfolio was very strong

**The Interview Confession:**
- In the actual interview, Keith told interviewer:
- "Hey, you know? I didn't think that you would really consider me because I don't have any schooling"

**The Response:**
- Interviewer (who became his boss): "Well, we ignore that anyway"
- "We just want to look at your portfolio and how you are with clients"
- Education didn't matter—results did
- Portfolio and client skills were what counted

**Why He Applied Anyway:**
- Despite self-doubt
- Despite feeling unqualified
- Needed job security ahead of economic crisis
- "Led by Holy Spirit" to apply

**The Validation:**
- Got the job (August 2007)
- Worked there until February 2008
- Proved he WAS qualified despite lack of formal education
- Early pattern: Results matter more than credentials

**The Lesson:**
Keith's pattern of self-doubt despite actual competence. He was MORE qualified than he felt—portfolio spoke for itself. This self-doubt theme continues throughout career despite consistent success.

## FREELANCING - ALMOST GAVE UP MULTIPLE TIMES (2006-2008)

### The Craigslist Saturation Crisis

**The Problem:**
- Craigslist became very saturated
- People from overseas posting with extremely cheap prices
- Divided the market:
  - Some people wanted to work only with US-based providers
  - Others didn't care—just wanted cheapest price
  - "They didn't understand the difference because it was so new doing websites and all that kind of stuff"

**The Struggle:**
- Not being able to find jobs
- Not being able to find clients
- Constant competition with below-market rates
- Market confusion about value vs. price

**Almost Gave Up:**
- Keith: "I almost gave that up"
- The saturation made it feel impossible
- Relentlessly posting on Craigslist with minimal response

### The Rent Miracle Story

**The Crisis:**
- Rent was within the 5-day grace period
- It was around the 1st of the month
- Keith kept relentlessly posting on Craigslist
- Thinking: "Man, this is just almost impossible"

**The Breakthrough:**
- Somebody contacted him for "a pretty big project"
- Went to see them the next day
- Day after that: Had money in hand
- **Amount: ALL of his rent money**
- "To be honest with you, all of my rent money just from posting on Craigslist"
- Keith: "And that was very exciting"

**The Pattern:**
- Kept posting when it felt impossible
- Breakthrough came right at desperation moment
- Rent-level financial pressure throughout freelance years
- But kept going despite pressure

### Had to Stop Freelancing After Housing Crash

**2007-2008 Context:**
- Phoenix housing market peaked in 2006
- Keith spotted crisis signs mid-2007
- Clients pulling back
- Had to get corporate job (Union Digital Media)
- "I had to stop focusing on freelancing after the Phoenix housing market crash"
- Keith: "I always loved freelancing" (but circumstances forced pause)

**Clarification:**
Keith doesn't view this as "almost gave up"—it was strategic pivot due to market collapse. He always loved freelancing, just had to adapt to survive economically.

## ZONE 5 - ALMOST DIDN'T LEAVE IN MARCH 2024

### The Two-Year Delay

**The Context:**
- Culture shift started ~5-6 months before beginning of 2023
- Major shift at beginning of 2023
- Didn't leave until May/June 2024
- Keith: "We stayed too long. Probably 2 years too long"

### The Temptation to Stay

**The Easy Path:**
- "It would have been much easier to just try to take whatever weird correction that the boss and his wife wanted to give us"
- Not that they were correcting Keith and Stacie as people
- But would be "doubtable and have a correction in our mindset"
- Essentially: Say "Yeah, you know, we just want to stay here at the company or whatever"
- "We don't really see ourselves growing and being established here"
- **Translation:** Hide and bury what they really wanted to do

**The Secret Plan That Would Have Failed:**
- Continue for job security
- Secretly build out what they wanted to do on the side
- Keith: "But to be honest, we needed a catalyst in our life"
- **Why it would have failed:**
  - "We're so much the type of people to stick stuff through and to be loyal and work hard"
  - "We never really would have done that. To be honest with you, we never really would have left"
  - Or if they tried the secret plan: "We wouldn't have enough time to work on stuff anyways"

**The Personality Factor:**
- Keith and Stacie's strength: Loyalty, persistence, hard work
- Became weakness: Made them stay in toxic situation
- Couldn't half-commit to leaving
- Needed clean break, not gradual exit

**The Catalyst:**
- The March 2024 confrontation
- Being told "you're not bearing fruit" (gaslighting)
- The broken promise of "we'll invest in your ministry"
- Finally provided the catalyst to act on what they knew

**The Lesson:**
Sometimes you need an external catalyst because your own loyalty/persistence keeps you trapped. The very qualities that make you valuable (loyalty, hard work) can blind you to when it's time to leave.

## PANIC DISORDER - LOWEST MOMENTS (2018-2020)

### The Constant Mental Battle

**The Context:**
- Wheel explosion July 1, 2018
- 2.5-3 years of panic disorder
- "All I could ever think about"
- Could not give himself "an hour off of working to the max"
- Had a lot of low moments in panic

### Low Moment #1: The 8 Appointments Saturday (First Month Sales)

**The Setup:**
- Within first month of door-to-door solar sales
- Keith had finally figured out how to set appointments
- Had **8 appointments booked for Saturday**
- This felt like breakthrough after struggling

**The Disaster:**
- 7 of the 8 fell through
- One after another canceling or not showing
- It was almost time for the 8th appointment
- Keith talking to coworker: "I don't know how to do this"

**The Crisis of Faith:**
- Keith to coworker: **"I don't even know what faith is"**
- **"I don't know how to have faith and stay positive"**
- "You know, my kids are all depending on me"
- Desperation point—questioning fundamental beliefs
- Family pressure weighing on him during panic

**The Turnaround:**
- Keith: "But I just had a good attitude"
- Went to 8th appointment with Brandon (manager, also pastor)
- **Closed the deal**
- Made $500
- First breakthrough after devastating Saturday

**Why This Matters:**
- Shows Keith functioning while in crisis
- "I don't even know what faith is" = raw honesty
- Kept showing up despite internal collapse
- Good attitude despite questioning everything
- One win after seven losses sustained him

### Low Moment #2: The Job Interview He Almost Took (Good Years at US Solar)

**The Context:**
- During the "good years" at US Solar (2020-2022)
- But Keith was struggling in sales
- Company had "already kind of given up on me"
- Remember: They weren't training, gave him paperwork and iPad, left him to figure it out

**The Escape Plan:**
- **Thursday:** Booked interview for another job for following Monday
- Was ready to leave US Solar
- Felt like he was failing

**The Prayer:**
- Keith prayed: "If there's something that I can get out of this, because I truly do want to learn how to sell, you know, help me Lord"
- Despite booking exit interview, still prayed for breakthrough
- Wanted to learn sales skills even if leaving

**The Friday-Saturday Miracle:**
- Thursday: Booked Monday interview
- Friday: Went out door-to-door anyway
- **Saturday: Got walk-in sale by himself**
- Made probably **$1,300** from that sale
- Changed everything

### The Walk-In Sale Story (Saturday)

**The Details:**
- Same-day appointment
- Keith knocked on door
- They said: "Come back in an hour"
- Keith came back, totally sold them himself
- Manager Brandon came by to fill out paperwork
- Brandon was "really surprised that I did it"

**The Decision:**
- Didn't go to Monday interview
- Kept at US Solar
- "Was able to pretty much like make a normal amount of money for a few months"

**What Kept Him Going:**
- "Just the need to survive"
- "The need to figure this out"
- **Critical insight:** "Because I knew that there was something on the other side of what my mind didn't know, to be honest"
- Faith that breakthrough existed even when he couldn't see how

**The Lesson:**
Keith had one foot out the door (literally scheduled exit interview) but stayed because of one breakthrough. Shows his pattern: One win convinces him to keep going. This persistence built his career but also kept him at Zone 5 too long.

## PATTERN ANALYSIS ACROSS ALL "ALMOST QUIT" MOMENTS

### Common Threads

**1. Financial Desperation as Primary Driver:**
- Burns Construction: Family needed income despite physical danger
- Freelancing: Rent within 5-day grace period
- Zone 5: Job security vs. desire to leave
- Panic moments: "My kids are all depending on me"

**2. One Breakthrough Sustains Through Many Failures:**
- 7 appointments fell through → 8th closed ($500)
- 35-36 calls no pickups → 36th changed everything
- Ready to leave US Solar → $1,300 walk-in sale kept him there
- Rent crisis → Big project came through just in time

**3. Self-Doubt Despite Competence:**
- Union Digital Media: Didn't think qualified (but was)
- Freelancing: Portfolio not strong (but employer disagreed)
- Panic moments: "I don't know how to do this" (but did it anyway)

**4. Persistence as Both Strength and Weakness:**
- **Strength:** Built career through not quitting when hard
- **Weakness:** Stayed at Zone 5 ~2 years too long because doesn't quit
- **Lesson:** Sometimes quitting IS the strong move

**5. Divine Timing/Provision:**
- Rent miracle: Exact amount needed from Craigslist
- Union Digital Media: Applied despite self-doubt, got job ahead of crash
- $1,300 sale: Right before leaving for other job
- $500 sale: 8th appointment after 7 failures

**6. Functioning Through Crisis:**
- Fell through floor → Kept working
- Panic disorder → Still closed sales
- 7 failures → Showed up for 8th with "good attitude"
- Self-doubt → Applied anyway

## THE META-LESSON: WHEN TO QUIT VS. WHEN TO PERSIST

**Keith's Wisdom:**
"It's important to paint this backdrop that there are a lot of times when I didn't quit. And I moved ahead anyways, and even though that's a strength in one regard, it became a weakness and a blindness at US Solar when I should have quit much earlier than I did, but I didn't."

**The Discernment Question:**
How do you know when persistence is strength vs. stubbornness?

**Keith's Pattern Suggests:**
- **Keep going when:** One breakthrough after many failures, divine timing moments, learning is happening
- **Quit when:** Toxic environment destroying family, 2+ years of hoping it changes, your strength (loyalty) being exploited

**The Zone 5 Lesson:**
Keith's persistence built his career ($60M success) but his same persistence kept him in toxic environment too long. The meta-story is learning when each response is appropriate.
