## Salvation for All Who Believe

### Introduction: Discovering the Unshakable Foundation

Have you ever found yourself in a situation where you knew God was leading you to do something, but the obstacles seemed insurmountable? It's in these moments that we discover the true power that lies within the gospel message.
In this chapter, I'll share my personal journey of discovering the unshakable foundation of the gospel and how it became the driving force behind our "Revival Now" meeting. Join me as we explore the transformative power of the gospel and how it can ignite a revival in our lives and communities.

### Backstory: A Vision for Revival

I had one of the craziest dreams, one of the most incredible visions I've ever experienced. The result of it was a clear directive to hold a revival meeting in a neighboring town, which we called "Revival Now." It was going to be a Holy Ghost fire-filled meeting where we demonstrated healing, baptism of the Holy Spirit, and miracles. At that time, I was simply following God's voice, even though I didn't fully understand it.
I had been watching "Azusa Now," put on by Lou Engle, and was closely following Bethel Church in Northern California, who were a part of it. Something deep inside me sparked, and God touched it. The following year, He told me to do a one-day meeting called "Revival Now," and I had a crazy vision about it.
As I started putting everything together, everybody was excited and wanted to be a part of it, even though it seemed pretty much out of our reach. Throughout the process, I kept having this feeling that I didn't know how the big promise I saw in my spirit from God would work out. I didn't know how there would be miracles, how there would even be a revival, or how any of it would actually happen. But I knew I had to keep going.

### Desire: Longing for Something More

As you've come to know throughout my book so far, I really love the Lord and want to do good. In my journey to learn how to overcome, this would be one of my first experiences tapping into the very thing that allows the joy of the Lord to be my strength - the joy of my salvation and the revelation of my salvation being the cause of that joy, which ultimately allowed me to overcome in a very practical way.
I really wanted to do good for the Lord, so with all boldness, I decided to move forward and make this revival happen, even though it seemed impossible and I didn't know how it was going to work. I remember it started to change my view and paint me in a different light, even to myself.
More than anything, I wanted to see something big break loose. I longed to see revival, to witness something awesome happen. I desired to experience in person what I had seen on YouTube or Facebook Live from Azusa Now. As the Lord was leading me, however, it began to shape up a little differently. We didn't have a huge worship band, even though we had some worship. Nevertheless, I held onto the belief that somehow, some way, it would shape up to be the vision I had seen, the one I so desperately wanted to come to pass.

### Hitting the Wall: Opposition and Limitations

While I was excited about "Revival Now," not everyone shared my enthusiasm. One influential person in the town where we were going to hold the meeting was adamantly opposed to it. They felt that what I was doing was infringing on their territory and that it was against Assemblies of God policy. They were determined to find anything they could inside of our policies and bylaws to stop me, literally telling me that they would "throw the book at it."
They assured me that I knew what I was doing was wrong, which left me confused. I didn't see anything wrong with what I was doing, and I thought anyone who loved God and wanted to see revival would be happy about it. This opposition rocked me and set me back on my heels. In another set of circumstances or situation prior in my life, it could have actually kept me from going forward.
However, because I had such a deep revelation in my spirit and my heart for what God had revealed to me, I sought God more deeply. In seeking Him, I came to the understanding that I didn't possess the revelation of the power to actually move forward and accomplish what needed to be accomplished. Not only was there a person standing against me, but we also didn't have enough money, help, or a way to market the event. It became clear that I was in over my head.

### New Vehicle: The Power of the Gospel

In seeking God, I remember fasting for three days and then praying in tongues for a certain number of hours that I wanted to fulfill within a week. In doing so, I understood the verse in Romans that says, "the gospel is the power of God" (Romans 1:16).
As I explored what that meant, drawing from the book of Isaiah and that specific verse in Romans, I felt deeply impressed and led to preach the gospel message and make it a part of what I was advertising and putting out in all of our social media posts.
Having never intentionally done a gospel message before, I quickly learned that there is a difference between teaching the Bible, preaching the gospel, and simply preaching thoughts from the Bible. I discovered a power that comes out of the word of God—the actual words on the page and the words you speak—a power that is not your own. As I released that power out of me, I became really happy and started to attach myself to it in a greater way.
Even though I wanted revival, I didn't know how it was going to happen. Now, I could see how it could practically occur, not just by accident or God making a decision, but as a byproduct of the scripture that says, "I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God unto salvation to all who believe" (Romans 1:16). The gospel has to be preached, and when I think of that scripture, I realize that the gospel itself is the power of God.
Over the next several months, that power grew in me, reviving me and causing our "Revival Now" to truly be a place where people were set free, healed from cancer and addictions, baptized in the Holy Spirit, and spoke in tongues. When I preached my message there, that gospel power was released. It was stirring me, but the power was also inherently present, and people were, in a sense, activated to preach that gospel in the community and to see a revival or revelation of preaching the gospel in that revival.
We laid hands on everybody, and they fell backward under the power of God. The gospel truly is the power of God unto salvation for all who believe, and it became the unshakable foundation for the revival we experienced.

Salvation is the power to change anything and everything. Preach it every day and every week and give people an opportunity to be saved every time you preach.

> "For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek." (Romans 1:16, ESV)

> "And I, when I came to you, brothers, did not come proclaiming to you the testimony of God with lofty speech or wisdom. For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified." (1 Corinthians 2:1-2, ESV)

We were doing the Revival Now conference, simply following the Holy Spirit to put it on. Having this goal, it caused me to seek things out I hadn't before. At one point, I started to realize the difference between preaching the Word of God and the Gospel.
If you've never made this distinction before, let me tell you how I did it. I went through Isaiah 56. I studied it and did a Facebook Live video so I could preach it.
When I did, it was rough. I didn't understand it as much as I did other doctrines. And when I preached it, a noticeable power left my mouth while I was speaking. A power came over me like fire.
This is when I realized that the Gospel, if you simply preach it or even talk about it, releases God's power to save.
If you've never done a gospel sermon, or simply think those types of messages are only for evangelists, you need to do it the next time you preach.

> "As for you, always be sober-minded, endure suffering, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry." (2 Timothy 4:5, ESV)

No matter what your ministry is, doing the work of an evangelist makes full proof of it.
