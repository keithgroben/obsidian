# Chapter 7: The Deep Well of Your Prosperity

## Introduction: You Need Breakthrough Power

### Unlocking the Secrets of True Prosperity

Have you ever found yourself at a crossroads, desperately seeking a breakthrough in your life?
A moment where you know deep down that there's more to your journey than what you've experienced so far?
That's exactly where I found myself when I stepped into Dr. Rodney Howard-Brown's church in Tampa, Florida. Little did I know that this single day would reshape my understanding of prosperity and set me on a path to discovering the overflowing abundance God had in store for me.
Join me as we dive into the transformative story of how God taught me to tap into the deep well of true prosperity. Together, we'll uncover the keys to breaking free from limitation, embracing God's abundant blessings, and stepping into the purpose He has for your life.

## The Backstory: A Surreal Experience

My second day in Tampa and first day at Dr. Rodney Howard-Brown's church is something I'll never forget.
We arrived on time but had to sit in the back, probably with one of the worst seats, right behind a pillar and under a speaker. I couldn't see Dr. Brown very well unless he moved to the left or right of the pillar.
It felt surreal to see him in person when I'd only seen him on a small phone screen or computer monitor.
At one point in the service, I felt led to pray, and the Holy Spirit began to speak to me, revealing why I was there.

## The Desire: Pursuing the Breakthrough

We had left everything behind—sold over half of what we owned, put the rest in storage, packed up our kids and 26-foot camper, and drove an old suburban that broke down on the way (that's another story)—from a small mountain town in Nevada to sea-level, subtropical Tampa, Florida.
All of this because my greatest desire was to have a breakthrough in prosperity. In Ely, we had dug out the trenches of our faith in healing and rekindled the flames of evangelism, but the breakthrough power of prosperity, which leads to financial abundance, required a different scenario to learn.
I desperately wanted to break through, succeed in God's will, and overcome every limitation I had ever known. More than that, I wanted my children and wife to have the abundant life I read about in scripture.
I wanted this so badly that I moved without knowing anyone, having no job prospects, and lacking the qualifications for a job due to my past decade as a pastor. But there I was, sitting and listening, when God told me to read Ezekiel 37:1-14 about the dry bones and said, "Teach my people to prosper." That would become my primary ministry.

## Hitting the Wall: Facing Limitations

Just as obvious as two tons of iron, there I was, sitting without a job, job prospects, secular skills, money, or connections. I had moved there to learn the very thing God told me was to be my ministry.
My immediate response to the Lord was, "Okay, but I don't know how to do that."
His reply was, "I will teach you how." That exchange between God and me lasted maybe two or three minutes.
I wrote down the scripture reference and "teach my people to prosper" on a blank sheet of paper, fully expecting to get details from God about continuing the ministry I had in Nevada. But I would soon find out that all of that would die painfully and quickly.
I truly hit a wall of limitation in that moment and didn't know what to do. The next months, up to two years of my life, were challenging, difficult, and confusing.

## The Epiphany: A New Understanding

The scripture, "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge" (Hosea 4:6, NKJV), became very real to me as God taught me how to prosper so I could teach His people. I wanted to write this book to testify that you can come out of anywhere, no matter how dire your situation may seem.
I was living in abject, extreme religious poverty, lacking money and resources while pastoring a church and relying on government support. I couldn't even buy clothes for my kids without help.
I had a twisted religious experience and teaching that it was right for me to have too little, believing it was God's will. Jesus warned, "Take heed that the light which is in you is not darkness" (Luke 11:35, NKJV). How great is that darkness!

### The Transformation Process

In those 18 months, everything in my life was dug up and blown up. To all observation, I appeared to be a loser—someone who had given up on life and was extremely lost, despite having served the Lord for almost 20 years.
But God did hidden work in my heart to undo everything. Three men in my life provided patience and employment to help me get out of it. Then, what was wrong on the inside started to become right—the true definition of new wine going into a new wineskin (see Matthew 9:17).
While my wife and I grew financially, reaching the upper middle class, it might have appeared that we had made it. But I really did arrive when I overcame the biggest internal battle of my life—the key to prosperity.

### The Key to Prosperity

You need overcoming power, and if you think of money whenever someone says "prosperity," you are not thinking of biblical prosperity. Money is a result of biblical prosperity, among many other results.
The biggest lesson I learned as God was rebuilding me is that I have to prophesy what God is saying about me, calling things that are not as though they were, just like in Ezekiel 37. You have to keep persisting in prophesying over your life what God has said about you.
That's 50% of the battle. The other part is truly understanding the word of God for yourself, getting a full revelation of who Jesus Christ is to you in your personal prosperity.

Lastly, in order to achieve measurable Great Commission success, you are going to need a breakthrough power. Just to let you in on the truth, this breakthrough power is accessible all the time.

There is no prophet who should ever come and tell you about a special breakthrough anointing. The Anointing breaks the yoke of bondage. The Anointing itself is special and breaks through.

> "And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing." (Isaiah 10:27, ESV)

If you look back in this book at the Great Commission, remember it has elements. Those elements are connected together like a triangle. Power is one of them. It takes power to accomplish Evangelism. The Gospel is the power of God. As you preach it, the revelation of that power sticks in you and you carry power much like we read about the apostles. Handkerchiefs, shadows.

As you teach believers to obey Christ's commands, it takes injecting the Gospel message into it for the power of change to be released to them. And again, this power clings to you and you carry it.

Eventually, this power gets so strong that you are enabled to accomplish more and more towards Great Commission success. You will have times of personal revelation where God eliminates your sins, your flesh, and your doubt. All of these things and more that He touches cause you to host more of His breakthrough anointing.

Without this power, you will be stopped by the works of the enemy and the weakness of your flesh. Jesus came to destroy the works of the devil. The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak.

What allows you to "tap into" that power is perhaps so simple, it's easy to miss. It's a focused mind and a believing heart. You have the mind of Christ. Think on things above and not below.

As challenges to your mission of speaking your faith and the Gospel arise, your Christ mindset and believing heart will allow you to loose the power which has been planted on the inside.

It's almost incremental, the growth. As you dig to get more gospel revelation, power is deposited. You then speak it, realizing that salvation, breakthrough power to others. Then miracles happen because it's the power of God unto salvation coming out of your mouth. You then step back and consider more and greater things you can do for Jesus to spread his mission. This in turn requires a greater degree of power in you. So, you study and preach or teach, bringing people into salvation (evangelism) or fuller salvation (discipleship).

This was the last element my wife and I got. And perhaps it's one that took a higher level of concentration and focus from us. Undoing bad mindsets and some twisted doctrine takes time, as it did for us. More so for me than her. For everyone, including you, who wants to live a life of Great Commission success, power is what will get you there.

Take that first step and dig into the gospel message in Isaiah 56.
