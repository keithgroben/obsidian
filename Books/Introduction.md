# What is the Ministry Jesus is Doing?

Are you pouring your heart and soul into your ministry and falling short of the success you dream of?

*I know firsthand the challenges that come with pastoring.*
This job is rewarding and difficult. Some challenges will make you feel stuck or question yourself. Others will make you question God and your calling.

In my two decades of ministry, I have served as a Senior Pastor for one of them.

I took it on with a great amount of patience and persistence. Ultimately it led me to uncover seven crucial truths I wish I had known before I became a pastor.
- I discovered these because of my great need.
- They were a result of much fasting and prayer.
- They are doctrinally sound.
- I've proven them in my life and see them proving in others.
- They are the keys to successful ministry.

## In this book I'm honest and vulnerable

I have nothing to gain if you're impressed with my knowledge, accomplishments or life. As much as I want you to learn what I know, I want you to understand the stories of how I learned these keys.

*Why?*
I want you to succeed— That's it. There is no other reason I wrote this book.
Your ministry has peoples eternal souls connected to it.
Jesus knows how to reach them.
You can partner with Him in His ministry.

## Have a Thriving Ministry Today

If you're a pastor with years of experience, or you're just starting out, the insights I share meet you where you are. You'll learn what can change the course of your ministry and life.

### Here's what's inside

1. The great commission is the Church's mission.
2. There is a baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire, and you need it.
3. See everyone like Christ does.
4. Remember the joy of your salvation every day.
5. Believe it or not, there is a miraculous source of power that has nothing to do with you.
6. Without creating community you will go absolutely nowhere.
7. There's supernatural power you need to achieve in life.

Explore these lessons, discover the biblical truths and take action steps for your own breakthrough.
