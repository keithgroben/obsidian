# Introduction: What You Don't Know Limits You

When the Bible says, 'My people perish for lack of knowledge" it describes the cause of many problems I've faced. Chances are, this is also the cause for some problems you've faced.
It's incredible to think of, actually. Kind of like standing at a cliffs edge and not realizing the danger if you fall. Lack of knowledge causes destruction.
For many years I didn't know there was a powerful baptism in the Holy Spirit and fire. As and AG minister I was well aware of the baptism of the Holy Spirit with the initial physical evidence of speaking in tongues.
The baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire should have been something I had before entering ministry full time. Yet it was something I'd discover many years later.
- I was saved for over 15 years.
- I was in the ministry.
- I spoke in tongues
- I lacked power

## Let's Get Out And Push

You can't drive a car without gas. To make it move without fuel, you'd have to push it.
If you push it down a flat road, it could move with some effort. With a few friends you can get enough speed to go up a hill. Evenually, though, it would roll back on you.
I imagine this is why some church leaders who don't know about this baptism quench it in their services. If the Holy spirit was let loose and they aren't baptized, it would be like they are going downhill in a car without gas. They'd be out of control.
Most people don't like to be out of control. So if you understand the baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire you won't be.
In service to an almighty, all powerful God, you don't get very far without His power.
Sure, you'll make some progress on your own. But you'll navigate toward the path of least resistance, like a flat road. And eventually you'll face a hill. Without power that hill is an impossible obstacle that will harm you if you try to push up it.

## Now Power Through

You need the Holy Spirit's power to climb over obstacles the same way a car needs gas. It's obvious that a car with gas can go up a hill, travel great distances, and reach impossible locations compared to pushing the car.
In life (and ministry) the baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire is gas in the car. It gives you achieving power.
If your ministry is focused on what I call a fringe benefit of Christianity, power isn't needed to make progress.
If you're not focused on Jesus you don't need power.

**The Great Commission was Jesus ministry when He was present on the earth.**
- Today the Great Commission is your ministry.
- You are the body of Christ, Christ is still doing the same ministry.
- Jesus has not changed his ministry.

He gives you power to continue His ministry. Without His power it's almost impossible.

# The Backstory: Find A Need and Fill It

## Love In Action

Tommy Barnett is an amazing Pastor. He was my pastor in Phoenix for 12 years. His heart of finding a need and filling it is the thrust of his ministry. In his life he's pastored one of the fastest growing churches, started the Dream Center ministry, won millions of souls, and is converting colleges into a way to fund multiple inner-city outreaches across America.
Well into his 80's he's still going today. If you've ever heard him preach, you'll instantly see why he is so successful.
He travels and preaches all for the sake of winning the lost and meeting needs of hurting people.

One powerful ministry I learned from him is called Adopt-A-Block.
To give you a picture Adopt-A-Block is a sustained outreach ministry aimed at transforming neighborhoods.
- Clean the streets
- Assist the needy with things like yard work and food
- Help those who can't help themselves
- Pastor your neighborhoods
- Win the lost and grow the church

Shortly after starting my pastorate I announced plans to start an Adopt-a-Block at our church. I saw first hand in Phoenix how powerful this ministry is. In summary, you go into your city to pastor the people instead of hoping people come to you. The service you provide to them shows your genuine love and breaks preoccupation.
It's the perfect sustained outreach that opens up many possibilities and church growth.
I immediately got pushback from my church, all the time. Trying to overcome their resistance was impossible for me.
After a few weeks of begging people to come to a planning meeting, gather tools, and join me on a Saturday, not one person was willing to do it.
So I canceled the ministry before it started.
I realize now I was leading in my own strength. I needed something beyond myself, something supernatural.

## The Dream: A Growing Church

### Phoenix Beginnings

Pastor Barnett modeled a thriving church. I served there for a decade. That "dna" stuck with me.
I worked first hand in many ministries. Adopt a block was one of them.
It was hard work, selfless love.
It opened doors to preaching the gospel that door to door evangelism didn't.
It was a long term investment in an area to pastor the people there. Expanding your church.
Stacie and I were very involved.
I wanted the same thing for my church.

### Inexperience Has its Consequences

Inexperience told me to stop. Inexperience told me to "build the people up into evangelism".
Even though this was something I learned from a Bible College teacher. It was still the voice of inexperience.
I don't blame anyone. I executed on my knowledge and faced the results.
I wanted to reach people with Gods love.
It would be years later when I learned to turn around opposition.

## HITTING THE WALL: The New Right Thing With The Same Old Challenges

### This Burning Fire

Fast forward those years.
Almost immediately after I repented that Saturday by the baptismal I got something.
A burning fire and hunger for Gods righteousness.
This drove me to search out soulwinning methods. I spent a couple of weeks researching that and creating a discipleship course.
I remember this time very clearly. So much change. The Holy Spirit and Fire at work.
I was born again, again it seemed.
Stacie and I sat at our kitchen table day after day researching, learning, and organizing methods to win the lost and make disciples.
- We finished our studies.
- We got them ready to print.
- We organized a meeting for the church.

### The Finish Line Was Near

One of the evangelism methods involved a soulwinning script. You just read it to people and pray with them.
When I called the special meeting 8 people came. It was more than enough.
I taught on the great commission and why we needed to win souls every Saturday.
I shared the method. I read the script. This was going to work.
But the room was too quiet. Everyone was reluctant to commit to Saturday soulwinning.
After the meeting they all left quickly except a married couple.
One of them came to me to explain how this will never work. They spoke passionately about it.
After de-escalating them, I realized it happened again.
No one was on board, again. History repeated itself.

## MY TURNING POINT: The Method isn't Sacred, the Message is.

### A Lack of Belief

I went back home realizing I hit the same wall.
- Why was everyone silent?
- Why did they leave so fast?
- Why did they oppose the idea?

Instead of giving up or changing course, I prayed. My prayer had the Holy Spirit and fire in it.
God me gave me wisdom.
The people didn't believe they could do it. They were afraid of talking to strangers.
And since I didn't have a large group of people to pull from, I couldn't find others if they weren't going to help win souls.
The Great Commission is our mission, there is no other mission.
How could I teach them to succeed?

### Same Gift, New Packaging

I went back to my soulwinning toolbox.
I realized I could reduce the barrier of entry for them by doing an outreach.
At an outreach, the people come to us. My church could serve them on neutral ground.
Stacie and I planned to give away backpacks filled with school supplies.
We made an announcement at church about another meeting to win souls. In the announcement I told them about the outreach.
This time 15 people showed up to the special meeting.
When we met I discovered I was right. They were scared and didn't believe they could do it.
This time the room filled with belief.
Because I overcame, they overcame. My fire spread.
One by one they spoke up to offer help.
- "I can order the backpacks!"
- "I can lend you my stage!"
- "I can get an ad on the radio!"

I learned not to compromise the mission.
I learned it takes the baptism of the Holy Spirit and Fire to fulfill the Great Commission.
The message is sacred.
Jesus came to seek and save the lost. That's what we're doing as a church. I just changed the method.
The method isn't sacred.
I overcame my weakness from the beginning of my pastorate.
I led the people into the ministry of reconciliation, not away from it.
I preached weekly on this fire and the Holy Spirit. The church caught it!
The result was outstanding. Over 260 people came and 75 were saved.
And that couple who opposed me, were the biggest volunteers and givers to the outreach.

## SCRIPTURE'S WISDOM

The baptism of the Holy Spirit and fire is power. You might even call it fire power! Here's what the Bible says about it. Accurate scripture knowledge will rule out any private interpretation of scripture.

> "knowing this first of all, that no prophecy of Scripture comes from someone's own interpretation. For no prophecy was ever produced by the will of man, but men spoke from God as they were carried along by the Holy Spirit." (2 Peter 1:20-21, ESV)

Anytime man's spin is put on Gods word, that word is robbed of power:

> "thus making void the word of God by your tradition that you have handed down. And many such things you do." (Mark 7:13, ESV)

Jesus is the baptized.

> "I baptize you with water for repentance, but he who is coming after me is mightier than I, whose sandals I am not worthy to carry. He will baptize you with the Holy Spirit and fire." (Matthew 3:11, ESV)

There's an evidence of speaking in other tongues:

> "And suddenly there came from heaven a sound like a mighty rushing wind, and it filled the entire house where they were sitting. And divided tongues as of fire appeared to them and rested on each one of them." (Acts 2:2-3, ESV)

Acts 1, Peter stood up and preached. Acts 1 it's to be a witness. Acts, we didn't even know there was a Holy Spirit…
