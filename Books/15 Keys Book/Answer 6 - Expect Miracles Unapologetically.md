# Answer 6. Expect Miracles Unapologetically

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/11x9MwTAdxJGYe6rlTqR3bY4WqugrwK_u70DZ3e23i0k*

Miracles aren't optional extras in ministry — they're the birthright of every believer. But nothing happens unless you **expect it**, preach for it, and even *require it* by faith.

> "Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all His benefits: who forgives all your iniquities, who heals all your diseases, who redeems your life from destruction, who crowns you with lovingkindness and tender mercies, who satisfies your mouth with good things..." — **Psalm 103:2-5**

Faith is the bridge between your preaching and supernatural results. If you're not seeing miracles, check your expectations.

## Faith Isn't Passive — It's a Demand

Kenneth E. Hagin taught that when the Bible says "ask," the Greek word *aiteo* often carries a deeper meaning: **to require, demand, or claim as a right**. When you pray in Jesus' name, you're not begging — you're accessing covenant authority.

> "Whatever you ask (aiteo) the Father in My name, He will give you... ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be full." — **John 16:23-24**

This isn't arrogance. It's faith rooted in God's promises. You're not demanding from God — you're demanding what He's already promised.

## How to "Require" Miracles

1. **Preach with Expectation** — Don't just preach *about* miracles — preach *for* them. End sermons with an altar call for miracles and salvation.
2. **Keep a Miracle Journal** — Write down every miracle you're believing for. When one happens, record it and thank God publicly.
3. **Train Your People to Expect** — Role-play bold prayers: *"Father, I require healing for my friend in Jesus' name!"*
4. **Celebrate Miracles Weekly** — Share testimonies every service.

## Why This Works

- **Faith activates God's promises**: Miracles follow those who believe (Mark 16:17-18)
- **Authority dismantles doubt**: Praying in Jesus' name enforces His victory over sickness, poverty, bondage
- **Expectation creates culture**: A church that expects miracles becomes a magnet for the supernatural

**Stop apologizing for expecting God to move.** Preach, pray, and demand miracles — then watch Him fill your "jugs" with overflow.

**"Faith isn't hoping God *might* move — it's knowing He *will*."**
