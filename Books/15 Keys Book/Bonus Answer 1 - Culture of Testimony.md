# Bonus Answer 1: Create a Culture of Testimony

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/192OlHUXc7UvqAgTlCR0DxZzikt6ybvw9vdwUiz9k1HA*

Testimonies build faith and expectation. They remind people that God is still moving, still saving, still healing, and still delivering.

> "And they have conquered him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, for they loved not their lives even unto death." — **Revelation 12:11 (ESV)**

## Why It Matters
- Testimonies build faith by telling of Jesus' power in action
- They create expectation in the hearer
- They glorify God and remind us of His everlasting faithfulness

## How to Implement
1. **Regularly Share Testimonies** — Dedicate time in services for people to share what God has done
2. **Collect Testimonies** — Use a web form or paper form available in your church
3. **Use Social Media** — Post testimonies online to reach a broader audience

## Result
A culture of testimony fuels revival by reminding people of God's active presence and power. It builds faith, expectation, and hunger for more.
