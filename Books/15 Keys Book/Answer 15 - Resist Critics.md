# Answer 15. Resist Critics (Including Yourself)

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YTK1tixry-FbEjXUFhMyDiIgZ5FgtNB0hE027ihuMgI*

Revival isn't without its challenges. As you step out, you'll face critics — both external and internal. External critics may question your methods, motives, or message. Internal critics — your own doubts and insecurities — might whisper lies that undermine your confidence. If you're going to see revival, you must resist critics, stand on God's word, and keep going. People are depending on you.

> Preach the word, it works! *"So shall My word be that goes out from My mouth; it shall not return to Me empty, but it shall accomplish that which I purpose, and shall succeed in the thing for which I sent it."* — **Isaiah 55:11 (ESV)**

## Overcoming Doubt and Opposition

1. **Remember Your Mission** — The Great Commission is the Church's Mission. There is No Other Mission. (Matthew 28:19-20)
2. **You Need God's Approval, Not Man's** — Keep going and eventually critics will go away. *"If I were still trying to please man, I would not be a servant of Christ."* (Galatians 1:10)
3. **Respond with Grace** — *"A soft answer turns away wrath."* (Proverbs 15:1)
4. **Surround Yourself with Encouragers** — *"Iron sharpens iron."* (Proverbs 27:17)

## Stand on God's Word — "My Word Will Not Return Void"

1. **God's Word is Powerful** — *"The word of God is living and active."* (Hebrews 4:12)
2. **God's Word is Unchanging** — *"The grass withers, the flower fades, but the word of our God will stand forever."* (Isaiah 40:8)
3. **God's Word is Effective** — *"So shall My word be that goes out from My mouth."* (Isaiah 55:11)

## Practical Steps to Resist Critics

1. **Guard Your Heart** — Fill it with God's Word
2. **Pray for Your Critics** — Ask God to bless them
3. **Stay Focused on the Mission** — Don't let distractions derail you
4. **Celebrate Small Wins** — What you speak about grows
5. **Speak Truth to Yourself** — When doubt creeps in, attack it with Bible truth
