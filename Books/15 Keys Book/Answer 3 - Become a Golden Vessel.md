## Revival May Be Hit and Miss Without Holiness

If you want to see God move in power, first become a vessel He can use.

> "Therefore, if anyone cleanses himself from what is dishonorable, he will be a vessel for honorable use, set apart as holy, useful to the master of the house, ready for every good work." — **2 Timothy 2:21**

Holiness isn't optional. It's a predecessor of revival. When you go after it, you position yourself as a **golden vessel** — useful to the master, ready for EVERY good work.

## Holiness starts with personal repentance

After pastoring for 10 years, God took me on a journey to cleanse myself from what was dishonorable. Even though I had already served Him, I knew I needed it. I asked Him for it.

> "Blessed be the Lord, who daily loads us with benefits, even the God of our salvation." — **Psalm 68:19**

Repentance isn't a one-time event — it's a lifestyle of feeding from the vine (John 15:5).

In my case, I had to learn how to push past many limitations that caused me to be absent for my wife and children.

I examined my heart daily, responding to the Holy Spirit.

Then, on September 2nd, 2023, during the end of the afternoon session at the Kingdom Business Men's Conference in Tampa, Florida, God spoke to me, declaring I am a golden vessel. He said:

> *"You've proven yourself to be a golden vessel, cleansed yourself from all that is dishonorable. And the same way you went after your family and your children, chase after Mine, and I will make you more abundant than you ever could become by yourself."*

Friends, this isn't easy. It's very hard. **But you can do it.**

It took everything I had. I was a senior pastor, in ministry. I left that all to become holy in Jesus' eyes. I'd never trade that time.

## What About You?

Right now, if the Holy Spirit is convicting you, stop reading and seek the Lord with all of your heart. Call out, "Jesus!" Commit to fasting and prayer.

- **Repent.**
- **Be clean.**
- **Be fit.**
- **Get re-wired for revival.**
- **Re-launch!**

This is the answer to long-term, sustained revival. God uses a holy man or woman and no one else.
