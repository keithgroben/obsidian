# Answer 5. Organize Your Jugs: Planned Gospel Events

Revival doesn't happen by accident. It happens when you **plan for it**.

## Lessons from Pastor Tommy Barnett: "Enlarge Your Circle of Love"

In the late '90s and early 2000s, Tommy Barnett was my pastor at Phoenix First Assembly of God (now Dream City Church). I love him deeply — and in many ways, he still feels like my pastor today.

One message that sticks with me: ***"Enlarge your circle of love."***

- To keep growing outwardly, you must expand your love for others
- In that expansion, you'll find the answers to the problems you've been stuck on

Phoenix First didn't just talk about love — they *organized* it. Under Barnett's leadership, the church exploded with **200+ ministries**, weekly outreaches, and massive annual events designed to share Jesus.

***But here's the secret sauce:*** No event — no matter how "secular" it seemed — happened without the gospel being preached and a large altar call.

**Action Step:** Train your people to invite others relentlessly. Teach them to say: *"Come with me this Saturday — we're giving away free groceries and hope."*

## The Elisha and the Widow's Oil Principle (2 Kings 4:1-7)

The widow had nothing but empty jars. Elisha told her: ***"Go borrow more."*** When she obeyed, God filled every jar with oil.

- Empty jars = opportunities for God's overflow
- Your job isn't to *make* oil — it's to *gather jars*

**Application:**
- Food drives (empty pantry → filled with hope)
- School supply giveaways (backpacks → gospel conversations)
- Free car washes (dirty cars → clean hearts)

## Structured Outreaches Create Space for God's Overflow

The Holy Spirit moves in chaos, but He *multiplies* in structure.

- **Host "No Strings Attached" Events** — Free meals, haircuts, diapers. No sermon — just love. Then invite to Sunday service.
- **Preach an Altar Call Every. Single. Time.** — Barnett did it. You should too.
- **Empower Inviters** — Print invite cards for your congregation.

## Street Evangelism: Planned and Spirit-Led

**Two Approaches:**
1. **The Script** — Use tools like the Gospel Soulwinning Script from The River at Tampa Bay: *"Has anyone ever told you that God loves you and has a wonderful plan for your life?"*
2. **The Spirit** — Ask the Holy Spirit: *"Who needs prayer today?"* Then walk up: *"Can I pray for you?"*

## The Result: Overflow and Revival

At Dream City Church, we saw drug dealers saved at car washes, single moms healed at food pantries, and teenagers baptized in parking lots.

Start small. One outreach a month. Stay consistent.

**"Empty jars + obedience = revival."**
