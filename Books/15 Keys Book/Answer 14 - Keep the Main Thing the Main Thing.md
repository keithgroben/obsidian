# Answer 14. Keep the Main Thing the Main Thing

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1fCK28CjF_9gWODYG8v4Cwgxs5cODStevpQejvAg9rUU*

In the busyness of ministry, it's easy to get distracted. Programs, events, and even good intentions can pull us away from the central mission: the gospel. Revival thrives when we keep the main thing the main thing.

> *"I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and Him crucified."* — **1 Corinthians 2:2**

## Why Staying Focused on the Gospel is Essential

1. **It's the Power of God** — *"The power of God for salvation to everyone who believes."* (Romans 1:16)
2. **It's the Foundation of the Church** — *"Go therefore and make disciples."* (Matthew 28:19)
3. **It's the Heart of Revival** — *"This gospel of the kingdom will be proclaimed throughout the whole world."* (Matthew 24:14)

## Practical Tip: End Every Sermon with a Salvation Altar Call

1. **Keeps the Gospel Front and Center** — *"Of first importance... Christ died for our sins."* (1 Corinthians 15:3-4)
2. **Provides an Opportunity for Response** — *"Now is the favorable time; now is the day of salvation."* (2 Corinthians 6:2)
3. **Demonstrates Urgency** — *"Today, if you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts."* (Hebrews 3:15)
4. **Creates a Culture of Evangelism** — *"How are they to hear without someone preaching?"* (Romans 10:14)

## How to Lead a Salvation Altar Call

1. **Explain the Gospel Clearly** — *"Jesus died for your sins, was buried, and rose again. He offers you forgiveness, new life, and a relationship with God."*
2. **Invite People to Respond** — *"If you want to receive Jesus as your Savior, come forward now."*
3. **Lead Them in Prayer** — *"Lord Jesus, I believe You died for me and rose again. I confess my sins and ask for Your forgiveness. Come into my life and be my Savior. Thank You for saving me. In Jesus' name, Amen."*
4. **Follow Up** — Bible, discipleship book, next steps.

**"Keep the main thing the main thing: The gospel is the power of God for revival."**
