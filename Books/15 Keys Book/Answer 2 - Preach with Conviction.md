# Answer 2. Preach with Conviction, Don't Hold Back

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1CD8tfEyuoxoh781KXPLEERt84gDHariyJcbbIFApi6E*

Reading the Gospel off the page is enough to transfer salvation power to the hearer. This became known to me during the outreach.

## Conviction over Eloquence

When I preached it with conviction, more of Jesus' power was released. Jesus said *"these signs will follow those that believe..."*

Power is no mystery. God put the formula in His word. When you preach the Gospel with belief, powerful signs are released. Miracles multiply the impact.

As a pastor, you're not meant to struggle to have an effective ministry. I pastored for years without knowing this key. When I learned it, I discovered an external force that works on people — convincing them of their need for Jesus, convicting them of sin to repent, and providing faith for salvation and healing.

The external force of miraculous power is released through preaching the gospel. Healing, casting out demons, and deliverance are released every time.

> John 7:38 — "Whoever believes in me, as the Scripture has said, 'Out of his heart will flow rivers of living water.'"

## Three Benefits of the Cross

**The cross provides three clear blessings every believer may access:**

1. **Forgiveness of Sins** — *"If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us."* (1 John 1:9)
2. **Healing of our Body** — Physical healing is part of the SAME atonement. *"By His stripes, we are healed."* (1 Peter 2:24)
3. **Prosperity and Abundance** — *"Though He was rich, yet for your sakes He became poor, that you through His poverty might become rich."* (2 Corinthians 8:9)

What the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ provides isn't a theological concept — these are realities. Preach the Gospel with confidence and conviction.
