# Answer 1. The Gospel is the Power of God

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1uNcQuT9lR-xZKx88vgx-ZaRACxSv2j_vS20Tydw8LEQ*

Revival begins with recognizing the gospel's inherent power.

> "For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the **power** of God **for salvation** to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek." — Romans 1:16

At my church we were passionate about worship. I wanted to expand our stage to make room for more people to fit. For months, I planned the removal of an old, barely used baptismal. It took up prime stage space where the drums could go.

*It was an eyesore, and I was ready to cut it out.*

But deep down, I didn't truly feel like that was a God idea. I was doing it because I had seen successful churches with fancy stages. Nice stage = better worship = more people.

## A Sawzall and the Voice of God

The demo day came. My Sawzall was ready, the church was prepped, I was positioned to make the first cut.

Then, clearly, as if He were standing next to me, God spoke:

- *"Keith, what are you doing?"*
  - *"I'm cutting out the baptismal to make room for the drums."*
- *"Why aren't you using the baptismal?"*
  - *"Because nobody needs baptized."*
- *"And why doesn't anyone need baptized?"*
  - *"Because no one is getting saved."*
- *"And why is that?"*
  - *"Because I'm not telling them about You."*

It hit me like a ton of bricks. I strayed from my first love—Jesus. My ministry was not about people.

## The Gospel Is Enough

*God's words broke me.* I'd been trying to get God to move when He was waiting for me to do something — share the gospel.

I planned an outreach. Not long after the planning, my mom died a few weeks before the outreach. Overwhelmed with grief and responsibilities, I wasn't prepared. I barely had time to outline a message.

In that moment I remembered: *"Let the dead bury their own dead. You, come follow Me." — Luke 9:60.*

I got to work, keeping my eye on the prize. Souls for Jesus.

## There's Power in the Gospel

On outreach day, I stood in front of nearly 250 people with a 17-minute gospel message written on folded up paper.

I read it. No fancy intro. No polished delivery.

God honored His Word. The Spirit of God moved on the crowd in a powerful way. **80 people raised their hands to accept Jesus.**

That's when it clicked: The gospel ***is*** the power of God for salvation. It doesn't need my degree, my charisma, or my polished delivery. It just needs to be shared.

**"The gospel is the power of God to salvation."**
