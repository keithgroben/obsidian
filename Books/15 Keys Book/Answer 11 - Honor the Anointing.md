# Answer 11. Honor the Anointing: Respect Spiritual Leadership

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i0vr_6isag3tU82jGoYuTDsgfOZJ6vw_QdOgKX3oBjY*

Revival thrives in an atmosphere of honor. When we respect and honor spiritual leaders, we create an environment where the Holy Spirit can move freely. But when we dishonor leaders, we quench the Spirit and hinder revival.

> "You shall not speak evil of a ruler of your people." — **Acts 23:5 (ESV)**

Honoring spiritual leadership is not just about respecting individuals — it's about recognizing the anointing and authority God has placed on their lives.

## How Dishonoring Leaders Quenches Revival

1. **It Disrupts Unity** — Dishonor breeds division and strife. *"Behold, how good and pleasant it is when brothers dwell in unity!"* (Psalm 133:1-2)
2. **It Undermines Authority** — *"Obey your leaders and submit to them, for they are keeping watch over your souls."* (Hebrews 13:17)
3. **It Quenches the Spirit** — *"And do not grieve the Holy Spirit of God."* (Ephesians 4:30)
4. **It Blocks Blessings** — *"Honor your father and mother... that it may go well with you."* (Ephesians 6:2-3)

## Cultivating Humility and Unity

1. **Recognize the Anointing** — *"Do not touch my anointed ones."* (Psalm 105:15)
2. **Submit to Authority** — *"Let every person be subject to the governing authorities."* (Romans 13:1)
3. **Speak Life, Not Criticism** — *"Let no corrupting talk come out of your mouths."* (Ephesians 4:29)
4. **Pray for Your Leaders** — *"Brothers, pray for us."* (1 Thessalonians 5:25)
5. **Serve with a Willing Heart** — *"As each has received a gift, use it to serve one another."* (1 Peter 4:10)

## The Blessings of Honoring Leadership

1. **Unity Flourishes** (1 Peter 3:8)
2. **The Spirit Moves Freely** (2 Corinthians 3:17)
3. **Blessings Flow** (Proverbs 3:9-10)
4. **Revival is Sustained** (Psalm 133:1-2)

## Action Steps
1. Evaluate your heart — are you walking in honor?
2. Speak life — choose encouragement over criticism
3. Pray for your leaders regularly
4. Serve with humility
5. Cultivate unity in your church

**"Honor the anointing: Respect spiritual leadership and watch revival flourish."**
