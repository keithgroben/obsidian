# Bonus Answer 2: Pray for Boldness, Not Just Results

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_OvdcfdanZ8oap7ehCQrmuILC6k6IhmKTyayU03te1w*

The early church didn't just pray for results — they prayed for boldness to preach the gospel. Boldness is key to breaking fear, hesitation, and procrastination. Prayer unlocks this tool.

> "And now, Lord, look upon their threats and grant to Your servants to continue to speak Your word with all boldness." — **Acts 4:29 (ESV)**

Boldness makes revival more automatic as believers step out in faith.

## Why It Matters
- Boldness breaks fear and hesitation
- It enables believers to share the gospel confidently
- It aligns our prayers with God's will for the lost to be saved

## How to Implement
1. **Pray for Boldness** — Make boldness a regular part of your prayers
2. **Teach on Boldness** — Teach your church from the scriptures to step out in faith
3. **Model Boldness** — Lead by example, sharing your faith openly

## Result
When believers pray for boldness, they step out in faith, and revival becomes more automatic as the gospel is preached without fear.
