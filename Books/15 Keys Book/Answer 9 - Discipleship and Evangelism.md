# Answer 9. Discipleship and Evangelism: Two Sides of the Same Coin

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Qe5YjxWnhlKmg8eKsrA56k1UxmJFQU9uHfQgoLOomwM*

Evangelism and discipleship are inseparable. Two sides of the same coin. Evangelism brings people into the kingdom; discipleship ensures they grow and thrive.

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them... teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you." — **Matthew 28:19-20**

Jesus didn't just call us to make converts; He called us to make disciples.

## The Gospel's Power to Save *and* Transform

1. **Evangelism: Salvation of the Spirit** — *"Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved."* (Romans 10:13)
2. **Discipleship: Salvation of the Soul** — *"Be transformed by the renewal of your mind."* (Romans 12:2)

## Why Discipleship is Essential

1. **It Sustains Revival** (Hebrews 10:39)
2. **It Transforms Lives** — *"Transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another."* (2 Corinthians 3:18)
3. **It Equips Believers for Ministry** — *"To equip the saints for the work of ministry."* (Ephesians 4:11-12)

## Evangelism → Discipleship → More Evangelism

1. **Evangelism Plants the Seed** — *"I planted, Apollos watered, but God gave the growth."* (1 Corinthians 3:6)
2. **Discipleship Nurtures the Seed** — *"Stir up one another to love and good works."* (Hebrews 10:24)
3. **Discipleship Leads to More Evangelism** — *"Entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also."* (2 Timothy 2:2)

## Practical Steps for Discipleship

1. **Teach the Basics** — salvation, baptism, Bible, prayer, Holy Spirit
2. **Model Christlike Living**
3. **Encourage Accountability**
4. **Provide Resources** — books, Bible studies
5. **Pray Together**
6. **Involve Them in Ministry**

**"The gospel's power to save *and* transform is the foundation of true discipleship."**
