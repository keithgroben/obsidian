# Answer 13. Baptism: Water, Spirit, and Fire

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OBt1BBn1kwsuLD9LPs9LPgESDinFKC_rE2UtyVT2Ew4*

Baptism is not just a symbolic act — it's a powerful, transformative experience that accelerates revival. Three distinct baptisms: **water baptism**, **baptism in the Holy Spirit**, and **baptism by fire**.

> "Repent and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins, and you will receive the gift of the Holy Spirit." — **Acts 2:38-39**

## 1. Water Baptism: The Outward Declaration of Faith

Outward declaration of inward transformation.

**What It Represents:**
- Death to sin and resurrection to new life (Romans 6:4)
- Cleansing and forgiveness of sins (Acts 22:16)
- Identification with Jesus' death, burial, resurrection (Colossians 2:12)

## 2. Baptism in the Holy Spirit: Empowerment for Ministry

Distinct experience from salvation. Promise of the Father.

**What It Represents:**
- Infilling of the Holy Spirit (Acts 1:8)
- Empowerment for ministry (Luke 24:49)
- Release of spiritual gifts (1 Corinthians 12:7-11)

## 3. Baptism by Fire: Purification and Passion

Often misunderstood, but vital. Refining work of the Holy Spirit.

**What It Represents:**
- Purification and refinement (Malachi 3:2-3)
- Burning passion for God (Luke 12:49)
- Fire of God's presence consuming everything unlike Him (Hebrews 12:29)

## How to Lead Others into These Experiences

### Water Baptism
- Teach importance, encourage obedience, provide baptism services

### Baptism in the Holy Spirit
- Teach the promise (Acts 1:8, Luke 11:13)
- Create atmosphere of expectation
- Lay hands, pray for tongues as Spirit gives utterance

### Baptism by Fire
- Teach on holiness
- Encourage surrender of every area
- Pray for fire of God to fall
