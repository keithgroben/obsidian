# Bonus Answer 3: Leverage Technology and Media for Gospel Impact

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1C_NbaHN617D-DNhXgsbTSpqBdQaNZxoBL0YtD_GZVZg*

Technology and media are powerful tools for preaching the gospel. They allow us to reach people we would never meet in person, fulfilling the Great Commission easier and on a global scale.

> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." — **Matthew 28:19 (ESV)**

The word "preach" also carries a definition of "publish." Go and preach/publish the gospel to all nations.

## Why It Matters
- Technology amplifies the reach of the gospel
- It allows us to share with people we'd never meet in person
- It creates opportunities for discipleship and follow-up

## How to Implement
1. **Use Social Media** — Share gospel messages, testimonies, and discipleship teachings on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube
2. **Livestream Services** — Broadcast your services online
3. **Create Podcasts** — Produce content that teaches and inspires
4. **Focus on Giving the Message** — The goal is to share the gospel, not just to fill seats

## Result
Revival spreads faster and farther when the gospel is shared through modern publishing tools. Technology allows us to reach more people, making the Great Commission more achievable.
