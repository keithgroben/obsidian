# Answer 8. Evangelism is Everyone's Responsibility (2 Timothy 4:5)

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ZOig2IBKGjsz15nuu7mcVknlit_35GsUIZCbCLyQbeY*

Evangelism is not just for the "professionals." Not reserved for pastors, evangelists, or missionaries. It's the responsibility of every believer.

> "As for you, always be sober-minded, endure suffering, do the work of an evangelist, fulfill your ministry." — **2 Timothy 4:5 (ESV)**

## Debunking the "Evangelist-Only" Myth

The church has operated under the misconception that evangelism is the job of a select few. We've delegated the responsibility to pastors, evangelists, and missionaries. But that's not what the Bible teaches.

- **Evangelists equip the church**: Ephesians 4:11-12 — their role is to train and empower *you* to share the gospel
- **Every believer is a witness**: Jesus said *"You will be my witnesses"* (Acts 1:8)

You don't need a title, a pulpit, or a theology degree. You just need a heart for Jesus and a willingness to obey.

## Why Evangelism is Essential

1. **Obedience to Jesus' Command** — *"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations."* (Matthew 28:19)
2. **The Power to Save** — The gospel is the power of God for salvation (Romans 1:16)
3. **The Heart of God** — God desires all people to be saved (1 Timothy 2:4)

## Practical Ways to Share the Gospel in Daily Life

1. **Start with Prayer** — Ask God to open doors, pray for boldness, wisdom, divine appointments
2. **Build Relationships** — Get to know neighbors, coworkers, friends. Show the love of Christ
3. **Share Your Testimony** — Keep it simple. Focus on what Jesus has done for you
4. **Use Everyday Opportunities** — If someone shares a struggle, offer to pray. Mention church naturally
5. **Invite People to Church or Events**
6. **Carry Gospel Tracts or Resources**
7. **Be Ready to Share the Gospel** — Memorize Roman Road (Romans 3:23, 6:23, 5:8, 10:9-10) or ABCs (Admit, Believe, Confess)
8. **Live a Life That Points to Jesus** — Integrity, joy, love. Your life is a testimony.

## Overcoming Fear and Excuses

- **It's not about you** — The power is in the gospel, not your delivery
- **God is with you** — *"I am with you always"* (Matthew 28:20)
- **Practice makes progress** — The more you share, the more confident you become

**"The Great Commission is the church's mission — and it's your mission too."**

## Action Steps
1. Pray for Opportunities
2. Start Small — Share your testimony with one person this week
3. Be Intentional — Bring Jesus into everyday conversations
4. Invite Someone to church or a community event
5. Trust God — The power is in the gospel, not in you
