# Answer 7. Salvation is More Than Forgiveness

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/19iODCw8mj7giLn2X-52BKT4l2i2wNkhptB8FnFgENkI*

Salvation is often misunderstood. Many believers think of it solely in terms of forgiveness of sins, but the Bible paints a far grander picture. Salvation is not just about being saved *from* something — it's about being saved *for* something. Complete restoration of every area: spirit, soul, and body.

> "Whoever makes a practice of sinning is of the devil, for the devil has been sinning from the beginning. The reason the Son of God appeared was to destroy the works of the devil." — **1 John 3:8 (ESV)**

Salvation is the total destruction of the devil's works in our lives. It's forgiveness, yes, but it's also healing, transformation, and abundance.

## The Goodness of God Leads to Repentance

Salvation begins with an encounter with the goodness of God. Not fear, guilt, or condemnation — His kindness.

> "Or do you presume on the riches of his kindness and forbearance and patience, not knowing that God's kindness is meant to lead you to repentance?" — **Romans 2:4**

## Salvation: A Complete Package

Do you believe that Jesus, today...
- Forgives sin completely?
- Heals bodies wholly?
- Transforms souls into His image?
- Provides abundantly so we can fulfill His mission?

If you doubt any part, in love, I tell you: You either doubt or don't fully understand the scope of Jesus' work on the cross.

## The Fourfold Work of Salvation

1. **Forgiveness of Sins** — *"He was pierced for our transgressions... by his wounds we are healed."* (Isaiah 53:5)
2. **Healing of Our Bodies** — *"I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health."* (3 John 1:2)
3. **Transformation of Our Souls** — *"Be transformed by the renewal of your mind."* (Romans 12:2)
4. **Provision for Our Lives** — *"My God will supply every need of yours according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus."* (Philippians 4:19)

## Experiencing Salvation for Yourself

> "Whatever you ask in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours." — **Mark 11:24**

Ask God to reveal His goodness to you. Ask Him to heal your body, transform your mind, and provide for your needs. Believe, and watch as He moves.

**"Salvation is more than forgiveness — it's the fullness of God's power working in you."**

## Action Steps
1. **Pray for Revelation**: Ask God to reveal the fullness of salvation to you
2. **Believe for Healing**: Pray for healing, believe by His stripes, you are healed
3. **Renew Your Mind**: Meditate on Scriptures about God's transforming power
