# Answer 12. Burn with Love for God

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1K7zXtE7dJSQmfU5ZqWWtUyEDzgP9VDe6mvVt6rpcKZo*

Revival begins in the heart. When your heart is on fire for Jesus, that fire spreads to others, igniting revival. Jesus said the greatest commandment is to love God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength (Matthew 22:37).

> "And you shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind and with all your strength." — **Mark 12:30 (ESV)**

To burn with love for God is to be baptized not only with water but with the fire of the Holy Spirit.

## Passionate Devotion as Revival Fuel

1. **It Draws You Closer to God** — *"Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you."* (James 4:8)
2. **It Ignites Your Faith** — *"Faith working through love."* (Galatians 5:6)
3. **It Inspires Others** — *"Let your light shine before others."* (Matthew 5:16)
4. **It Sustains Revival** — *"You have abandoned the love you had at first. Remember therefore from where you have fallen; repent."* (Revelation 2:4-5)

## Daily Habits to Keep Your Spiritual Fire Ablaze

1. **Spend Time in God's Presence** — Pray, worship, meditate on Scripture daily. (Matthew 6:6)
2. **Worship with Abandon** — Pour out your heart to God. (Psalm 95:6)
3. **Study the Word Daily** — *"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."* (Psalm 119:105)
4. **Fast and Pray** — (Matthew 6:17-18)
5. **Serve Others** — *"The Son of Man came not to be served but to serve."* (Mark 10:45)
6. **Guard Your Heart** — *"Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life."* (Proverbs 4:23)
7. **Stay Connected to the Body of Christ** — *"Stir up one another to love and good works... not neglecting to meet together."* (Hebrews 10:24-25)

## Action Steps
1. Commit to daily prayer, worship, and Scripture
2. Worship with abandon
3. Fast and pray regularly
4. Serve and bless those around you
5. Guard your heart from distractions
6. Surround yourself with passionate believers

**"Burn with love for God, and watch revival ignite in your life and beyond."**
