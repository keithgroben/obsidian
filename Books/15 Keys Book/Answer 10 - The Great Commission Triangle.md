# Answer 10. The Great Commission Triangle: Evangelism, Discipleship, Power

The Great Commission is not a one-dimensional command. It's a multi-faceted mission that requires the church to operate in three essential areas: **Evangelism, Discipleship, and Power**. These three elements are interconnected and interdependent. Remove one, and the mission becomes unbalanced. Exaggerate one at the expense of the others, and the mission becomes ineffective. Together, they form the Great Commission Triangle—a blueprint for the church's work on earth.

## Why All Three Are Inseparable

The Great Commission is not just about preaching the gospel or making disciples or demonstrating God's power. It's about all three working together in harmony.

1. **Evangelism**: Proclaims the gospel and brings people into the kingdom.
2. **Discipleship**: Teaches and trains believers to live like Christ and fulfill their calling.
3. **Power**: Demonstrates the reality of the gospel through signs, wonders, and the Holy Spirit's presence.

When these three elements are operating together, the church becomes a powerful force for kingdom advancement.

## The Great Commission in Scripture

### 1. Discipleship: Matthew 28:18-20
"And Jesus came and said to them, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.'"

### 2. Evangelism and Power: Mark 16:15-18
"And he said to them, 'Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to the whole creation. Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. And these signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak in new tongues; they will pick up serpents with their hands; and if they drink any deadly poison, it will not hurt them; they will lay their hands on the sick, and they will recover.'"

### 3. Evangelism and Power: Luke 24:46-49
"Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer and on the third day rise from the dead, and that repentance for the forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem. You are witnesses of these things. And behold, I am sending the promise of my Father upon you. But stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high."

### 4. Evangelism and Power: John 20:21-23
"Jesus said to them again, 'Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you.' And when he had said this, he breathed on them and said to them, 'Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven them; if you withhold forgiveness from any, it is withheld.'"

### 5. Power and Evangelism: Acts 1:8
"But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth."

### 6. Discipleship: Acts 11:25-26, Acts 18:25
"So Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul, and when he had found him, he brought him to Antioch. For a whole year they met with the church and taught a great many people. And in Antioch the disciples were first called Christians."

## The Danger of Imbalance

1. **Evangelism Without Discipleship**: Converts are made, but they lack depth and maturity.
2. **Discipleship Without Evangelism**: The church becomes inward-focused.
3. **Power Without Evangelism and Discipleship**: Miracles may happen, but without foundation they lack lasting impact.

## Action Steps

1. **Evaluate**: Do you love the people Jesus loves? Are you committed to fulfilling the Great Commission in its entirety?
2. **Commit to Change**: Repent and ask God for a heart of compassion.
3. **Understand the Great Commission**: Recognize evangelism, discipleship, and power are interconnected.
4. **Study the Scriptures**: Dive into Matthew 28, Mark 16, Luke 24, John 20, Acts 1, and Isaiah 56.
5. **Do Something Today**: Preach a gospel-only message. Record yourself preaching the gospel and share it.
