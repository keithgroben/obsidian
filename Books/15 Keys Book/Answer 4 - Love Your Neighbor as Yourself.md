# Answer 4. Love Your Neighbor as Yourself (Matthew 22:39)

*Source: Google Drive — https://docs.google.com/document/d/1DwE_u6uIOSv9cB5HeuyyRkoaAoynE_Br6ByArfyHNT0*

Revival thrives when selflessness replaces self-interest — especially for the pastor.

I had a big issue for a long time as a pastor. It was with people. I remember saying often the classic, dreaded line: *"Ministry would be great if it wasn't for the people."* Wow. I should have quit or been fired. But it was just a sign of a much deeper problem buried underneath something that was, ironically, good.

Like most ministers, I got into ministry because I loved God with all my heart, soul, mind, and strength (Mark 12:30). I answered the call, and by and large, what I did was all about that call. But here's the problem: in many ways, my ministry became all about *me*. It took on weird forms of self-centeredness. I often used Scripture to justify the ugly parts. And the ugly parts all had one thing in common: **I didn't love people as myself.**

## What Does It Mean to Love Your Neighbor as Yourself?

You must become **generous**. Flip the switch into 100% give mode. Do your ministry as if you expect nothing from anyone. Just love them.

- Don't bend over backwards
- Don't do ministry broke
- Don't give away all your time and neglect your family (**don't do that** — if you are, quit)

Many pastors run their churches to fill seats. *How can I get people in here?* That's backwards.

**Them coming is the byproduct of your export of love.** Serve others. Give them the Gospel. Be a channel to load others up with the benefits God has blessed you with.

> "Let each of you look out not only for his own interests, but also for the interests of others." — **Philippians 2:4**

## The Benefits of Loving Your Neighbor

1. **You Become a Conduit for Revival** — People see Jesus in your actions. *"By this all will know that you are My disciples, if you have love for one another."* (John 13:35)
2. **You Experience God's Favor** — *"Give, and it will be given to you."* (Luke 6:38)
3. **You Fulfill the Great Commandment** — *"You shall love your neighbor as yourself."* (Matthew 22:39)

Revival starts and stops with you. When you love your neighbor as yourself, you create a culture of selflessness that attracts God's presence, keeps His power moving, and keeps your church growing.

**Remember: Them coming is the byproduct of your export of love.**
