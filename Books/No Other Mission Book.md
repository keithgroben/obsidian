## Chapter Template

- Introduction
- MY STARTING POINT (Backstory / Old Vehicle)
- MY BIG DREAM (Desire)
- HITTING THE WALL (Conflict)
- MY TURNING POINT (Epiphany / New Vehicle)
- Scripture (up to 7)
- TAKE YOUR NEXT STEP (Action)
  1. Evaluate
  2. Commit to change
  3. Understand what the chapter lesson is
  4. Study
  5. Do

## Chapter Length Plan

Reference: Mike Murdock's *7 Laws You Must Honor To Have Uncommon Success* — 12,683 words across 7 chapters (~1,800 words each). Adjusted structure:

- I. THE SPARK: Your Wake-Up Call (150–200 words)
- II. MY STARTING POINT (250–300 words) — A. The Struggle Is Real · B. Trying Everything, Going Nowhere
- III. THE HEART'S CRY: What You Really Want (250–300) — A. You're Not the Only One · B. My Quest, Your Destination
- IV. HITTING THE WALL: When You Feel Like Giving Up (250–300) — A. The Obstacles Are Opportunities · B. The Moment Everything Changed
- V. THE TURNING POINT: Embracing a New Way (250–300) — A. Light Breaks Through the Darkness · B. Change Starts with a Choice
- VI. WHAT THE BIBLE SAYS: God's Word, Your Anchor (250–300) — A. Verses That Will Transform Your Life · B. Connecting the Dots
- VII. PUTTING IT INTO PRACTICE: Your Action Plan (250–300) — A. Real-Life Breakthroughs · B. Overcoming Every Obstacle
- VIII. YOUR NEXT STEPS: Seize Your Moment (150–200) — A. Your Key Takeaways · B. Your Challenge, Your Destiny

**Target total:** 1,800–2,200 words per chapter × 7 chapters ≈ 12,600–15,400 words.
