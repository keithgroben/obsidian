## You're Not Alone in This Journey

Friends, I've taken you through this journey, but this isn't even 25% of the story.
I hope you reach out to me and follow me on social media, subscribing to my emails because I continue to tell this story as a testimony of the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ and the revolution that I know we're in—a time where the church acts, lives, and performs as the church whom Jesus Christ is coming for.

## The Key to Prosperity: God's Voice and Word

The greatest lesson you can learn is that you must have the voice of God and the word of God in your life.
You must be centralized on the Great Commission and determine that you belong in the prosperity of God's will, His word, and His covenant with you through the Lord Jesus Christ.
You have to determine that you are an inheritor and an heir of God's great benefits for your life.

## Prophesy What God Has Said About You

If you are on a journey out of something or into something, and that journey has to do with you getting everything God wants for you, I want you to know that if there's pain, lack, or brokenness in your life, you can prophesy what God has said about you.
I'm not talking about on a stage; I'm talking about to yourself.
If you can get a full revelation and confidence in God's written word, you're going to have the dynamic duo that God talks about in Deuteronomy 28 and in the Gospels, where it says, "But when I leave, the Comforter will come to you. He will guide you into all truth, reveal to you things to come."

## God Speaks to Us Through His Word and Mouth

In both the Old and New Covenants, we have revealed to us the nature of God—that He speaks to us by His word and by His mouth.
Job 22:21 says, "Agree with God and be at peace; thereby good will come to you. Receive instructions from His mouth."
When you understand what the word "agree" means, it actually means to be responsible or to serve actively and perform work that is a benefit to another.

## God Is Unearthing the Gold in You

Inside of you, God has placed great gold, and He is doing everything He can to unearth it.
If you can identify with any bit of my past, no matter how far down or up you are, my story should give you hope that no matter where you're at, God is digging up the gold in your life, even if it's the hardest thing you ever go through.
You are worth all the effort God is giving you.

## Don't Be Cut Off from God's Covenant Provision

One thing I realized and want to speak to is the Israelites in Ezekiel 37.
Once they came to and could speak to God that they were indeed cut off and their hope was lost, God's response indicated that they were cut off from the blessing, the covenant provision that belonged to them from Abraham.
If you study the scriptures, you have that same provision.
If you're not living the fullest, best life that you know God has written about in His word, I want you to know that is the life God has for you.

## God Has Healing and Resurrecting Power for You

But if you feel cut off from that, like the Israelites did, and your hope is lost, you are suffering from a crushed spirit.
The Bible says, "A man can overcome sickness, but a crushed spirit who can bear?"
When Jesus' soul was put into anguish, crushed, pressed, and destroyed (Isaiah 53), it was so ours doesn't have to be.
In Luke 4, it says He opens the prison doors of those who are bruised—that word also means crushed.
God has healing, continual healing, and resurrecting power going for you and any of His lost children, like a lost sheep of the house of Israel.
If you have started a journey with God but feel isolated, alone, or hopeless, God is now calling you not just back to Him, but to the complete work of salvation that He desires for you through His Son, Jesus Christ.
