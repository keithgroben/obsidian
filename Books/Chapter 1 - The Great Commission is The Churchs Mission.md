# Chapter 1: The Great Commission is The Church's Mission

# There is No Other Mission

You've felt it, haven't you?
That nagging sense there must be more to ministry than this.
You pour your heart and soul into your church. You try new strategies and ideas, and growth doesn't come.

I've been there. It's painful.

Standing at the crossroads of frustration and desperation, I searched for the missing piece of the puzzle.

But what if I told you the answer is in front of you? Hidden in plain sight within the pages of Scripture.
Get ready to fire up your passion and purpose and uncover the truth.
A simple statement that can revolutionize your ministry and transform your life.

> The Great Commission is the Church's mission, there is no other mission.

# The Backstory: A Small Town and Big Dreams

## The Time I Fulfilled My Lifelong Dream of Becoming a Pastor

I Pastored Calvary Assembly of God from 2009 to 2018 in a small rural Nevada town named Ely (pronounced ee-lee).
You would love it if you visited. Beautiful mountains, pine trees, and some of the kindest people you'll meet. After I got saved at 14 not far from Ely. Shortly after I heard the call of God to pastor. That dream came true when I took on this church.

## It's Time for Overhaul… Again

In the summer of 2016 I started work on plans I made to remodel the church stage.
- It was an old church, built in the 1950s.
- It had a nostalgic 1970s feel to it.
- The church fit the town— old, run down, but standing strong.

The sanctuary wasn't made for an electric sound system or drums. The 50's were a different time. That's why I was going to remodel the stage.
The drums needed a new home and there was a giant metal baptismal in their place.

Like you, I'm always looking for ways to improve my work for the Lord.
And while most of my improvement ideas failed to make people stick with the church, I was certain this was the missing piece of the puzzle.

## Experimenting for Success

I was confident, the church needed to be "more attractive" for people. I was set out to make a place of worship that God would want to "hang out" in. And if God was there, people would join us and stay with us. That was the idea, anyway.

Moving the drums represented better worship. I thought, "If I remove this thing, we'll have room for drums, and better praise and worship will help us grow." I tried so many things before. Some worked, most of the others didn't.

This remodel was the next thing on my list to try and figure out the missing ingredient to church growth.

# The Desire: Hosting the Presence of God

## Build, Build, Build (What's the Trick Here?)

Don't you want a church full of people? I think every pastor does.
And like many pastors, I'm sure, my burning desire to succeed became a painful contrast when I'd watch successful ministries online like Bethel or Elevation Church.
They seemed to have it all together. Tons of people, funded, lots of ministries. Every pastor I know wants something like that. I wanted something like that.

So I'd do what I could. All of my projects and improvements were imitating what I saw to be success.
Over time I made changes like:
- Improving the kids' ministry
- Hosting community events
- Rebranding
- Remodeling
- Services at different times
- A discipleship school
- Ads and marketing
- So many more…

Some things became staples for me. Some were miserable failures and huge wastes of time. But nothing seemed to give me the breakthrough I wanted. Nothing really seemed to make a dent in what I needed.

## One Last Experiment

Around that time there was a movement many called "prophetic worship". Maybe you've heard of it? This was a sub culture of the larger Prophetic-Apostolic movement of the early 2000's.
Most of the ministries in it were very active online.
Live streams, videos, posts, worship— difficult to miss. Their media helped me see what "hosting the presence" was like.
That was the goal.

## The Prophetic-Apostolic Revolution

I'd be surprised if you haven't heard the terms "Prophetic" or "Apostolic" before. They're still around and make up a huge community of people.
For some reason to me, worship was the miracle button to get God to move in Ely. I think it was a common understanding for a lot of people in that movement. Maybe still is.

The thought I had was: Great praise and worship equals God saving and healing people all by Himself.

At least for me, there was an underlying understanding that God moves and revives at His own discretion. No one really knew what provoked Him to pour out His Spirit. In my circle, we just knew that He does. After all, we've seen it.

### Pour Out Your Spirit, Oh God

Some notable times are the Jesus movement of the late 1960s and early 1970s. There was no praise and worship movement preceding it that I'm aware of.

God's moved at other times both with and without an emphasis on worship.
Like the Toronto blessing in 1994, the Brownsville revival in 1995, and recently the Ausbury revival in 2023.

But what about the sustained revivals? What about Burn 24/7 or Sean Feucht? Some people had the formula down. I was determined to find it too.

# The Wall: Something Was About To Give

## Revival: Mysterious Movement or Deliberate Response?

What happened next led to me discovering Bible truth about revival. It would be the key to 'getting God to show up' at any time, anywhere, and for however long I wanted.

Before that day, revival was a mystery to me. Maybe it's a mystery to you too. To me, at the time, revival simply meant God moving and doing miracles for an extended period of time. Then, it was over. Could have been months, days, years.

I was convinced:
- Extended revivals weren't powerful
- Any ministry who claimed they had one also had bad theology
- God moves unpredictably
- You can't really bring it on, its a by product

While spontaneous revivals should always be cherished; they aren't God's primary way of moving and reviving. He will move and revive anywhere at anytime once you understand the secret.

## Don't Cut Out That Baptismal

One Saturday, with sawzall in hand, I thought out carefully how I would cut out the baptismal out and place it in the dumpster.
I set my blade on the metal, placed my finger on the trigger and was immediately interrupted by the voice of God.

As if He was standing next to me, He said:
- "Keith, what are you doing?"
- "I'm cutting out the baptismal to make room for the drums."
- He asked me, "Why aren't you using the baptismal?"
- "Because nobody needs baptized." His next reply hit me like a ton of bricks.
- "And why doesn't anyone need baptized?"
- Soberingly I said, "Because no one is getting saved."
- "And why is that?"
- "Because I'm not telling them about you."

## God's Heartbreak and the Truth About My Condition

At that moment, I felt God's sadness and heartbreak. I strayed far away from my first love. Jesus is the only reason I was saved and was pastoring that church.
My ministry had become some fringe effort to convince God to do what He would freely do at any time. After God spoke to me, I was flooded with memories of the importance of winning souls.

These thoughts were branded into me from 1997-2010 in Tommy Barnetts church Phoenix First. I had trained as a soul-winner. I even graduated from his Pastor's College.
But I had lost my way, forgetting to love my neighbor as my self.
Forgetting to love who God loves.

I had been 'trained' some time after Bible school that Evangelism is not a pastors job. That it wasn't my calling. I learned that Evangelism is part of the church, not it's mission.

# The Truth: Repentance and the Return to My First Love

## I Changed in .003 Seconds

- The great commission is the church's mission, there is no other mission.
- I repented immediately. Changed from the inside out.
- Over the next few days my fire for Jesus grew.
- I immediately changed my personal priorities.
- I immediately changed my churches priorities.

The baptismal was saved. And as a result, people would be too. I painted the baptismal and got it ready for all the souls that were going to be saved as I preached the Gospel.
Before this encounter, I justified working only part of the great commission. I would disciple, yes, but truly believed evangelism was somebody else's job. The result of that skewed understanding was a major obstacle to my personal growth and my church's growth.

## Repentance Is A Gift

I planned outreaches, improved our operations and changed our priorities as a church. I created membership classes and other discipleship courses to help the newly saved people.
It was incredible to see the growth and transformation happening in my church. The growth seemed effortless, but I worked harder than I did before.
When I focused on the mission of Jesus, He funded my church, healed and delivered people every week, and brought major growth. We were in continual, sustained, and growing revival.

No worship needed. But that got better too.

# The Big Lesson: What is The Great Commission?

The Great Commission is the church's work of Evangelism, Discipleship, and the demonstration of God's power. The church is responsible for executing all three equally, not just parts of it.

Take a look at how the Bible spells this out for you.

## 1. Discipleship: as seen in Matthew 28:18-20

> "And Jesus came and said to them, 'All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age.'" (Matthew 28:18-20, ESV)

## 2. Evangelism, Power: as seen in Mark 16:15-18

> And he said to them, "Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to the whole creation. Whoever believes and is baptized will be saved, but whoever does not believe will be condemned. And these signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak in new tongues; they will pick up serpents with their hands; and if they drink any deadly poison, it will not hurt them; they will lay their hands on the sick, and they will recover." (Mark 16:15-18, ESV)

## 3. Evangelism, Power: as seen in Luke 24:46-49

> and said to them, "Thus it is written, that the Christ should suffer and on the third day rise from the dead, and that repentance for the forgiveness of sins should be proclaimed in his name to all nations, beginning from Jerusalem. You are witnesses of these things. And behold, I am sending the promise of my Father upon you. But stay in the city until you are clothed with power from on high." (Luke 24:46-49, ESV)

## 4. Evangelism, Power: as seen in John 20:21-23

> Jesus said to them again, "Peace be with you. As the Father has sent me, even so I am sending you." And when he had said this, he breathed on them and said to them, "Receive the Holy Spirit. If you forgive the sins of any, they are forgiven them; if you withhold forgiveness from any, it is withheld." (John 20:21-23, ESV)

## 5. Power, Evangelism: as seen in Acts 1:8

> But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you, and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the end of the earth." (Acts 1:8, ESV)

## 6. Discipleship: as seen in Acts 11:25-26, Acts 18:25

> So Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul, and when he had found him, he brought him to Antioch. For a whole year they met with the church and taught a great many people. And in Antioch the disciples were first called Christians. (Acts 11:25-26, ESV)

> He had been instructed in the way of the Lord. And being fervent in spirit, he spoke and taught accurately the things concerning Jesus, though he knew only the baptism of John. (Acts 18:25, ESV)

# Take Action: Simple Steps For Maximum Impact

1. **Evaluate.** Do you love the people who Jesus loves?
2. **Commit to change.** If you need to, repent and ask for the heart of compassion Jesus has.
3. **Understand** what the Great Commission is.
   - Continuation of Jesus' ministry on earth
   - Evangelism, discipleship, and power are interconnected and interdependent
   - Effectiveness depends on a strong church community
   - Isolating any one part (evangelism, discipleship, power, or community) weakens the overall mission
   - Many people and denominations tend to focus on individual aspects rather than all four
4. **Study** the scriptures.
   - Study the scriptures in this chapter.
   - Then study the work of salvation in Isaiah the 56th chapter.
5. **Do** something today.
   - Preach a Gospel only message to your church.
   - If you don't have a church, record yourself preaching the gospel from your studies. Send it to someone.
