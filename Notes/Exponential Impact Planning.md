## Master exponential planning

Skills to learn in order of importance to master exponential planning in process improvement and operations management:

1. **Strategic thinking** — identify trends, opportunities, and threats; develop plans to capitalize on them.
2. **Data analysis** — collect, analyze, and interpret data; statistics, data mining, predictive analytics.
3. **Systems thinking** — see the big picture; understand how system parts interact; identify bottlenecks and redundancies.
4. **Process improvement methodologies** — Lean Six Sigma, Six Sigma DMAIC, Kaizen.
5. **Project management** — careful planning and execution of complex projects.
6. **Change management** — minimize disruption and resistance when implementing plans.
7. **Communication** — clearly articulate vision; explain rationale; answer questions.
8. **Leadership** — build consensus, delegate, manage conflict.
9. **Experimentation** — test new ideas; learn from failure; take risks.
10. **Agility** — adapt plans quickly to changing circumstances.

## Key Methodologies

### Lean Six Sigma
Systematic approach combining Lean (eliminate waste, improve efficiency) and Six Sigma (reduce defects, improve quality). Uses **DMAIC**: Define → Measure → Analyze → Improve → Control.

### Six Sigma DMAIC
Data-driven improvement methodology for existing processes. Identifies and eliminates root causes of defects and variation.

### Kaizen
Japanese "continuous improvement." Everyone empowered to make small changes; focus on customer; teamwork; daily improvement.

### Lean
Eliminate waste (time, materials, effort); create continuous flow of value; pull production based on demand; build quality in; respect for people.

## Strategic Thinking Frameworks

- **SWOT Analysis** — strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.
- **Porter's Five Forces** — threat of new entrants, supplier power, buyer power, substitutes, rivalry.
- **Competitive Advantage Analysis** — cost leadership or differentiation.
- **Value Chain Analysis** — primary activities (logistics, operations, sales, service) and support activities (procurement, tech, HR, infrastructure).
- **Scenario Planning** — identify key drivers of change; develop plausible futures; plan responses.
- **Balanced Scorecard** — four perspectives: financial, customer, internal processes, learning and growth.

## Process Mapping Standards

- **BPMN** (Business Process Model and Notation) — graphical standard for describing processes using activities, data objects, flows, gateways, events.
- **SIPOC** — Supplier, Input, Process, Output, Customer.
- **DFD** (Data Flow Diagram) — processes, data stores, external entities, flows.

### BPMN Key Rules
1. Every process has clearly defined start and end events (circles).
2. Activities = rounded rectangles.
3. Gateways = diamonds for decisions (XOR, OR, AND).
4. Sequence flows connect activities in order; no crossing.
5. Message flows = dashed lines between participants.
6. Pools = participants; lanes = subdivisions.
7. Artifacts add context (data objects, groups, annotations).
