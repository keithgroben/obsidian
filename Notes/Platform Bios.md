## The Wayfinder About Page

**From the Desk of Keith**

I've never fit neatly into one box. To the marketers, I'm the "technical guy." To the developers, I'm the "creative guy." To the creatives, I'm the "systems guy."

For a long time, I thought this was a bug. I tried to niche down. I tried to be just one thing. Then I realized that every domain I've worked in — marketing, design, copywriting, software, content creation, SEO, business process — they all share the same pattern underneath. Every one of them has bottlenecks, and most of those bottlenecks can be solved with self-driven, intelligent systems.

So that's what I build.

I'm an Operational Architect. I help owner-operators stop being trapped in their own businesses by turning the knowledge stuck in their heads into systems that run without them. I've been that person — pastoring, running projects, carrying everything in my head with no infrastructure to hold it. I build what I wish I'd had.

My work is powered by AI agents and automation, but the conviction behind it is simpler than the technology: I believe creating real value for people is the point. Not the business model. The actual point. That belief comes from my faith, and it shapes everything I build.

**The Wayfinder** is where I write about what I'm building, what I'm learning, and what I believe. Systems, marketing, faith, and the patterns that connect them. Always short. Never fluff.
