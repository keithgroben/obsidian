# Who I Am

*Source: Notion — https://www.notion.so/3106abe00ba481f4b308f8227d5e8de6*

## The Core Identity

Systems thinker who builds from lived experience, not theory. Cross-domain pattern recognition is the superpower. You don't learn a domain and move on. You learn it, internalize the patterns, and look for the connective tissue between that domain and everything else you know.

## The Tension

The architect vs the explorer. The architect wants elegant, complete systems. The explorer wants to chase the next insight, the next connection, the next domain. Neither impulse is wrong, but they compete for the same hours. Your best work happens when the architect builds a structure that channels the explorer's energy instead of trying to contain it.

## The Philosophy

You create under compression. No infinite budgets, no large teams. You build clever systems instead. You'd rather build a machine that multiplies your effort than hire ten people. That's the Operational Architect identity showing up in your own life before you ever sold it to a client.

## The Conviction

The spiritual thread isn't decoration. Creating real value for people is the point. Not the business model. The actual point. That's why you gravitate toward owner-operators and bivocational pastors and people who are stuck. You see yourself in them. You've been the person trapped in the bottleneck of your own knowledge, and you're building your way out in public.

## The Identity Statement

A builder-philosopher who happens to monetize through consulting. The consulting is the vehicle, but the drive is to understand how things work and then make them work better for people who don't have the bandwidth to figure it out themselves.

## Personality

ENTP energy (tested INTJ during pastoral season, likely situational). The "spin up many things" tendency is pattern recognition meeting explorer energy. The Initiative Tracker is the pruning discipline that turns this from a liability into a portfolio strategy.

## Historical Parallels

- **Walt Disney:** Cross-domain systems thinker who built miniatures to recruit, not to ship. The prototype as a recruiting tool, not the end product.
- **Benjamin Franklin:** Printer, scientist, diplomat, inventor, publisher. Different expressions of the same curiosity about how systems work and how to make them serve people.
- **Craig Groeschel:** Pastor turned organizational leader who systematized church operations and taught those systems to thousands. Made the leap from doing the ministry to building the machine that scales the ministry.

## The Wall

Isolation. The tendency to build in the basement and hope content finds the tribe. The solution isn't networking. It's targeted demos shown to specific people. Content is the door, not the destination. The miniature on the table is the recruiting tool.

## Domains of Expertise

Marketing, design, interactive design, presentation, copywriting, small ad campaigns, business software, business processes and change management, reputation management, local search and trust marketing, website development and SEO, content creation (written, blogs, long and short video, emails, graphic posts), and product development.
